A Confirmatory, Dual Center Nonhuman Primate Study on the Efficacy of Novel Oropharyngeal Spray Immunization With an Adenoviral Vector Vaccine Against RSV-Important Lessons Learned.

Authors: Matthias Tenbusch, Gerrit Koopman, Petra Mooij, Berit Roshani, Pascal Irrgang, Dennis Lapuente, Ivanela Kondova, Willy M. Bogers, Edmond J. Remarque, Ramona Vestweber
Original abstract (English)
ABSTRACT Background In a randomized, blinded, dual center study across two European primate research facilities, we evaluated the immunogenicity and protective efficacy of a DNA prime—oropharyngeal adenoviral boost vaccination strategy against respiratory syncytial virus (RSV). Methods Rhesus macaques received an RSV fusion protein (F) DNA vaccine via electroporation, followed by two mucosal administrations of a recombinant adenovirus vector encoding the same antigen. Mock‐vaccinated animals and a comparator group mimicking natural immunity induced by primary RSV infection were included as controls. Systemic and mucosal immune responses were monitored longitudinally. Results We detected robust cellular and humoral immune responses, but with some variability between the two centers. Nevertheless, RSV‐vaccinated animals demonstrated markedly reduced viral replication in the upper and lower respiratory tract after RSV challenge infection. Unexpected RSV‐specific immunity in the control group at one single study site prevented confirmation of the predefined primary endpoint. Conclusion Our results support the potential of mucosal adenoviral boosting following DNA priming to induce protective immunity against RSV, while highlighting challenges associated with multi‐center preclinical vaccine studies.

In Vitro Evaluation of the Virucidal Activity of Ethanol Against Human Adenoviruses
作者： Yuriko Matsumura, Hong Ying Wu, Yoshihiko Fujioka, Youichi Suzuki, Atsuo Iwasawa, Takashi Nakano
摘要中文翻译乙醇对无包膜病毒的有效性各不相同;然而,一些报告表明它具有针对 人腺病毒 (HAdV) 的杀病毒活性。45 w/w% 和 95 w/w% 之间的乙醇浓度在 30 秒内显示出足够的抗 5 型 HAdV 活性,而浓度在 72.5 w/w% 和 77 w/w% 之间则在 60 秒内有效对抗 7 型 HAdV。然而,2 型和 8 型不太容易受到影响。在本研究中,我们研究了不同浓度的乙醇灭活和改变 6 个亚群 19 种 HAdV 类型结构的能力,并使用病毒感染滴度进行评估。乙醇传播 1分钟后,用传播电子显微镜观察HAdV 5型和8型。消毒乙醇对 A、B1(21 型除外)和 C 亚组的 HAdV 的杀病毒活性高于 B2、D、E 和 F 亚组。我们观察到“U 形”波动模式,其中减少因子在浓度 70-80% 时下降,然后在 90% 时再次上升。治疗用40%乙醇处理后,TEM图像显示8型HAdV没有重大变化,但在5型HAdV中在病毒颗粒周围观察到有机物;治疗与66%乙醇混合后,两种类型的病毒颗粒均发生聚集。这表明轴区域重复次数较少的 HAdV 表现出较高的乙醇抗性。

本研究旨在评估乙醇对 人腺病毒 (HAdV) 的杀病毒活性,因为乙醇对无包膜病毒具有不同的功效,并且先前的报告表明它可能对 HAdV 有效。

研究结果提供了关于乙醇作为消毒剂对抗特定 HAdV 类型的有效性的重要见解,这可以为医疗机构中制定更有针对性的消毒方案提供信息。

该研究仅限于体外条件,可能无法完全反映现实世界的情况,并且证据基于针对一组特定 HAdV 类型的单一研究,因此可信度中等。

In Vitro Evaluation of the Virucidal Activity of Ethanol Against Human Adenoviruses

Authors: Yuriko Matsumura, Hong Ying Wu, Yoshihiko Fujioka, Youichi Suzuki, Atsuo Iwasawa, Takashi Nakano
Original abstract (English)
Ethanol has variable effectiveness against non-enveloped viruses; however, several reports suggest that it possesses virucidal activity against human adenoviruses (HAdVs). Ethanol concentrations between 45 w/w% and 95 w/w% showed sufficient activity against HAdV type 5 over a 30 s period, while concentration between 72.5 w/w% and 77 w/w% was effective against type 7 over 60 s period. Type 2 and 8 were less susceptible, however. In this study, we investigated the ability of different concentrations of ethanol to inactivate and change the structure of 19 HAdV types across six subgroups, using the virus infectivity titer for evaluation. After 1 min of ethanol treatment, HAdV types 5 and 8 were observed with transmission electron microscopy. The virucidal activity of disinfectant ethanol against HAdVs in subgroups A, B1 (except for type 21), and C was higher than that in subgroups B2, D, E, and F. We observed a ‘U-shaped’ fluctuation pattern where the reduction factor dropped at concentrations of 70–80% and then rose again at 90%. After treatment with 40% ethanol, TEM images showed no major changes in type 8 HAdVs, but organic matter was observed around the virus particles in type 5 HAdVs; after treatment with 66% ethanol, virus particles aggregated in both types. This suggests that HAdVs with a lower number of repeats in the shaft region exhibited higher ethanol resistance.

Limitations and evidence strength
The study was limited to in vitro conditions, which may not fully reflect real-world scenarios, and the evidence is based on a single study with a specific set of HAdV types, resulting in moderate confidence.

Adenovirus as a Causative Agent of an Acute Gastroenteritis Outbreak in Salvador, Bahia, Brazil.

Authors: Beatriz Peres Vidal, Silvana Oliveira Neves, Ively Paixão Santos, Silvia Ines Sardi, Gubio Soares Campos, Santos, I. P., Sardi, S. I., Campos, G. S.
Original abstract (English)
Abstract Introduction Acute gastroenteritis (AGE) is a cause of childhood morbidity and may be associated with human adenovirus (HAdV) infection. This study aimed to investigate the occurrence of HAdV in fecal samples from children with AGE in Salvador, Bahia, Brazil, as well as to molecularly characterize the identified strains and evaluate their cytopathic effects in cell culture. Methods A total of 131 samples were analyzed using the nested polymerase chain reaction (Nested-PCR) technique. Molecular characterization was performed through Sanger sequencing and phylogenetic analysis, while Vero cell cultures were used to investigate the occurrence of cytopathic effects. Question and background
This study aimed to investigate the presence and characteristics of HAdV-related signals in bronchoalveolar lavage fluid (BALF) from pediatric and adult patients, addressing the limited sequence-level evidence for HAdV in lower respiratory tract samples.
Design and population
An observational virome analysis was conducted using 189 residual BALF samples collected from pediatric and adult clinical cohorts between July 2025 and January 2026. The pediatric cohort included 30 samples from the Affiliated Children’s Hospital of Jiangnan University, while the adult cohort comprised 159 samples, with 149 from Yixing People’s Hospital and 10 from the Affiliated Hospital of Jiangsu University (Jiangbin Hospital).
Core methods
BALF samples were collected in sterile tubes and transported on dry ice to a biosafety level 2 laboratory for virome analysis. Environmental controls were included as background references, and MEGAN read classification was used to assess Adenoviridae-related signals. The comparison between pediatric and adult BALF groups was performed using the Wilcoxon rank-sum test.
Main results
Adenoviridae-classified abundance was more prominent in several pediatric BALF libraries, whereas signals in most adult BALF libraries and laboratory environmental controls were low or close to the background range. Several pediatric samples showed broad coverage of a BALF-derived HAdV-B-related representative contig, and capsid protein phylogenies supported HAdV-B-related lineage placement.
Interpretation and novelty
The study identified stronger Adenoviridae read-classification signals in pediatric BALF libraries, suggesting potential differences in viral load or presence between pediatric and adult cohorts. These findings provide genomic information from clinically selected lower respiratory tract samples.
Scientific and public-health significance
The results contribute to understanding the genomic presence of HAdV in lower respiratory tract infections, particularly in pediatric populations, which may inform improved diagnostic approaches and surveillance strategies for adenoviral pneumonia.
Limitations and evidence strength
The study is observational and does not establish an independent age effect, population prevalence, or type-specific disease severity. The evidence is moderate due to the reliance on residual samples and the absence of direct clinical diagnosis for HAdV infection.
