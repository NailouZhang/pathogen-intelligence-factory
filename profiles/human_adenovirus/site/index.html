Human CD46 humanized mice reveal interferon associated pulmonary immune remodeling during human adenovirus type 7 lung infection
作者： Zhong-Ying Yang, Zi-Jun Meng, Ming-Ling Wan, Ying Lu, Li-Fen Rao, Hui Qu, Jian-Hua Wei, Jing-Qian Liao, En-Mei Liu, Na Zang
摘要中文翻译背景 人腺病毒 7 型(HAdV-7)是严重小儿肺炎的主要原因,但病毒感染与肺部免疫重塑和肺部病理学之间的联系机制仍不完全清楚。由于缺乏概括人类易感性的生理相关动物模型,进展受到限制。方法 我们生成人 CD46 (hCD46) 人源化小鼠,并评估 HAdV-7 感染后的肺部病毒负荷、可恢复的感染性病毒和肺部病理学。在对照和感染条件下对野生型和 hCD46 人源化小鼠的肺组织进行单细胞 RNA 测序,以表征细胞组成、转录状态和细胞间通讯。进一步分析了儿科 HAdV 感染的人 PBMC 大量转录组,以评估小鼠肺源性免疫程序的系统相关性。结果 hCD46 人源化小鼠在 HAdV-7 感染后表现出肺部病毒负荷增加、感染性病毒粒子可恢复以及肺部病理更加严重。单细胞分析揭示了受感染 hCD46 肺中常驻肺泡巨噬细胞的消耗、炎症巨噬细胞亚群的显着扩张以及淋巴转录状态的重塑。这些变化伴随着显着的 I/II 型干扰素和干扰素相关趋化因子程序。B 细胞以及 CD4 + 和 CD8 + T 细胞显示出与干扰素刺激基因 (ISG) 相关的显着转录状态,以及选定的效应器和记忆相关程序以及计算推断轨迹的变化。将小鼠肺源性特征投射到人外周血单核细胞的转录组上表明,在儿科 HAdV 感染中也可以系统地检测到选定的干扰素相关的骨髓和淋巴程序。结论 hCD46 人源化小鼠中的 HAdV-7 感染与富含干扰素的肺部炎症环境、显着的炎症骨髓重塑和淋巴转录状态改变有关。这种易感小鼠模型为研究 HAdV-7 相关肺部疾病以及 抗病毒药物、免疫调节和 疫苗 策略的临床前评估提供了有用的实验工具。

Human CD46 humanized mice reveal interferon associated pulmonary immune remodeling during human adenovirus type 7 lung infection

Authors: Zhong-Ying Yang, Zi-Jun Meng, Ming-Ling Wan, Ying Lu, Li-Fen Rao, Hui Qu, Jian-Hua Wei, Jing-Qian Liao, En-Mei Liu, Na Zang
Original abstract (English)
Background Human adenovirus type 7(HAdV-7) is a major cause of severe pediatric pneumonia, but the mechanisms linking viral infection to pulmonary immune remodeling and lung pathology remain incompletely understood. Progress has been limited by the lack of physiologically relevant animal models that recapitulate human susceptibility. Methods We generated human CD46 (hCD46) humanized mice and evaluated pulmonary viral burden, recoverable infectious virus, and lung pathology after HAdV-7 infection. Single-cell RNA sequencing was performed on lung tissues from wild type and hCD46 humanized mice under control and infected conditions to characterize cellular composition, transcriptional states, and intercellular communication. Human PBMC bulk transcriptomes from pediatric HAdV infection were further analyzed to assess the systemic relevance of mouse lung derived immune programs. Results hCD46 humanized mice showed increased pulmonary viral burden, recoverable infectious virions, and more severe lung pathology after HAdV-7 infection. Single-cell profiling revealed depletion of resident alveolar macrophages, pronounced expansion of inflammatory macrophage subsets, and remodeling of lymphoid transcriptional states in infected hCD46 lungs. These changes were accompanied by prominent type I/II interferon and interferon associated chemokine programs. B cells and CD4 + and CD8 + T cells showed prominent interferon stimulated genes (ISGs) associated transcriptional states, together with alterations in selected effector- and memory-associated programs and computationally inferred trajectories. Projection of mouse lung derived signatures onto transcriptomes of human peripheral blood mononuclear cell showed that selected interferon associated myeloid and lymphoid programs were also detectable systemically in pediatric HAdV infection. Conclusions HAdV-7 infection in hCD46 humanized mice was associated with an interferon enriched pulmonary inflammatory environment, prominent inflammatory myeloid remodeling, and altered lymphoid transcriptional states. This susceptible mouse model provides a useful experimental tool for studying HAdV-7 associated lung disease and for preclinical evaluation of antiviral, immunomodulatory, and vaccine strategies.

Spatiotemporal Analysis of Intranuclear Inclusion Body and Virus Formation in Human Adenovirus Type 5-Infected Cells

Authors: Hong Ying Wu, Yoshihiko Fujioka, Shoichi Sakaguchi, Chinami Odake, Youichi Suzuki, Takashi Nakano
Original abstract (English)
Human adenovirus type 5 (HAdV-5) forms a characteristic intranuclear inclusion body in infected cells. However, the relationship between the intranuclear inclusion body and virus formation in HAdV-5-infected cells remains unclear. This study conducted a detailed investigation of the relationship between the structure of the inclusion body and the localization of the virus in HAdV-5-infected cells using electron microscopy and three-dimensional (3D) tomography analytical techniques. Immunostaining for viral proteins was also performed to determine their colocalization with the intranuclear inclusion body. 3D analysis revealed the intranuclear inclusion body as the major site of viral particle accumulation in the cells. Viral particles were identified within the intranuclear inclusion body and arranged in a crystalline structure parallel to the fibrous structures of this body. Moreover, mature viral particles were predominantly distributed around the intranuclear inclusion body. Certain viral proteins were also detected in the intranuclear inclusion body by immunoelectron microscopy. We propose that intranuclear inclusion bodies serve as sites of viral replication in HAdV-5-infected cells. Through the 3D analysis, we clarified the relationship between the distribution and localization of intranuclear inclusion bodies and viral particles in HAdV-5-infected cells. These findings can improve our understanding of adenoviral infection mechanisms and may contribute toward developing novel antiviral strategies.

作者： Tyler Barr, Elif Aktar, Sian Drake, Magdalena Karwatka, Erica Wilson, Ruth Hughes, George Eric Blair, Graham P. Cook
摘要中文翻译大多数腺病毒 (Ad) 媒介 基于人类 Ad 5 型 (Ad5) 的 基因组,其目标是进入表达柯萨奇和腺病毒受体(CAR 或 CXADR)的细胞。然而,某些人类广告不使用 CAR;例如,Ad35 与细胞表面 CD46 相互作用,而 Ad3 使用桥粒芯糖蛋白 2 (DSG2) 进入细胞。在这项研究中,使用转录组和蛋白质组数据库对不同的 Ad 受体进行比较表明,CD46 在人类细胞和组织中广泛表达,而 CAR 和 DSG2 更限于上皮细胞。我们使用了一种混合病毒 Ad5F35,它包含 Ad5 基因组,其中 Ad5 纤维被 Ad35 纤维取代,从而将病毒从 CAR 重定位到表达 CD46 的细胞,并能够转导原代人 NK 和 T 细胞。然而,与 A549 上皮细胞相比,淋巴细胞每个细胞 (ppc) 需要大约 10 至 20 倍的 Ad5F35 颗粒才能达到相似的转导水平。与此一致的是,对细胞表面 CD46 分子密度的定量显示,原代 NK 细胞中每 μm2 大约有 100 个 CD46 分子,而 HeLa 细胞中每 μm2 大约有 2000 个 CD46 分子,相差 20 倍。HeLa 细胞中的 RNA 干扰使细胞表面 CD46 密度降低约 95%,达到接近 NK 细胞的水平。较低的 CD46 密度减少了 Ad5F35 的转导,但这可以通过增加 MOI 来补偿。我们的结果确定细胞表面 CD46 的密度是 Ad5F35 转导的关键决定因素,并证明 Ad5F35 是原代人 NK 细胞中基因传递的有效 媒介。

Original abstract (English)
Porcine adenoviruses (PAdVs) are promising but still underdeveloped viral-vector platforms for swine vaccine development. Compared with human adenovirus-based vectors, PAdV vectors may offer potential advantages in host association, veterinary relevance, and mucosal delivery while avoiding direct reliance on common human adenovirus backbones. This review summarizes current knowledge on PAdV biology, vector engineering, vaccine applications, immune responses, translational challenges, and future priorities. Early studies showed that recombinant PAdV vectors can express heterologous antigens from important swine pathogens, including classical swine fever virus, pseudorabies virus, and transmissible gastroenteritis virus. The strongest direct proof-of-concept evidence comes from recombinant PAdV expressing classical swine fever virus E2/gp55, which induced neutralizing antibodies and protection in pigs. Comparative adenovirus-vectored swine vaccine studies support the broader feasibility of adenoviral antigen delivery for porcine reproductive and respiratory syndrome, porcine epidemic diarrhea, swine influenza, and African swine fever, but these data should not be interpreted as direct validation of PAdV for these diseases. Major gaps remain in anti-PAdV immunity, genetic stability, biosafety, shedding, target-animal safety, DIVA compatibility, manufacturing, and regulatory readiness. Recent advances in PAdV-5 reverse genetics provide new opportunities for modern vector design.
