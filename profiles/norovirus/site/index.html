A new wave of resurge for GII.17[P17] norovirus in multiple provinces of China in 2024

Authors: Shuting Yang, Jian Gao, Lijiao Cao, Hailong Zhang, Lina Ren, Ziping Miao, Qing Zhang, Xiangyu Kong, Guoyong Mei, Lingli Sun
Original abstract (English)
INTRODUCTION: Noroviruses is a major cause of outbreaks and sporadic acute gastroenteritis (AGE) across all age groups. Although the GII.4 norovirus has been the primary cause of most AGE outbreaks, Non-GII.4 norovirus has been reported globally. AIM: We aimed to report an increase in GII.17 norovirus outbreaks of acute gastroenteritis in multiple provinces of China during the second half of 2024. METHODS: Data and specimens from AGE outbreaks (January 2022-December 2024) were collected. Samples were tested via real-time RT-PCR; positive GII.17[P17] samples underwent sequencing and phylogenetic analysis. RESULTS: Among 1,579 norovirus outbreaks, 1,225 were genotyped. The first halves of 2022-2024 saw co-circulation of genotypes GII.3[P12], GII.4 Sydney, GII.17[P17], and GII.7[P7]. GII.4 Sydney dominated the second halves of 2022 (46.15%) and 2023 (51.63%). Strikingly, in the second half of 2024, GII.17[P17] became predominant, causing 75.28% of outbreaks. This shift was driven by a novel GII.17 variant (Romania-2021 like, subcluster C2), first detected in February 2024, which replaced the previous Kawasaki 308-like variant. The novel variant predominantly affected the 6-18 years age group (54.97%) and elementary schools (34.33%). Relative to Cluster D, this novel variant harbors five amino acid mutations in antigenic epitopes, one mutation in the histo-blood group antigen (HBGA) binding site, and two deletions. CONCLUSIONS: A novel GII.17 variant has overtaken GII.4 as the predominant norovirus strain in China since late 2024, underscoring the need for continued molecular surveillance.

Question and background
Norovirus causes widespread outbreaks of acute gastroenteritis (AGE) across all age groups, yet the role and dynamics of non-GII.4 genotypes remain insufficiently characterized in China. The study was needed to document a reported resurgence of GII.17 norovirus and understand its public health implications.
Design and population
This was a national observational study of norovirus outbreaks in China from January 2022 to December 2024, collecting outbreak data and specimens for genotyping and sequencing. The study covered multiple provinces and included 1,579 norovirus outbreaks, of which 1,225 were genotyped.
Core methods
Outbreak samples were tested using real-time RT-PCR, and GII.17[P17]-positive samples underwent sequencing and phylogenetic analysis to identify variants and mutations. Data were collected systematically across the study period.
Main results
Among 1,579 norovirus outbreaks, 1,225 were genotyped. GII.4 Sydney dominated in the second halves of 2022 (46.15%) and 2023 (51.63%), while GII.17[P17] became predominant in the second half of 2024, causing 75.28% of outbreaks.
Interpretation and novelty
The shift to GII.17[P17] was driven by a novel variant (Romania-2021 like, subcluster C2) first detected in February 2024, which replaced the previous Kawasaki 308-like variant. This variant harbored five amino acid mutations in antigenic epitopes, one mutation in the HBGA binding site, and two deletions.
Scientific and public-health significance
The emergence of this novel GII.17 variant as the predominant strain in China since late 2024 highlights the need for continued molecular surveillance to inform outbreak response and vaccine development strategies.
Limitations and evidence strength
The study relies on outbreak-based surveillance data, which may underrepresent sporadic cases and limit generalizability to non-outbreak settings. The evidence is moderate due to the observational design and potential sampling bias.

摘要中文翻译肠道病毒持续以高浓度从人类粪便中排出,并通过经过处理和未经处理的废水传播到水生环境中,其中大部分最终排入沿海海水。因此,这些病原体的环境 监测 可能反映了它们在人群中的传播,并可作为新病毒或变种出现以及现有病毒或变种 传播 增加的预警工具。在这项研究中,我们调查了坎帕尼亚地区(意大利南部)沿海水域中主要胃肠病毒(腺病毒、诺如病毒、轮状病毒)以及具有不同临床相关性的其他肠道病毒的发生和分子多样性。根据季节、采样年份和废水排放类型对病毒患病率进行评估。总共收集了 500 个海水样本,其中近一半 (44.0%) 的至少一种病毒目标检测呈阳性。病毒检出率多年来有所不同,可能是受到与 COVID-19 大流行相关的流行病学动态的影响。值得注意的是,系统发育分析表明,大多数环境病毒序列与先前在同一地理区域的人类临床病例中鉴定的毒株相同或密切相关,这支持了人类感染与环境污染之间的强大流行病学联系。总体而言,这些发现强调了综合环境 监测 在“同一个健康”框架内进行公共卫生风险评估的价值。

问题与背景
肠道病毒通过废水广泛传播到水生环境中,但在 COVID-19 大流行期间和之后,其环境 患病率 和分子多样性在沿海海水中的特征仍然不充分。该研究旨在评估坎帕尼亚地区(意大利南部)沿海水域主要胃肠病毒的发生和遗传多样性,为公共卫生提供信息 监测。
设计与对象
这是一项在意大利南部坎帕尼亚地区沿海水域进行的为期三年的环境监测研究。研究期间共采集了 500 个海水样本,根据季节、采样年份和废水排放类型对病毒 患病率 进行评估。
