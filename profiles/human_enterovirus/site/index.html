G20 国家儿童非脊髓灰质炎肠道病毒相关脑膜炎的疾病负担:基于 GBD 数据和 2050 年预测的分析

Disease burden of non-polio enterovirus-associated meningitis in children in G20 countries: an analysis based on GBD data and projections to 2050
作者： Yan Guo, Jinfu Wei, Yonghan Luo, Jie Zhang
摘要中文翻译
查看研究七要素
问题与背景
非脊髓灰质炎肠道病毒 (NPEV) 是全球儿童脑膜炎的主要原因,但 G20 国家内其长期疾病负担和趋势仍不充分。这项研究需要系统评估 G20 国家儿童 NPEV 相关脑膜炎的负担,并预测到 2050 年的未来趋势。
设计与对象
该研究分析了2023年全球疾病负担研究(GBD 2023)数据库的数据,以评估1990年至2023年G20国家0-14岁儿童中NPEV相关脑膜炎的负担。
核心方法
主要结局指标包括 死亡率 和 NPEV 相关脑膜炎导致的伤残调整生命年 (DALY)。计算估计年度百分比变化 (EAPC) 以量化时间趋势,斯皮尔曼等级相关性评估疾病负担与社会人口指数 (SDI) 之间的关系。使用贝叶斯年龄周期 队列研究 (BAPC) 模型预测 2050 年的疾病负担。
主要结果
From 1990 to 2023, the mortality rate for pediatric NPEV-associated meningitis in G20 countries declined from 1.153 per 100,000 (95% UI: 0.902-1.453) to 0.088 per 100,000 (95% UI: 0.070-0.113) (EAPC: -7.72%, 95% CI: -8.19 to -7.24). The DALY rate decreased from 107.732 to 9.487 per 100,000 (EAPC: -7.35%, 95% CI: -7.80 to -6.91). Infants younger than 1 year had the highest burden (mortality: 0.890 per 100,000; DALY: 80.649 per 100,000).
解释与创新
作者将研究结果解释为G20国家过去30年NPEV相关脑膜炎负担显著下降,但各国和年龄组间差异持续存在。研究的创新之处在于采用先进建模技术对趋势进行全面量化并预测至2050年。
科研与公卫意义
研究结果为印度尼西亚、南非和印度等高负担 G20 国家的针对性 监测 和干预策略提供了信息,并强调需要加强资源较低成员国的诊断能力,以减少 G20 内部的差距。
局限与证据强度
该研究依赖于GBD 2023数据,该数据在G20国家的案例确定和代表性方面可能存在固有的局限性。由于生态研究设计和潜在的混杂因素,时间趋势和预测的证据强度较高,但因果推断的证据强度适中。

Disease burden of non-polio enterovirus-associated meningitis in children in G20 countries: an analysis based on GBD data and projections to 2050

Authors: Yan Guo, Jinfu Wei, Yonghan Luo, Jie Zhang
Original abstract (English)
BACKGROUND: Non-polio enteroviruses (NPEVs) have emerged as the leading etiological cause of childhood meningitis globally, yet the long-term disease burden and its epidemiological trends within G20 countries remain insufficiently characterized. This study aims to systematically evaluate the burden of pediatric NPEV-associated meningitis in G20 countries from 1990 to 2023 and to project future trends to 2050. METHODS: Data were extracted from the Global Burden of Disease Study 2023 (GBD 2023) database. The primary outcome measures were mortality and disability-adjusted life years (DALYs) due to NPEV-associated meningitis in children aged 0-14 years. Estimated annual percentage changes (EAPCs) were calculated to quantify temporal trends. Spearman's rank correlation was used to assess the relationship between disease burden and the Socio-demographic Index (SDI) across G20 countries. A Bayesian age-period-cohort (BAPC) model was employed to forecast disease burden to 2050. RESULTS: In G20 countries, the mortality rate for pediatric NPEV-associated meningitis declined from 1.153 per 100,000 (95% UI: 0.902-1.453) in 1990 to 0.088 per 100,000 (95% UI: 0.070-0.113) in 2023 (EAPC: -7.72%, 95% CI: -8.19 to -7.24). The DALY rate decreased from 107.732 to 9.487 per 100,000 over the same period (EAPC: -7.35%, 95% CI: -7.80 to -6.91). Significant age-related disparities were observed: infants younger than 1 year bore the highest burden (mortality: 0.890 per 100,000; DALY: 80.649 per 100,000), despite showing smaller relative declines compared to older children. At the national level, Indonesia had the highest mortality rate (0.374 per 100,000) and DALY rate (34.344 per 100,000)\. Notably, South Africa exhibited the slowest decline among all G20 members (mortality EAPC: -3.80%; DALY EAPC: -3.76%), far below the G20 averages. Both mortality and DALY rates showed significant negative correlations with SDI across G20 countries (r = -0.849 and - 0.832, respectively, both p < 0.001). Projections to 2050 indicate continued declines, with mortality and DALY rates decreasing by over 95% in G20 countries. CONCLUSIONS: From 1990 to 2023, the burden of pediatric NPEV meningitis in G20 countries declined significantly, yet substantial intra-G20 disparities persist. Indonesia had the highest rates, India the largest absolute numbers, and South Africa the slowest decline. Infants younger than 1 remain the most vulnerable group. Future efforts should prioritize enhanced surveillance and targeted interventions in high-burden countries including Indonesia, South Africa, and India, and strengthen diagnostic capacities in lower-resourced member states to narrow gaps within this influential group of nations.

The authors interpret the findings as a significant decline in NPEV-associated meningitis burden in G20 countries over three decades, with persistent disparities across nations and age groups. 作者： Pham Khanh Hoang Luu

EVALUATING THE MECHANISMS OF PATHOGENESIS AND ANTIBODY-MEDIATED PROTECTION IN ENTEROVIRUS D68-INDUCED ACUTE FLACCID MYELITIS

Authors: Pham Khanh Hoang Luu
Original abstract (English)
Enterovirus D68 (EV-D68) is a major cause of acute flaccid myelitis (AFM), a severe neurologic disease in children that can result in sudden paralysis and lifelong disability. Currently, no approved therapies specifically target EV-D68-associated AFM. Monoclonal antibodies (mAbs) represent a promising therapeutic strategy because they combine viral neutralization through the Fab region with immune-cell recruitment through the Fc region. However, the contribution of Fc effector function to protection against EV-D68-induced neurologic disease remains unclear. This study investigated whether Fc effector function enhances protection against AFM using the partially neutralizing mAb EV68-41. Wild-type EV68-41 (EV68-41-WT), which retains Fc effector function, was compared with an Fc-deficient LALAPG variant (EV68-41-LALAPG) in an animal model of EV-D68 infection. Survival and neurologic outcomes were assessed and compared with the potently neutralizing mAb EV68-228. No significant difference in survival was observed between EV68-41-WT and EV68-41-LALAPG, with both providing approximately 60% survival. However, EV68-41-LALAPG-treated mice exhibited higher peak neurologic scores, indicating more severe limb paralysis, compared with EV68-41-WT-treated mice. In contrast, both EV68-228 variants provided 100% survival and complete neurologic protection. These findings demonstrate that Fc effector function can reduce neurologic disease severity even when it does not significantly improve survival. Together, the results suggest that effective mAb therapies for EV-D68-associated AFM should combine potent viral neutralization with Fc-mediated immune recruitment, potentially improving neurologic outcomes and reducing the burden of childhood paralysis.

厦门儿童肠道病毒D68血清流行病学:2022年横断面研究

Seroepidemiology of Enterovirus D68 in Xiamen Children: A Cross-Sectional Study in 2022
作者： Liting Wang, Qiguo Zhu, Yi Lu, Yuanyuan Wu, Zhifeng Ke, Qingbing Zheng, Longfa Xu, Tong Cheng, Rui Zhu, Jun Shen
摘要中文翻译
查看综述五要素
范围与问题
肠道病毒 D68 (EV-D68) 是一种与严重 急性弛缓性脊髓炎 (AFM) 和肺炎相关的重新出现的病原体,主要发生在儿童中,但也能够在免疫功能低下的成人中引起严重疾病。传播 数据对于了解群体免疫、年龄特异性易感性和病毒 传播 动态至关重要;然而,COVID-19 大流行之后的此类数据却很少。
证据基础与方法
我们进行了一项横断面血清学调查,以表征 2022 年中国厦门儿童中 EV-D68 的中和抗体谱。共有 453 名 0-16 岁儿童(217 名患有急性呼吸道感染 [ARTI],236 名未患有急性呼吸道感染)进行了研究。使用微中和测定法测量针对 EV-D68 STL 菌株的中和抗体 (NtAb) 滴度,血清阳性定义为滴度≥1:16。使用多变量回归和协方差分析来调整年龄和性别。

总体 血清流行率 为 96.0% (435/453),每个年龄亚组均超过 90.0%(0-1 岁:100%;1-3 岁:97.6%;3-5 岁:92.6%;≥5 岁:96.2%)。几何平均滴度 (GMT) 为 76.78 (95% CI: 68.32–85.24)。
争议与缺口
After adjustment, ARTI status was not significantly associated with seropositivity (adjusted OR = 1.42, 95% CI: 0.46–4.41, p = 0.542). In an exploratory comparison, children with ARTI showed nominally higher antibody titers than those without (Mann–Whitney U test, p = 0.003), although the absence of EV-D68-specific PCR testing precludes etiologic attribution of respiratory symptoms to EV-D68 infection.
科研与实践启示
这些发现为正在进行的血清监测、暴发 风险评估以及未来 疫苗 或免疫预防策略(如果可用)的评估提供了关键参考数据。

Seroepidemiology of Enterovirus D68 in Xiamen Children: A Cross-Sectional Study in 2022

Authors: Liting Wang, Qiguo Zhu, Yi Lu, Yuanyuan Wu, Zhifeng Ke, Qingbing Zheng, Longfa Xu, Tong Cheng, Rui Zhu, Jun Shen
Original abstract (English)
Enterovirus D68 (EV-D68) is a re-emerging pathogen associated with severe acute flaccid myelitis (AFM) and pneumonia, predominantly in children but also capable of causing severe disease in immunocompromised adults. Seroprevalence data are essential for understanding population immunity, age-specific susceptibility, and viral transmission dynamics; yet, such data for the period after the COVID-19 pandemic are scarce. We conducted a cross-sectional serological survey to characterize the neutralizing antibody profile of EV-D68 among children in Xiamen, China, in 2022. A total of 453 children aged 0–16 years (217 with acute respiratory tract infection [ARTI] and 236 without) were enrolled. Neutralizing antibody (NtAb) titers against the EV-D68 STL strain were measured using a microneutralization assay, seropositivity was defined as a titer ≥1:16. Multivariable regression and analysis of covariance were used to adjust for age and sex. Scope and question
Enterovirus D68 (EV-D68) is a re-emerging pathogen associated with severe acute flaccid myelitis (AFM) and pneumonia, predominantly in children but also capable of causing severe disease in immunocompromised adults. Seroprevalence data are essential for understanding population immunity, age-specific susceptibility, and viral transmission dynamics; yet, such data for the period after the COVID-19 pandemic are scarce.
Evidence base and method
We conducted a cross-sectional serological survey to characterize the neutralizing antibody profile of EV-D68 among children in Xiamen, China, in 2022. A total of 453 children aged 0–16 years (217 with acute respiratory tract infection [ARTI] and 236 without) were enrolled. Neutralizing antibody (NtAb) titers against the EV-D68 STL strain were measured using a microneutralization assay, seropositivity was defined as a titer ≥1:16. Multivariable regression and analysis of covariance were used to adjust for age and sex.
Consensus and conclusions
Overall seroprevalence was 96.0% (435/453), exceeding 90.0% in every age subgroup (0–1 y: 100%; 1–3 y: 97.6%; 3–5 y: 92.6%; ≥5 y: 96.2%). The geometric mean titer (GMT) was 76.78 (95% CI: 68.32–85.24).
Controversies and gaps
After adjustment, ARTI status was not significantly associated with seropositivity (adjusted OR = 1.42, 95% CI: 0.46–4.41, p = 0.542). In an exploratory comparison, children with ARTI showed nominally higher antibody titers than those without (Mann–Whitney U test, p = 0.003), although the absence of EV-D68-specific PCR testing precludes etiologic attribution of respiratory symptoms to EV-D68 infection.
Research and practice implications
These findings provide key reference data for ongoing serosurveillance, outbreak risk assessment, and the evaluation of future vaccines or immunoprophylactic strategies should they become available.
