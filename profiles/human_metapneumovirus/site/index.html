2010-2021 年全球病毒性下呼吸道疾病发病情况、住院情况和按病因划分的临床结果。

Global Viral Lower Respiratory Disease Episodes, Hospitalisations, and Clinical Outcomes by Aetiology, 2010-2021.
作者： Ekaterina Maslova, Quinn Rafferty, Jiali Lei, Chengbin Wang, Jam Suba, Maja Pasovic, Samuel B Ewald, Darwin Del Castillo, Charlotte Lupton, Yao Qiao
摘要中文翻译
查看研究七要素
问题与背景
病毒性下呼吸道疾病 (LRTD) 是全球发病率和 死亡率 的主要原因,但病原体特异性负担估计仍然有限,这给政策和临床实践带来了不确定性。该研究旨在量化 2010 年至 2021 年按病因分类的病毒性 LRTD 发作、住院治疗和严重临床结果的全球负担。
设计与对象
该研究使用2021年全球疾病负担(GBD)建模框架对2010年至2021年204个国家和地区、21个地区和7个超级地区进行了估计。数据输入包括监测系统、临床信息学、已发表的文献、调查和人口动态登记。
核心方法
使用 GBD 贝叶斯元回归工具 DisMod-MR 2.1 估算病毒 LRTD 发病率,并使用针对医疗保健可及性进行调整的特定地点入院标量得出住院情况。使用元回归贝叶斯正则化修剪模型来估计特定病因的临床结果比例,包括 ICU 需求、IMV 需求和住院 死亡率,并将其应用于住院估计。
主要结果
Globally, viral LRTD episodes increased from 82.3 million (95% UI 76.4–88.2) in 2010 to 94.9 million (88.8–101.2) in 2019, while episode rates remained stable at approximately 1200 per 100,000 population. Between 2010 and 2019, global rates of ICU need increased from 16.1 (14.3–18.0) to 23.7 (21.0–26.6) per 100,000, IMV need from 7.5 (6.7–8.4) to 11.2 (10.0–12.6), and in-hospital mortality from 9.4 (8.4–10.5) to 13.9 (12.3–15.6) per 100,000.
解释与创新
2020年之前,病毒性LRTD的全球发病率保持稳定,但住院率和严重后果大幅增加,表明临床严重程度和医疗保健需求不断上升。该研究首次提供了十多年来病毒 LRTD 的全面、特定病因的全球负担估计,强调了显着的年龄和地区差异。
科研与公卫意义
这些发现强调了全球范围内严重病毒性 LRTD 的巨大负担,特别是在老年人中,并揭示了明显的地区差异。结果支持需要持续的病因学特异性 监测、足够的医疗保健能力以及公平获得预防和治疗干预措施。
局限与证据强度
该研究依赖于模型估计而不是原始数据,这可能会带来不确定性并限制对特定环境的普遍性。由于缺乏直接测量以及数据输入和建模假设中的潜在偏差,证据强度中等。

Global Viral Lower Respiratory Disease Episodes, Hospitalisations, and Clinical Outcomes by Aetiology, 2010-2021.

Authors: Ekaterina Maslova, Quinn Rafferty, Jiali Lei, Chengbin Wang, Jam Suba, Maja Pasovic, Samuel B Ewald, Darwin Del Castillo, Charlotte Lupton, Yao Qiao
Original abstract (English)
INTRODUCTION: Viral lower respiratory tract disease (LRTD) is a major cause of global morbidity and mortality, but pathogen-specific estimates remain limited. We aimed to quantify the global burden of viral LRTD episodes, hospitalisations, and severe clinical outcomes by aetiology from 2010 to 2021. METHODS: We used the Global Burden of Disease (GBD) 2021 modelling framework to generate estimates of viral LRTD incidence, hospitalisations, and clinical outcomes from 2010 to 2021 for 204 countries and territories, 21 regions, and seven super-regions. Aetiology-specific estimates were generated for five viral categories: influenza, respiratory syncytial virus (RSV), human metapneumovirus (hMPV), SARS-CoV-2, and "other" viral pathogens. Data inputs included surveillance systems, clinical informatics, published literature, surveys, and vital registration. Viral LRTD incidence was estimated using DisMod-MR 2.1, a GBD Bayesian meta-regression tool for disease incidence. We then applied location-specific admission scalars, adjusted for healthcare access, to derive hospitalisations. Aetiology-specific clinical outcome proportions-including intensive care unit (ICU) need, invasive mechanical ventilation (IMV) need, and in-hospital mortality-were estimated using meta-regression-Bayesian, regularised, trimming models. These were then applied to the hospitalisation estimates. COVID-19 incidence and hospitalisations were estimated using established GBD COVID-19 methods. All estimates are reported with 95% uncertainty intervals (UIs). RESULTS: Globally, viral LRTD episodes increased from 82.3 million (95% UI 76.4-88.2) in 2010 to 94.9 million (88.8-101.2) in 2019, while episode rates remained relatively stable at approximately 1200 per 100,000 population. Over the same period, hospitalisation rates increased by 40%, from 103.7 (96.1-111.4) to 145.4 (134.6-156.4) per 100,000 population. In 2020 and 2021, the emergence of SARS-CoV-2 resulted in sharp increases in burden, with episodes exceeding 1.1 billion in 2020 and 1.5 billion in 2021. During this period, 2020-2021, episode and hospitalisation rates associated with influenza, RSV, and hMPV declined by nearly 50%. Between 2010 and 2019, global rates of ICU need increased from 16.1 (14.3-18.0) to 23.7 (21.0-26.6) per 100,000 population, IMV need from 7.5 (6.7-8.4) to 11.2 (10.0-12.6), and in-hospital mortality from 9.4 (8.4-10.5) to 13.9 (12.3-15.6). In 2020-2021, SARS-CoV-2 dominated severe outcomes, with in-hospital mortality reaching 87.7 (83.3-92.2) per 100,000 population in 2021. Across the study period, older adults consistently experienced the highest rates of severe outcomes, and substantial regional variation was observed. CONCLUSIONS: Before 2020, global incidence of viral LRTD was stable; however, hospitalisations and severe outcomes increased substantially, suggesting rising clinical severity and healthcare demand. The COVID-19 pandemic profoundly altered the global viral LRTD landscape, driving unprecedented increases in hospitalisations, intensive care use, and mortality, while suppressing other respiratory viruses in the years 2020 and 2021. These findings demonstrate a substantial burden of severe viral LRTD globally, with marked age and regional variations, underscoring the importance of sustained aetiology-specific surveillance, adequate healthcare capacity, and equitable access to preventive and therapeutic interventions.

针对呼吸道病毒的亲环蛋白抑制剂 抗病毒药物 谱的优化。

Optimization of the antiviral spectrum of cyclophilin inhibitors targeting respiratory viruses.
作者： Laurent Softic, Bryan Jimenez-Araya, Nazim Ahnou, Maxime Duval, Quentin Nevers, Rozenn Brillet, Jean-Francois Guichou, Christophe Rodriguez, Patrice Bruscella, Jean-Michel Pawlotsky
摘要中文翻译摘要 我们评估了 36 种基于尿素的小分子亲环蛋白抑制剂 (SMCypIs) 库的 抗病毒药物 对抗人呼吸道合胞病毒 (hRSV) 和 人偏肺病毒 (hMPV) 的活性。结构谱分析揭示了 抗病毒药物 功效的病毒特异性结构要求。通过结合每种病毒确定的关键决定因素,我们合理地设计了优化的双作用衍生物。其中,优化的化合物 F832/33 成为一种有效的广谱 抗病毒药物 药物,对 hRSV 和 hMPV 具有纳摩尔级活性,对亲环蛋白 (Cyp) 肽基脯氨酰异构酶 (PPIase) 活性具有亚微摩尔级抑制作用,并且没有可检测到的细胞毒性。有趣的是,在 Cyp PPIase 抑制和 抗病毒药物 效力之间没有观察到直接相关性,这表明 抗病毒药物 活性需要 Cyp 参与而不是酶抑制本身。使用阿拉泊韦的竞争测定证实,SMCypIs 的 抗病毒药物 效应取决于它们与 Cyp 的特异性相互作用。除了其双重功效之外,F832/33 在永生化细胞系和完全分化的人鼻上皮细胞(一种生理相关的呼吸道感染模型)中,对其他临床相关呼吸道病原体(包括副流感病毒 3 型、肠道病毒 D68 以及祖先和 Omicron 严重急性呼吸综合征冠状病毒 2 株)表现出强大的 抗病毒药物 活性。总的来说,我们的结果表明,结构-活性关系引导的优化可以成功地应用于扩展 SMCypIs 的 抗病毒药物 谱,将 F832/33 定位为有前途的 宿主 靶向广谱 抗病毒药物 候选药物。需要进一步的体内研究来评估其治疗潜力并阐明其精确的作用机制。

问题与背景
该研究旨在评估基于尿素的小分子亲环蛋白抑制剂库对人呼吸道合胞病毒(hRSV)和人偏肺病毒(hMPV)的抗病毒药物活性,以满足针对呼吸道病毒的广谱抗病毒药物药物的需求。

该研究涉及针对 hRSV 和 hMPV 的 36 种亲环蛋白抑制剂的体外测试,随后对双作用衍生物进行优化,并在永生化细胞系和完全分化的人鼻上皮细胞中进行评估。
核心方法
采用结构-谱分析和结构-活性关系引导的优化来设计和测试优化的化合物,并使用阿拉泊韦进行竞争测定来确认作用机制。
主要结果
优化的化合物 F832/33 表现出强效、广谱的 抗病毒药物 活性,对 hRSV 和 hMPV 具有纳摩尔级功效,对亲环蛋白 PPIase 活性具有亚微摩尔级抑制作用,无细胞毒性,并且对副流感病毒 3 型、肠道病毒 D68 和 SARS-CoV-2 株具有额外的活性。

研究结果表明,抗病毒药物 活性需要亲环蛋白参与而不是酶抑制,并且结构-活性关系引导的优化成功扩展了 抗病毒药物 亲环蛋白抑制剂谱。

F832/33 被定位为一种有前途的 宿主 靶向广谱 抗病毒药物 候选药物,对治疗和预防多种呼吸道病原体引起的感染具有潜在影响。

Optimization of the antiviral spectrum of cyclophilin inhibitors targeting respiratory viruses.

Authors: Laurent Softic, Bryan Jimenez-Araya, Nazim Ahnou, Maxime Duval, Quentin Nevers, Rozenn Brillet, Jean-Francois Guichou, Christophe Rodriguez, Patrice Bruscella, Jean-Michel Pawlotsky
Original abstract (English)
ABSTRACT We evaluated the antiviral activity of a library of 36 urea-based small-molecule cyclophilin inhibitors (SMCypIs) against human respiratory syncytial virus (hRSV) and human metapneumovirus (hMPV). Structure–spectrum analysis revealed virus-specific structural requirements for antiviral efficacy. By combining the key determinants identified for each virus, we rationally designed optimized dual-acting derivatives. Among these, the optimized compound F832/33 emerged as a potent, broad-spectrum antiviral agent with nanomolar activity against both hRSV and hMPV, submicromolar inhibition of cyclophilin (Cyp) peptidyl-prolyl isomerase (PPIase) activity, and no detectable cytotoxicity. Interestingly, no direct correlation was observed between Cyp PPIase inhibition and antiviral potency, suggesting that Cyp engagement rather than enzymatic inhibition per se is required for antiviral activity. Competition assays with alisporivir confirmed that the antiviral effects of SMCypIs depend on their specific interaction with Cyp. Beyond its dual efficacy, F832/33 displayed strong antiviral activity against additional clinically relevant respiratory pathogens, including parainfluenza virus type 3, enterovirus D68, and both ancestral and Omicron severe acute respiratory syndrome coronavirus 2 strains, in immortalized cell lines and fully differentiated human nasal epithelial cells, a physiologically relevant model of respiratory infection. Collectively, our results demonstrate that structure–activity relationship-guided optimization can be successfully applied to expand the antiviral spectrum of SMCypIs, positioning F832/33 as a promising host-targeted broad-spectrum antiviral candidate. Further in vivo studies are warranted to evaluate its therapeutic potential and clarify its precise mechanism of action.

Question and background
The study aimed to evaluate the antiviral activity of a library of urea-based small-molecule cyclophilin inhibitors against human respiratory syncytial virus (hRSV) and human metapneumovirus (hMPV), addressing the need for broad-spectrum antiviral agents targeting respiratory viruses.
Design and population
The research involved in vitro testing of 36 cyclophilin inhibitors against hRSV and hMPV, with subsequent optimization of dual-acting derivatives and evaluation in immortalized cell lines and fully differentiated human nasal epithelial cells.
Core methods
Structure–spectrum analysis and structure–activity relationship-guided optimization were employed to design and test optimized compounds, with competition assays using alisporivir to confirm the mechanism of action.
Main results
The optimized compound F832/33 demonstrated potent, broad-spectrum antiviral activity with nanomolar efficacy against hRSV and hMPV, submicromolar inhibition of cyclophilin PPIase activity, no cytotoxicity, and additional activity against parainfluenza virus type 3, enterovirus D68, and SARS-CoV-2 strains.
Interpretation and novelty
The findings indicate that cyclophilin engagement rather than enzymatic inhibition is required for antiviral activity, and structure–activity relationship-guided optimization successfully expanded the antiviral spectrum of cyclophilin inhibitors.
Scientific and public-health significance
F832/33 is positioned as a promising host-targeted broad-spectrum antiviral candidate with potential implications for treating and preventing infections caused by multiple respiratory pathogens.
Limitations and evidence strength
The evidence is limited to in vitro studies, and further in vivo research is needed to evaluate therapeutic potential and clarify the precise mechanism of action; however, the robust in vitro results support a moderate evidence strength.

医学图像增强的经典方法和机器学习方法的比较评估

Comparative evaluation of classical and machine learning methods for medical image enhancement
作者： Md. Mehedi Hasan, Sujon Chandra Sutradhar, Zannatul Ferdushie, Rabeya Basri

Comparative evaluation of classical and machine learning methods for medical image enhancement

Authors: Md. Mehedi Hasan, Sujon Chandra Sutradhar, Zannatul Ferdushie, Rabeya Basri
Original abstract (English)
Medical imaging is critical for diagnostic accuracy, yet raw images often suffer from noise and low contrast. This study provides a comparative evaluation of classical methods, namely the Laplace transform (LT), Sobel operator (SO), and histogram equalization (HE), against a data-driven convolutional neural network (CNN) using the musculoskeletal radiographs (MURA) and Human Metapneumovirus (HMPV) lung computed tomography (CT) datasets. While quantitative analysis shows that HE and SO significantly outperform other methods in isolated contrast enhancement and edge definition, they often introduce artifacts. In contrast, the CNN based approach demonstrates superior detail preservation and entropy, offering a more balanced and adaptive solution for diverse diagnostic requirements.
