印度严重人类偏肺病毒感染的趋势

Trends of severe human metapneumovirus infections in India
作者： Neetu Vijay, Sumit Dutt Bhardwaj, Varsha Potdar, Rizwan Abdulkader Suliankatchi, Labanya Mukhopadhyay, Ramasamy Sabarinathan, Tapan Majumdar, Debasis Biswas, Rahul Narang, Manohar Lal Choudhary
摘要中文翻译背景:2024年12月和2025年1月初,中国报告了北半球冬季急性呼吸道感染增加,同时季节性流感、鼻病毒、呼吸道合胞病毒和人呼吸道合胞病毒(hMPV)的检测也增加。我们通过现有的全印度急性呼吸道疾病(ARI)和严重急性呼吸道感染(SARI)监测网络加强了hMPV的监测。方法:为了在不增加实验室负担的情况下迅速生成全国性的情况数据,每个监测点被要求在2025年1月至4月期间每周对最多五例符合条件的SARI样本进行hMPV检测。符合条件的样本是住院SARI病例,这些病例对流感、SARS-CoV-2和/或呼吸道合胞病毒(RSV)检测呈阴性,适用于基于年龄的检测算法,涉及70个实验室。结果:在8,246例入组的SARI病例中,4,149例符合条件的样本在限制性每周采样策略下进行了hMPV检测,其中59例呈阳性(1.42%;95% CI: 1.10-1.83)。各年龄组的阳性率从0.5%到2.9%不等,在<2岁的儿童中最高。大多数患者(63%)年龄<2岁。男性的阳性率较高(1.7%对比女性的1.1%),在1月达到最高(2.1%),然后在4月降至0.5%。最常见的症状是咳嗽(95%)、发热(88%)和呼吸困难(58%)。54/59例患者的结果可用:11例需要ICU住院,51例出院,3例死亡。在7例合并症患者中,所有患者均出院。超过一半的测序病毒属于B1亚型,其次是B2和A2b2。结论:在有限的哨点监测中,hMPV在严重呼吸道感染中检出率较低,但主要出现在幼儿中。需要持续多季节监测,以更好地定义印度的年龄特异性负担、时间模式和循环基因型。临床试验编号:不适用。

Trends of severe human metapneumovirus infections in India

Authors: Neetu Vijay, Sumit Dutt Bhardwaj, Varsha Potdar, Rizwan Abdulkader Suliankatchi, Labanya Mukhopadhyay, Ramasamy Sabarinathan, Tapan Majumdar, Debasis Biswas, Rahul Narang, Manohar Lal Choudhary
Original Abstract
BACKGROUND: In December 2024 and early January 2025, China reported increased acute respiratory infections with increased detections of seasonal influenza, rhinovirus, RSV and hMPV during the Northern Hemisphere winter. We strengthened hMPV surveillance through the existing pan-India surveillance network for acute respiratory illness (ARI) and severe acute respiratory infection (SARI). METHODOLOGY: To rapidly generate nationwide situational data without overburdening laboratories already performing routine respiratory virus surveillance, each site was asked to test up to five eligible SARI samples per week for hMPV from January to April 2025. Eligible samples were hospitalized SARI cases that were negative for influenza, SARS-CoV-2 and/or RSV, as applicable to age-based testing algorithms, across 70 laboratories. RESULTS: Of 8,246 enrolled SARI cases, 4,149 eligible samples were tested for hMPV under the capped weekly sampling strategy, and 59 were positive (1.42%; 95% CI: 1.10-1.83). Positivity ranged from 0.5% to 2.9% across age groups and was highest in children aged <2 years. Most patients (63%) were aged <2 years. Positivity was higher among males (1.7% vs 1.1% in females), and was highest in January (2.1%) before declining to 0.5% in April. The most common symptoms were cough (95%), fever (88%), and breathlessness (58%). Outcomes were available for 54/59 patients: 11 required ICU admission, 51 were discharged alive, and 3 died. Among seven patients with comorbidities, all were discharged alive. More than half of the sequenced viruses belonged to clade B1, followed by B2 and A2b2. CONCLUSION: In this limited sentinel surveillance of severe respiratory infections, hMPV was detected infrequently but was observed predominantly among young children. Continued multi-season surveillance is needed to better define age-specific burden, temporal patterns, and circulating genotypes in India. CLINICAL TRIAL NUMBER: Not applicable.

The Resurgence of Respiratory Viruses After COVID-19: Molecular Insights from Pediatric SARI in Kashmir.

Authors: Sanam Rasool Wani, Bashir Ahmad Fomda, Mushtaq Ahmad Bhat, Gulnaz Bashir, Shuguftah Roohi, Suraiya Mir, Fozia Subzar, Sheikh Imtiyaz
Original Abstract
BACKGROUND: Respiratory viruses are major contributors to severe acute respiratory infections (SARI) in children. Data on their molecular epidemiology in northern India remains limited, particularly in the post-COVID-19 period. This study aimed to determine the prevalence and distribution of respiratory viral pathogens among hospitalized pediatric SARI patients in Kashmir. METHODS: A prospective study was conducted from October 2023 to April 2025 at Sher-i-Kashmir Institute of Medical Sciences, Srinagar. Children aged ≤15 years meeting the World Health Organization SARI case definition were enrolled. Nasopharyngeal and/or oropharyngeal samples were collected and tested using real-time polymerase chain reaction assays for SARS-CoV-2, Influenza A and B, Respiratory Syncytial virus (RSV) A and B, Adenovirus, Human metapneumovirus, Rhinovirus, and Para-influenza viruses. RESULTS: A total of 326 pediatric SARI cases were included. At least one respiratory virus was detected in 200 patients (61.3%). RSV was the most frequently detected virus, with RSV-B identified in 111 cases (34.0%) and RSV-A in 26 cases (7.9%). Influenza viruses accounted for a smaller proportion, including influenza A(H1N1) in 12 cases (3.6%), influenza A(H3N2) in 6 cases (1.8%), and influenza B (Victoria lineage) in 21 cases (6.4%). Other viruses detected included Adenovirus, Para-influenza virus, Rhinovirus, and Human metapneumovirus. Most infections occurred during winter months and predominantly affected infants. CONCLUSION: Respiratory viruses account for a substantial proportion of pediatric SARI cases in Kashmir, with RSV emerging as the predominant pathogen in the post-pandemic period. Strengthening molecular surveillance may improve diagnostic stewardship and guide targeted prevention strategies.

针对呼吸道病毒的亲环蛋白抑制剂的抗病毒谱优化

Optimization of the antiviral spectrum of cyclophilin inhibitors targeting respiratory viruses
作者： Laurent Softic, Bryan Jimenez-Araya, Nazim Ahnou, Maxime Duval, Quentin Nevers, Rozenn Brillet, Jean‐François Guichou, Christophe Rodriguez, Patrice Bruscella, Jean–Michel Pawlotsky
摘要中文翻译摘要 我们评估了 36 种基于尿素的小分子亲环蛋白抑制剂 (SMCypIs) 库对人呼吸道合胞病毒 (hRSV) 和人偏肺病毒 (hMPV) 的抗病毒活性。结构谱分析揭示了抗病毒功效的病毒特异性结构要求。通过结合每种病毒确定的关键决定因素,我们合理地设计了优化的双作用衍生物。其中,优化的化合物 F832/33 成为一种有效的广谱抗病毒剂,对 hRSV 和 hMPV 具有纳摩尔级活性,对亲环蛋白 (Cyp) 肽基脯氨酰异构酶 (PPIase) 活性具有亚微摩尔级抑制作用,且未检测到细胞毒性。有趣的是,在 Cyp PPIase 抑制和抗病毒效力之间没有观察到直接相关性,这表明抗病毒活性需要 Cyp 参与而不是酶抑制本身。阿拉泊韦的竞争试验证实,SMCypIs 的抗病毒作用取决于它们与 Cyp 的特异性相互作用。除了其双重功效之外,F832/33 在永生化细胞系和完全分化的人鼻上皮细胞(一种生理相关的呼吸道感染模型)中对其他临床相关呼吸道病原体(包括副流感病毒 3 型、肠道病毒 D68 以及祖先和 Omicron 严重急性呼吸综合征冠状病毒 2 株)表现出强大的抗病毒活性。总的来说,我们的结果表明,结构-活性关系引导的优化可以成功地应用于扩大 SMCypIs 的抗病毒谱,将 F832/33 定位为有前途的针对宿主的广谱抗病毒候选药物。需要进一步的体内研究来评估其治疗潜力并阐明其精确的作用机制。

Original Abstract
ABSTRACT We evaluated the antiviral activity of a library of 36 urea-based small-molecule cyclophilin inhibitors (SMCypIs) against human respiratory syncytial virus (hRSV) and human metapneumovirus (hMPV). Structure–spectrum analysis revealed virus-specific structural requirements for antiviral efficacy. Comparative evaluation of classical and machine learning methods for medical image enhancement

Authors: Md. Mehedi Hasan, Sujon Chandra Sutradhar, Zannatul Ferdushie, Rabeya Basri
Original Abstract
Medical imaging is critical for diagnostic accuracy, yet raw images often suffer from noise and low contrast. This study provides a comparative evaluation of classical methods, namely the Laplace transform (LT), Sobel operator (SO), and histogram equalization (HE), against a data-driven convolutional neural network (CNN) using the musculoskeletal radiographs (MURA) and Human Metapneumovirus (HMPV) lung computed tomography (CT) datasets. While quantitative analysis shows that HE and SO significantly outperform other methods in isolated contrast enhancement and edge definition, they often introduce artifacts. In contrast, the CNN based approach demonstrates superior detail preservation and entropy, offering a more balanced and adaptive solution for diverse diagnostic requirements. Our findings statistically validate that although classical operators remain highly effective for specific boundary detection tasks, machine learning (ML) frameworks provide the most robust performance for cross-modality image enhancement, bridging the gap between raw data acquisition and clinical interpretation.
