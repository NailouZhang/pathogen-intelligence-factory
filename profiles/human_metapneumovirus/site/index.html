呼吸道合胞病毒和 人偏肺病毒 组合病毒样颗粒 疫苗 IVX-A12 在 60 岁以上成人中的 2 期试验

A Phase 2 Trial of a Respiratory Syncytial Virus and Human Metapneumovirus Combination Virus-Like Particle Vaccine IVX-A12 in Adults ≥60 Years of Age
作者： Temitope Oyedele, Lawrence D. Sher, Kelly Morales, Craig Shapiro, Elizabeth M. Adams, Margaret MacDonald, Judy Wen, John A Pura, Kathryn Shoemaker, Jennifer Price
摘要中文翻译摘要 背景 老年人(≥60岁)感染呼吸道合胞病毒(RSV)或人偏肺病毒(hMPV)后易出现严重后果。IVX-A12是一种正在研究中的疫苗,利用病毒样颗粒(VLPs)诱导针对RSV和hMPV的免疫应答。方法 本项II期试验(NCT06481579)将≥60岁的成年人按1:1比例随机分配,接受单剂量IVX-A12(150 μg RSV/150 μg hMPV)或已获批的RSV疫苗RSVPreF3(120 μg,与AS01E佐剂联合使用)。通过接种后180天评估安全性与免疫原性。结果 总共143名参与者接受了IVX-A12(n = 71)或RSVPreF3(n = 72)。接种后7天内报告的预期不良反应发生率分别为:IVX-A12 25例(35.2%),RSVPreF3 36例(50.0%)。非预期不良事件(AEs)在第29天收集,发生率分别为:IVX-A12 5例(7.0%),RSVPreF3 11例(15.3%)。严重不良事件(IVX-A12 2例 [2.8%];RSVPreF3 1例 [1.4%])和需要医疗干预的不良事件(IVX-A12 1例 [1.4%];RSVPreF3 9例 [12.5%])在第181天前均有记录。未发生任何特殊关注的不良事件和导致研究终止的不良事件。在第29天,IVX-A12和RSVPreF3接种均提高了RSV A型中和抗体(nAb)水平,几何均值倍增(GMFR)分别为3.4和6.5;RSV B型nAb水平GMFR分别为2.9和5.4。此外,IVX-A12还提高了hMPV A型(GMFR 3.0)和hMPV B型(GMFR 2.9)nAb水平,而RSVPreF3则没有。结论 IVX-A12和RSVPreF3均耐受良好,并诱导了针对其靶向病原体的中和抗体反应。鉴于IVX-A12诱导的中和抗体反应,有必要进一步研究RSV-hMPV VLP组合疫苗。

A Phase 2 Trial of a Respiratory Syncytial Virus and Human Metapneumovirus Combination Virus-Like Particle Vaccine IVX-A12 in Adults ≥60 Years of Age

Authors: Temitope Oyedele, Lawrence D. Sher, Kelly Morales, Craig Shapiro, Elizabeth M. Adams, Margaret MacDonald, Judy Wen, John A Pura, Kathryn Shoemaker, Jennifer Price
Original abstract (English)
Abstract Background Older adults (≥60 years) are susceptible to severe outcomes from infection with respiratory syncytial virus (RSV) or human metapneumovirus (hMPV). IVX-A12 is an investigational vaccine that uses virus-like particles (VLPs) to elicit an immune response against RSV and hMPV. Methods This Phase 2 trial (NCT06481579) randomized (1:1) adults ≥60 years of age to receive a single dose of IVX-A12 (150 μg RSV/150 μg hMPV) or the licensed RSV vaccine RSVPreF3 120 μg with AS01E adjuvant. Safety and immunogenicity were evaluated through 180 days post-vaccination. Results Overall, 143 participants received IVX-A12 (n = 71) or RSVPreF3 (n = 72). Solicited adverse reactions were reported through 7 days post-vaccination (IVX-A12, 25 [35.2%]; RSVPreF3, 36 [50.0%]). Unsolicited adverse events (AEs) were collected through Day 29 (IVX-A12, 5 [7.0%]; RSVPreF3, 11 [15.3%]). Serious AEs (IVX-A12, 2 [2.8%]; RSVPreF3, 1 [1.4%]) and medically attended AEs (IVX-A12, 1 [1.4%]; RSVPreF3, 9 [12.5%]) were documented through Day 181. There were no AEs of special interest and AEs leading to study discontinuation. At Day 29, IVX-A12 and RSVPreF3 administration increased RSV A neutralizing antibody (nAb) levels with geometric mean fold rise (GMFR) of 3.4 and 6.5, and RSV B nAb levels with GMFR of 2.9 and 5.4, respectively. IVX-A12, but not RSVPreF3, also increased hMPV A (GMFR 3.0) and hMPV B (GMFR 2.9) nAb levels. Conclusions IVX-A12 and RSVPreF3 were well tolerated and elicited nAb responses against their targeted pathogens. Given the neutralizing antibody response following IVX-A12, further investigation of an RSV-hMPV VLP combination vaccine is warranted.

Question and background
Older adults (≥60 years) are susceptible to severe outcomes from infection with respiratory syncytial virus (RSV) or human metapneumovirus (hMPV). This study aimed to evaluate the safety and immunogenicity of a combination vaccine targeting both pathogens.
Design and population
This Phase 2 trial randomized adults ≥60 years of age to receive a single dose of IVX-A12 (150 μg RSV/150 μg hMPV) or the licensed RSV vaccine RSVPreF3 (120 μg with AS01E adjuvant). Overall, 143 participants were enrolled, with 71 receiving IVX-A12 and 72 receiving RSVPreF3.
Core methods
Safety and immunogenicity were evaluated through 180 days post-vaccination. Solicited adverse reactions were monitored through Day 7, while unsolicited adverse events were collected through Day 29. Serious adverse events and medically attended adverse events were documented through Day 181.
Main results
Solicited adverse reactions occurred in 25 (35.2%) participants receiving IVX-A12 and 36 (50.0%) receiving RSVPreF3. Unsolicited adverse events were reported in 5 (7.0%) and 11 (15.3%) participants, respectively. Serious adverse events were observed in 2 (2.8%) and 1 (1.4%) participants, while medically attended adverse events were reported in 1 (1.4%) and 9 (12.5%) participants.
Interpretation and novelty
IVX-A12 and RSVPreF3 were well tolerated and elicited neutralizing antibody responses against their targeted pathogens. Notably, IVX-A12 also increased neutralizing antibody levels against hMPV A and B, which was not observed with RSVPreF3.
Scientific and public-health significance
The findings suggest that IVX-A12 may offer broader protection against both RSV and hMPV, which could inform future vaccine development and public health strategies for older adults at risk of severe respiratory infections.
Limitations and evidence strength
The study was limited to a single dose and short follow-up period, which may affect the generalizability of the results. The evidence is moderate in strength due to the observational nature of the safety assessments and the lack of long-term efficacy data.

作者： Daisuke Kitagawa, Antonio Di Lorenzo, Takumi Okamura, Soshi Hachisuka, Naohiro Yamamoto, Hiroki Nishikawa, Masayuki Onaka, Rika Suzuki, Soma Suzuki, Ayu Mukai
摘要中文翻译摘要 快速抗原检测广泛用于儿童呼吸道感染 (RTI) 的护理点 诊断,但病毒载量对敏感性的影响尚未全面表征多种儿科呼吸道病毒。在儿科中心进行的这项前瞻性配对样本研究中,从患有 RTI 的儿童身上同时采集了两份鼻咽拭子。使用基于抗原的快速检测试剂盒(ImunoAce 系列,Tauns Laboratories,Inc.)对一根拭子进行了 SARS-CoV-2、甲型流感、乙型流感、腺病毒、人偏肺病毒 (hMPV) 和呼吸道合胞病毒的检测;另一个使用 FilmArray 呼吸面板 (bioMérieux) 进行测试。任一方法呈阳性的样本均需进行验证性定量实时 PCR 以确定病毒载量。敏感性按病毒载量分层。总共分析了来自 1,232 名儿童(中位年龄:3.0 岁 [IQR:1.0–6.0];53.7% 男性)的 1,584 个配对样本。抗原测试的敏感性范围为 20.6%(腺病毒)至 79.7%(hMPV),而所有六个目标的特异性均超过 99%,总体一致性范围为 90.7% 至 99.0%。对于每种病毒,抗原检测阳性率随病毒载量急剧增加,病毒载量≥10 6 –10 7 拷贝/100 μL 时接近 90-100%,病毒载量≤10 4 –10 5 拷贝/100 μL 时下降到 50% 以下。针对常见儿科呼吸道病毒的基于抗原的快速检测显示出高特异性和病毒载量依赖性敏感性,可检测几乎所有具有高病毒载量的样本,无论目标病毒如何。这些发现支持抗原检测在识别病毒负荷最高的儿童(最有可能患有急性感染)方面的作用。重要性 呼吸道病毒快速抗原检测相对于基于 PCR 的检测的敏感性尚未完全确定。这项研究针对 1,584 个临床样本的多重 PCR 参考标准,评估了六种常见儿科呼吸道病毒(SARS-CoV-2、甲型流感、乙型流感、腺病毒、人偏肺病毒 和呼吸道合胞病毒)的抗原测试,并将测试性能直接与定量病毒载量联系起来。我们表明,抗原测试敏感性强烈且始终依赖于病毒载量,在高病毒载量时接近 90-100%,并且对所有六个目标具有非常高的特异性。这些发现阐明了快速抗原检测的临床作用:它可靠地识别出病毒负荷最高的儿童,这些儿童最有可能患有急性症状感染,需要立即采取临床行动。

Diagnostic performance of antigen-based rapid test kits for six respiratory viruses in children: comparison with a multiplex PCR panel and correlation with viral load.

Authors: Daisuke Kitagawa, Antonio Di Lorenzo, Takumi Okamura, Soshi Hachisuka, Naohiro Yamamoto, Hiroki Nishikawa, Masayuki Onaka, Rika Suzuki, Soma Suzuki, Ayu Mukai
Original abstract (English)
ABSTRACT Rapid antigen tests are widely used for point-of-care diagnosis of respiratory tract infection (RTI) in children, but how the sensitivity is influenced by viral load has not been comprehensively characterized across multiple pediatric respiratory viruses. In this prospective, paired-specimen study conducted at the pediatric center, two simultaneous nasopharyngeal swabs were obtained from children with RTI. One swab was tested using antigen-based rapid test kits (ImunoAce series, Tauns Laboratories, Inc.) for SARS-CoV-2, influenza A, influenza B, adenovirus, human metapneumovirus (hMPV), and respiratory syncytial virus; the other was tested using the FilmArray Respiratory Panel (bioMérieux). Specimens positive by either method underwent confirmatory quantitative real-time PCR to determine viral load. Sensitivity was stratified by viral load. A total of 1,584 paired specimens from 1,232 children (median age: 3.0 years [IQR: 1.0–6.0]; 53.7% male) were analyzed. Sensitivity of the antigen tests ranged from 20.6% (adenovirus) to 79.7% (hMPV), while specificity exceeded 99% for all six targets and overall agreement ranged from 90.7% to 99.0%. For every virus, antigen test positivity increased steeply with viral load, approaching 90–100% at viral loads ≥10 6 –10 7 copies/100 μL and falling below 50% at loads ≤10 4 –10 5 copies/100 μL. Antigen-based rapid tests for common pediatric respiratory viruses showed high specificity and viral-load-dependent sensitivity, detecting nearly all specimens with high viral load regardless of the target virus. These findings support a role for antigen testing in identifying children with the highest viral burden—who are most likely to have acute infection. IMPORTANCE The sensitivity of rapid antigen tests for respiratory viruses relative to PCR-based testing has been incompletely characterized. This study evaluated antigen tests for six common pediatric respiratory viruses—SARS-CoV-2, influenza A, influenza B, adenovirus, human metapneumovirus, and respiratory syncytial virus—against a multiplex PCR reference standard across 1,584 clinical specimens and linked test performance directly to quantitative viral load. We show that antigen test sensitivity is strongly and consistently dependent on viral load, approaching 90–100% at high viral loads with very high specificity across all six targets. These findings clarify the clinical role of rapid antigen testing: it reliably identifies children with the highest viral burden, who are most likely to have acute, symptomatic infection warranting immediate clinical action.

Question and background
The study aimed to characterize how the sensitivity of rapid antigen tests for respiratory viruses is influenced by viral load, particularly for human metapneumovirus (hMPV). The sensitivity of these tests relative to PCR-based testing has been incompletely characterized.
Design and population
This prospective, paired-specimen study was conducted at a pediatric center, where two simultaneous nasopharyngeal swabs were obtained from children with respiratory tract infections (RTI). A total of 1,584 paired specimens from 1,232 children (median age: 3.0 years [IQR: 1.0–6.0]; 53.7% male) were analyzed.

Specimens positive by either the antigen-based rapid test kits or the multiplex PCR panel underwent confirmatory quantitative real-time PCR to determine viral load. The study evaluated antigen tests for six common pediatric respiratory viruses, including hMPV, against a multiplex PCR reference standard across 1,584 clinical specimens.
Main results
Sensitivity of the antigen tests ranged from 20.6% (adenovirus) to 79.7% (hMPV), while specificity exceeded 99% for all six targets and overall agreement ranged from 90.7% to 99.0%. For every virus, antigen test positivity increased steeply with viral load, approaching 90–100% at viral loads ≥10^6–10^7 copies/100 μL and falling below 50% at loads ≤10^4–10^5 copies/100 μL.
Interpretation and novelty
The findings clarify the clinical role of rapid antigen testing, showing that it reliably identifies children with the highest viral burden, who are most likely to have acute, symptomatic infection warranting immediate clinical action.
Scientific and public-health significance
These findings support the use of antigen testing in identifying children with high viral loads of hMPV, which can inform clinical decision-making and prioritize interventions for those with acute, symptomatic infections.
Limitations and evidence strength
The study's evidence is based on a single center and may not be generalizable to other populations. The evidence strength is moderate, as the results are based on a well-characterized paired-specimen design with a clear link to viral load.

Authors: Jia-Yi Xie, Tong Ji, Min Zhou, Ling-Li Zheng, Ke-Xin Zhang, Qian Lan, Yi-Lin Mai, Meng Xu, Zheng-Fei Qi, Qingshan Bill Fu
Original abstract (English)
Human metapneumovirus (HMPV) is an important respiratory pathogen for which specific antiviral therapies are currently unavailable, and the mechanisms by which HMPV regulates host mitochondrial homeostasis remain poorly understood. Here, we investigated the role of HMPV nucleoprotein (N) in mitophagy regulation and mitochondrial quality control, with a focus on its association with selective autophagy-related factors. Using recombinant protein purification, surface plasmon resonance, co-immunoprecipitation, confocal microscopy, mitochondrial-targeted mitophagy reporter analysis, and immunoblotting, we examined the interaction between HMPV N and host autophagy-associated factors and evaluated its effects on mitophagy. We demonstrated that HMPV N interacts with TAX1BP1 and LC3, two key components of the selective autophagy machinery. HMPV N signals were spatially associated with mitochondrial regions, and HMPV N expression increased the spatial association of TAX1BP1-positive structures with mitochondria, while reducing the spatial association of LC3-positive structures with TOM20-positive mitochondria under stress conditions. Consistently, HMPV N expression reduced CCCP-induced mitochondrial delivery to acidic compartments and attenuated TOM20 degradation, while exerting limited effects on basal mitochondrial turnover. These findings identify HMPV N as a viral regulator of stress-induced mitophagy and suggest that its interactions with selective autophagy-associated factors contribute to altered mitochondrial homeostasis during infection.
