解析2024-2025年Réunion Island 基孔肯雅病毒疫情的流行病学与传播动态。

Unraveling the epidemiological and dispersal dynamics of the 2024-2025 chikungunya virus epidemic on Réunion Island.
作者： Etienne Frumence, Raphaëlle Klitting, Kyla Serres, Yu-Cai Shao, Filippo Monti, Muriel Vincent, Mandev S. Gill, Marc A. Suchard, Philippe Lemey, Xavier de Lamballerie
摘要中文翻译Réunion Island在2024-2025年经历了一场大规模的基孔肯雅病毒疫情,确诊病例超过54,000例。这是继20年前首次疫情高峰之后,该岛的第二次主要基孔肯雅热疫情。有人断言,此次新的暴发疫情起源于一次单一的引入事件,这为利用病毒基因组数据来解析引入的传播传播链的流行病学与传播动态提供了机会。我们对疫情期间收集的超过3,000个病毒基因组样本进行了测序。借助这一基因组数据集,我们采用多种流行病学和系统发育动力学方法,解析了传播传播链的传播路径以及可能影响其传播和流行病学动态的外部因素。我们的分析显示,传播模式符合重力模型动态,病毒传播事件在人口密集地区更为频繁。此外,我们发现疫情的时间动态和强度与气候变量,即温度和降水量密切相关。研究结果还表明,理论上,由此次疫情和之前2005-2006年疫情所产生的群体免疫力,足以单独解释导致疫情结束的传播率下降。尽管不能排除短期内疫情的再次暴发,但病毒在人类群体中大规模传播的风险在接下来的季节中似乎相对有限。

这些发现可以为监测监测和预防策略提供信息,特别是在理解气候对基孔肯雅病毒传播的影响以及未来暴发疫情的潜在可能性方面。

Unraveling the epidemiological and dispersal dynamics of the 2024-2025 chikungunya virus epidemic on Réunion Island.

Authors: Etienne Frumence, Raphaëlle Klitting, Kyla Serres, Yu-Cai Shao, Filippo Monti, Muriel Vincent, Mandev S. Gill, Marc A. Suchard, Philippe Lemey, Xavier de Lamballerie
Original abstract (English)
Réunion Island experienced a massive chikungunya virus epidemic in 2024-2025, with >54,000 confirmed cases. This is the second major chikungunya epidemic on the island, following the first one that peaked 20 years ago. It has been asserted that this new outbreak finds its origin in a single introduction event into the island, offering an opportunity to exploit viral genomic data to understand the epidemiological and dispersal dynamics of the introduced transmission chain. We sequenced >3,000 viral genomes collected during the epidemic. Harnessing this genomic dataset, we used several phylogeographic and phylodynamic approaches to unravel the paths taken by the transmission chain and the external factors that might have impacted its dispersal and epidemiological dynamics on the island. Our analyses highlight a dispersal pattern in line with a gravity-model dynamic with viral transition events being more frequent from and toward more populated areas. Our analyses reveal that the transmission chain was overall spatially intermixed, with frequent exchanges among residential areas. In addition, we show that the temporal dynamic and intensity of the epidemic were associated with climatic variables, namely temperature and precipitation. Our results also show that in theory, the population immunity-resulting from this epidemic and the previous one (2005-2006)-could be sufficient to explain on its own the decrease in the transmission rate that led to the end of the epidemic. While a short-term resurgence cannot be excluded, the risk of a large-scale circulation of the virus in the human population appears therefore relatively limited in the upcoming seasons.

尼加拉瓜马那瓜地区基孔肯雅热和寨卡病毒疫情中感染和疾病的空间分析

Spatial analyses of infection and disease during chikungunya and Zika epidemics in Managua, Nicaragua
作者： Fausto Andres Bustos Carrillo, Brenda Lopez Mercado, Jairo Carey Monterrey, Damaris Collado, Tatiana Miranda, Saira Saborio, Sergio Ojeda, Nery Sanchez, Miguel Plazaola, Harold Suazo Laguna
摘要中文翻译摘要 基孔肯雅病毒(CHIKV)和寨卡病毒(ZIKV)在美洲引发了广泛的疫情。在数年几乎无疫情之后,CHIKV再次在尼加拉瓜传播。我们对尼加拉瓜进行了一项前瞻性儿科队列研究,分析了2014年、2015年两次基孔肯雅疫情和2016年一次寨卡疫情中约3,000名最初未感染的参与者感染和疾病状况。我们使用广义估计方程、广义加性混合效应模型、Kuldorff扫描统计量和聚类相关性来分析每个疫情期间的感染和疾病情况。我们发现,感染率(新感染人数/总人口)和疾病率(新病例数/总人口)的空间模式往往不同,表明高感染区域可能并不表现出高疾病率。高感染率出现在研究地点西边的一个大型墓地附近。2015年的大型基孔肯雅疫情和2016年的寨卡疫情均始于该墓地,感染率在整个研究地点均较高,并且在疫情的月度动态中表现出广泛的变化。感染过剩的聚类区域较大,且靠近墓地,而未感染参与者过剩的聚类区域则出现在研究区域的北部和东部。值得注意的是,感染在家庭中的聚类相关性非常弱(约0.2),但感染状态在小于200米的距离内存在相关性。疫情之间的相似性表明,将干预措施针对建筑环境而非家庭环境可能有助于降低马那瓜地区的疫情潜力。总体而言,仅使用病例数据进行疫情的空间分析可能会显著低估病毒传播的范围,错过高感染区域的识别以及干预机会。

Spatial analyses of infection and disease during chikungunya and Zika epidemics in Managua, Nicaragua

Authors: Fausto Andres Bustos Carrillo, Brenda Lopez Mercado, Jairo Carey Monterrey, Damaris Collado, Tatiana Miranda, Saira Saborio, Sergio Ojeda, Nery Sanchez, Miguel Plazaola, Harold Suazo Laguna
Original abstract (English)
ABSTRACT Chikungunya (CHIKV) and Zika (ZIKV) viruses have caused widespread epidemics across the Americas. After several years of minimal transmission, CHIKV is once again spreading in Nicaragua. We investigated spatial aspects of two chikungunya epidemics (2014, 2015) and one Zika epidemic (2016) in a prospective pediatric cohort in Nicaragua. We used generalized estimating equations, generalized additive and mixed-effects models, Kuldorff’s scan statistic, and intracluster correlations to analyze the infection and disease status of ∼3,000 initially uninfected participants during each epidemic. We found that the incidence of infection (# new infections/total population) and disease (# new cases/total population) often had different spatial patterns, demonstrating that high-infection areas may not exhibit much disease. High infection incidence was observed near a large cemetery west of the study site. The large 2015 chikungunya epidemic and 2016 Zika epidemic started near the cemetery, exhibited high infection incidence throughout the study site, and had extensive month-to-month changes in spatiotemporal dynamics. Clusters of excess infections were large and adjacent to the cemetery, while clusters of excess uninfected participants were found in the northern and eastern sections of the study area. Notably, the intracluster correlation of infection was very weak in households (∼0.2), and infection status was correlated across distances <200 meters. Similarities across the epidemics suggest that targeting interventions to the built, non-household environment may reduce epidemic potential in Managua. Overall, spatial analyses of epidemics that only use case data may substantially underestimate the full extent of viral transmission, missing identification of high-infection areas and opportunities for intervention.

问题与背景
本研究旨在分析2021年至2025年间巴西基孔肯雅热的流行病学特征,考虑其时间、地理和社会人口学分布。基孔肯雅热是巴西重要的公共卫生问题,其变化与基孔肯雅病毒的传播动态相关。

本研究分析了首次症状年份、通知地区、性别、年龄组、种族/肤色、教育水平和病例演变等变量。采用生态学方法,探讨基孔肯雅热病例在不同人口和社会地理因素中的分布模式。

解释与创新
研究结果突显了基孔肯雅热在巴西的时间和空间异质性,病例主要集中在女性、20至59岁人群以及肤色为棕褐色的人群中。本研究提供了基孔肯雅热在五年期间的流行病学趋势的详细概述。
科研与公卫意义
这些结果可以为针对基孔肯雅热高发地区和人群的公共卫生干预措施提供信息,并强调改进基孔肯雅病毒监测和控制措施的必要性。同时,也突显了在通知系统中准确记录数据的重要性。
局限与证据强度
本研究基于来自SINAN的次级数据,可能在完整性和准确性方面存在局限。由于样本量大且使用了成熟的基孔肯雅病毒监测系统,证据强度较强,但生态学设计限制了推断个体层面因果关系的能力。

Authors: Rafael Ferreira Daher, Júlia Borges De Sá Guimarães, Andressa Kelly Rodrigues Vieira Silva, Maria Eduarda Uchôa Cavalcanti Moreira Da Silva, Maria Clara Azzi Vaz de Campos, Eduardo Neres Silva
Original abstract (English)
Introdução: A febre de chikungunya constitui importante problema de saúde pública no Brasil, apresentando variações temporais e espaciais relacionadas à dinâmica de transmissão do vírus. Original abstract (English)

FL health department proposes allowing pharmacists to administer chikungunya vaccine - NBC 6 South Florida

News Brief
The Florida Department of Health is proposing a rule that would allow pharmacists to administer the chikungunya vaccine, aiming to improve accessibility. According to the DOH's reportable diseases frequency report, there have been 189 reported cases of chikungunya disease in Florida this year, with the majority, 120 cases, concentrated in Miami-Dade County. The proposal comes as part of efforts to expand vaccination services, particularly in areas with high incidence rates. While the exact number of cases and their distribution are reported, the proposal itself is still under review and has not been implemented. The decision to allow pharmacists to administer the vaccine is based on the need to address the growing public health concern associated with chikungunya, which is a mosquito-borne illness that can cause fever and severe joint pain. The disease is often confused with dengue and Zika, making diagnosis challenging. This initiative reflects a proactive approach to managing the spread of chikungunya and ensuring timely immunization for at-risk populations.
