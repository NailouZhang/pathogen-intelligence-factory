Emetine dihydrochloride inhibits Chikungunya virus nsP2 helicase and shows robust antiviral activity in cells and mice
作者： Anshula Sharma, Chandru Subramani, K. A. Shouri, Ghanshyam Sharma, Brohmomoy Basu, Abhay Deep Pandey, Archana Rout, Devendra Sharma, Deepti Jain, Sudhanshu Vrati
摘要中文翻译摘要 背景 基孔肯雅病毒(CHIKV)是一种蚊媒甲病毒,引起基孔肯雅热在全球频繁流行,其特征是严重的关节疼痛和衰弱性关节炎。由于没有特定的抗病毒疗法,这些疫情对公共卫生构成了重大挑战,特别是在热带地区。因此,迫切需要开发新型抗病毒药物。药物再利用是识别针对 CHIKV 复制的潜在抗病毒药物的一种有吸引力的策略。方法使用高内涵细胞成像筛选已批准药物的谱库,以鉴定潜在的 CHIKV 复制抑制剂。这些 CHIKV 抑制剂的功效在基孔肯雅病 C57BL/6 小鼠模型中进行了评估,监测病毒血症和关节肿胀等临床症状。在 ERMS 细胞中研究了 CHIKV 抑制剂对病毒摄取、复制、RNA 合成和蛋白质产生的影响。使用计算机分子对接来研究抑制剂与 CHIKV 蛋白的结合。使用微尺度热泳和等温滴定量热法在体外验证结合。在存在二盐酸依米丁 (ED) 的情况下测量 CHIKV 解旋酶、蛋白酶和 ATP 酶活性,以确定其作用机制。结果 在通过高内涵筛选鉴定出的四种 CHIKV 抑制剂中,ED 可有效抑制小鼠模型中的 CHIKV 复制,从而显着降低病毒血症水平。值得注意的是,接受 ED 治疗的 CHIKV 感染小鼠没有表现出关节肿胀的临床症状。在 ERMS 细胞中,ED 通过抑制病毒 RNA 合成来阻断 CHIKV 的摄取和早期复制,从而阻止病毒蛋白的产生。计算模型预测 ED 与 CHIKV nsP2 解旋酶结构域的 RNA 结合位点周围的结合。体外验证证实了 ED 与 CHIKV nsP2 的剂量依赖性结合。ED 以浓度依赖性方式特异性抑制解旋酶解旋,而不影响 ATP 酶或蛋白酶活性。结论 本研究表明,ED 通过与 CHIKV nsP2 结合并抑制其解旋酶活性,抑制培养细胞和小鼠感染模型中的 CHIKV 复制。虽然这是 ED 抗病毒作用的一种有前途的病毒靶向机制,但其他宿主导向机制值得进一步研究。

Emetine dihydrochloride inhibits Chikungunya virus nsP2 helicase and shows robust antiviral activity in cells and mice

Authors: Anshula Sharma, Chandru Subramani, K. A. Shouri, Ghanshyam Sharma, Brohmomoy Basu, Abhay Deep Pandey, Archana Rout, Devendra Sharma, Deepti Jain, Sudhanshu Vrati
Original Abstract
Abstract Background Chikungunya virus (CHIKV), a mosquito-borne alphavirus, causes frequent global epidemics of chikungunya fever, characterized by severe joint pain and debilitating arthritis. With no specific antiviral therapies available, these outbreaks pose a major public health challenge, particularly in tropical regions. There is thus urgent need to develop novel antivirals. Drug repurposing is an attractive strategy to identify potential antivirals targeting CHIKV replication. Methods The Spectrum collection of approved drugs was screened using high-content cell imaging to identify potential CHIKV replication inhibitors. Efficacy of these CHIKV inhibitors was evaluated in a C57BL/6 mouse model of chikungunya disease, monitoring viremia and clinical symptoms like joint swelling. The CHIKV inhibitor's effect on virus uptake, replication, RNA synthesis, and protein production was studied in ERMS cells. In silico molecular docking was used to study the inhibitor’s binding to the CHIKV proteins. Binding was validated in vitro using microscale thermophoresis and isothermal titration calorimetry. CHIKV helicase, protease, and ATPase activities were measured in the presence of emetine dihydrochloride (ED) to determine its mechanism of action. Results Out of the four CHIKV inhibitors identified by high content screening, ED potently inhibited CHIKV replication in the mouse model, yielding significantly lower viremia levels. Notably, CHIKV-infected mice treated with ED showed no clinical symptoms of joint swelling. In ERMS cells, ED blocked CHIKV uptake and early replication by suppressing viral RNA synthesis, which in turn prevented viral protein production. Computational modelling predicted ED’s binding around the RNA-binding site of the CHIKV nsP2 helicase domain. In vitro validation confirmed dose-dependent ED binding with CHIKV nsP2. ED specifically inhibited helicase unwinding in a concentration-dependent manner without affecting ATPase or protease activity. Conclusions This study demonstrates that ED inhibits CHIKV replication in cultured cells and a mouse model of infection through binding to CHIKV nsP2 and inhibition of its helicase activity. While this is a promising virus-targeted mechanism of ED’s antiviral action, additional host-directed mechanisms warrant further investigation.

Clinical Characteristics of Acute Chikungunya Cases at IAHS Hospital

Authors: Zahedul Hoque, Md Minhaz Uddin, Sadia Ridita Shahid, Syed jabed Md, Noor Zahed Uddin, Satyajit Roy
Original Abstract
Background: Between April and September 2017, Bangladesh experienced a major outbreak of acute Chikungunya Virus (CHIKV) infection. Since then, Chikungunya has become a common cause of acute febrile illness associated with joint pain in the country. During outbreaks, dengue infection frequently co-circulates and presents with overlapping clinical features, creating diagnostic challenges. To describe the clinical and laboratory characteristics of acute Chikungunya virus infection among patients attending IAHS Hospital. Materials and methods: This cross-sectional descriptive study was conducted in the Outpatient Department of Medicine at IAHS Hospital from March 2025 to August 2025 on 50 patients. Cases presenting with acute febrile illness and joint symptoms were evaluated clinically and investigated serologically. Anti-Chikungunya IgM antibody testing was performed for laboratory confirmation. Results: Fever and joint swelling were present in 100% of patients. Rash and generalized weakness were observed in 80% and 90% of cases, respectively. Male patients constituted 60% of the study population. Lymphadenopathy was noted in 40% of patients. An oligoarticular pattern of joint involvement (60%) was more common than a polyarticular pattern (40%). Large joint involvement predominated, with ankle joint involvement seen in 80% of cases. All patients were positive for anti-Chikungunya IgM antibodies. Dengue–Chikungunya co-infection was identified in four patients. Conclusion: Chikungunya virus infection is an important cause of acute febrile illness with joint pain in Bangladesh. Given its clinical overlap with dengue and other febrile illnesses, accurate laboratory confirmation is essential for appropriate diagnosis and management. 基孔肯雅病毒感染后慢性关节痛的预测因素

Predictors of chronic arthralgia following chikungunya virus infection
作者： Hemali Jha, Mohammad Tauseef Khan, Nida Khan, Shilpi Gupta
摘要中文翻译目标:基孔肯雅热是一种以发烧和关节痛为特征的病毒性疾病。虽然通常是自限性的,但高达 43% 的患者可能会出现慢性关节痛。本研究的目的是评估基孔肯雅热感染后长期关节疼痛(> 90天)的频率和预测因素,来自印度北部的数据有限。方法:2023 年 9 月至 2023 年 12 月在北印度一家三级护理医院对实验室确诊的基孔肯雅热成人患者进行了一项前瞻性观察性研究。共有 56 名患者接受了长达 4 个月的随访,以评估关节疼痛的持续时间和严重程度。数据是通过初始检查和随访期间的临床检查和访谈收集的。结果:所有患者均出现关节疼痛;23% 的人表示关节疼痛持续超过 90 天。踝关节是最常受影响的关节(35.7%),其次是膝关节(25%)和掌指关节(21.4%)。慢性关节痛患者与无慢性关节痛患者之间的比较相关性显示,与疾病发作时出现皮疹、中度至重度关节疼痛和肿胀显着相关(p 值<0.05)。结论:在本研究中,发现发病时中度至重度关节疼痛、关节肿胀和皮疹是长期关节痛的预测因素。

Published in Targetome on 30 April 2026, the assay uses a multisite bridging-mediated strategy and gold@platinum nanoparticle colorimetric enhancement to achieve a visual detection limit as low as 2 pmol·L−1 for Chikungunya virus (CHIKV). In 36 suspected CHIKV mouse serum samples, the method matched RT-PCR results with 100% accuracy. The platform is designed for rapid field testing and outbreak surveillance, particularly in resource-limited settings where conventional laboratory equipment is unavailable. While the technology shows promise for detecting multiple mosquito-borne viruses, including Dengue, Zika, and Yellow fever, further clinical validation and regulatory processes are required before deployment.
