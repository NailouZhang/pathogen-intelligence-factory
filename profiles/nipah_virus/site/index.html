尼帕病毒 附着和融合糖蛋白在进入过程中发挥的独特作用的生物物理学阐明。

Biophysical Elucidation of the Distinct Roles the Nipah Virus Attachment and Fusion Glycoproteins Play in the Entry Process.
作者： Sreetama Pal, Hector C Aguilar, Susan Daniel
摘要中文翻译尼帕病毒 (NiV) 是世界卫生组织优先病原体,是副粘病毒科中的一种致命的有包膜 RNA 病毒。NiV附着G和融合F糖蛋白分别在病毒进入和合胞体形成过程中执行病毒-细胞或细胞-细胞膜融合。尽管有过去的研究,但这一过程背后的分子水平编排的几个方面,例如 F、G 及其相互作用如何调节融合,仍然知之甚少,并被复杂细胞环境中解耦融合决定因素的挑战所掩盖。在这里,我们报告了一种基于重构的方法,用于模拟 NiV-宿主 界面,采用带有 NiV M、F 和/或 G 蛋白的病毒样颗粒以及源自 NiV 允许的 宿主 细胞的质膜囊泡。我们发现这个最小的系统可以准确地重现 NiV-宿主 细胞融合,并使用该平台使用野生型或突变型 F 和 G 蛋白在不同的中间步骤影响融合,对 NiV 融合进行生物物理解剖。我们发现 F 和 G 以不同的方式影响 NiV 融合的半融合和孔隙形成阶段,揭示了严格调控的融合景观。我们的方法还提供了第一个直接测量 NiV 融合温度阈值的方法,为病毒融合性的能量需求提供了新的见解。这些发现证明了这种方法在剖析 NiV 融合方面的能力,这是通过基于细胞的测定无法实现的。因此,这项研究可以作为具有多组分融合机制和 宿主 蛋白受体的其他病毒的综合融合分析的蓝图,并且可以重新用于针对其融合机制的治疗筛选平台,这对于开发新型 抗病毒药物 是必需的。

Biophysical Elucidation of the Distinct Roles the Nipah Virus Attachment and Fusion Glycoproteins Play in the Entry Process.

Authors: Sreetama Pal, Hector C Aguilar, Susan Daniel
Original abstract (English)
Nipah virus (NiV), a World Health Organization priority pathogen, is a lethal enveloped RNA virus within the Paramyxoviridae family. NiV attachment G and fusion F glycoproteins execute viral-cell or cell-cell membrane fusion during viral entry and syncytia formation, respectively. Despite past studies, several aspects of the molecular-level orchestration underlying this process, such as how F, G, and their interactions regulate fusion, remain poorly understood, obscured by challenges in decoupling fusion determinants in complex cellular environments. Here, we report a reconstitution-based approach for mimicking the NiV-host interface, employing virus-like particles with NiV M, F, and/or G proteins and plasma membrane vesicles derived from NiV-permissive host cells. We found this minimal system can accurately recapitulate NiV-host cell fusion, and used this platform for biophysical dissection of NiV fusion using wild-type or mutant F and G proteins affecting fusion at distinct intermediate steps. We show that F and G affect hemifusion and pore formation stages of NiV fusion in distinct ways, revealing a tightly regulated fusion landscape. Our approach also delivers one of the first direct measurements of temperature thresholds for NiV fusion, providing novel insights into energetic requirements underlying viral fusogenicity. These findings demonstrate the power of this approach in dissecting aspects of NiV fusion not accessible via cell-based assays. Thus, this study serves as a blueprint for comprehensive fusion analysis of other viruses with multicomponent fusion machinery and host protein receptors, and could be repurposed into therapeutic screening platforms targeting their fusion mechanisms, necessary for developing novel antivirals.

人与人之间的复发和延迟引起的动力学 尼帕病毒 传播:数学建模研究

Relapse and delay-induced dynamics in human-to-human Nipah virus transmission: a mathematical modeling study
作者： Bugalia, S., Wang, H., Tyagi, S., Tripathi, J. P., Salvador, L.
摘要中文翻译尼帕病毒 (NiV) 表现出复杂的 传播 动态,由感染到 传播 的延迟、复发和恢复后延迟发生的神经系统并发症形成。这些时间过程的流行病学后果,特别是它们如何共同塑造 NiV 动态,在现有模型中仍然缺乏描述。我们开发了人与人 NiV 传播 的延迟微分方程模型,其中包含有效的群体水平感染到 传播 延迟、立即复发和延迟 脑炎 复发。我们推导出人与人之间的 传播 阈值 \(R_h\),并在 \(R_h > 1\) 时建立独特的生物学上可行的地方性平衡。无病平衡的局部稳定性由全线性化延迟系统控制;只要\(R_h > 1\),无病平衡就不稳定,并且模型在\(R_h = 1\)处表现出前向跨临界分岔。延迟可能会破坏地方性平衡的稳定性,并在假设参数范围内通过 Hopf 分岔引起持续振荡。对孟加拉国 NiV 发病率 数据(2001-2024 年)的校准产生 $R_h\approx0.81$,尽管不确定性承认统一双方的值,并且到 2030 年的预测表明,由复发和延迟机制驱动的稍后复苏。复发贡献分数表明,随着康复人群的积累,复发对感染室补充的贡献越来越大。敏感性分析确定 传播 和立即复发是 \(R_h\) 的主要决定因素:即刻复发率增加 30\% 会降低跨越 \(R_h = 1\) 所需的 传播 率增加从 24\% 到 15\%。立即复发对高峰期后的负担影响最大,而感染到 传播 的延迟则控制着流行的时间和程度。因此,即使在 \(R_h < 1\) 时,延迟和复发介导的反馈也会产生瞬态波,这强调了监测康复个体的重要性。

Relapse and delay-induced dynamics in human-to-human Nipah virus transmission: a mathematical modeling study

Authors: Bugalia, S., Wang, H., Tyagi, S., Tripathi, J. P., Salvador, L.
Original abstract (English)
Nipah virus (NiV) exhibits complex transmission dynamics shaped by infection-to-transmission delays, relapse, and delayed-onset neurological complications following recovery. The epidemiological consequences of these temporal processes, particularly how they jointly shape NiV dynamics, remain poorly characterized in existing models. We develop a delay differential equation model for human-to-human NiV transmission incorporating an effective population-level infection-to-transmission delay, immediate relapse, and delayed encephalitis relapse. We derive the human-to-human transmission threshold \(R_h\) and establish a unique biologically feasible endemic equilibrium when \(R_h > 1\). Local stability of the disease-free equilibrium is governed by the full linearized delay system; the disease-free equilibrium is unstable whenever \(R_h > 1\), and the model exhibits a forward transcritical bifurcation at \(R_h = 1\). Delays can destabilize the endemic equilibrium and induce sustained oscillations through Hopf bifurcation under hypothetical parameter regimes. Calibration to Bangladesh NiV incidence data (2001-2024) yields $R_h\approx0.81$, though uncertainty admits values on both sides of unity, and projections through 2030 suggest later resurgence driven by relapse and delay mechanisms. A relapse contribution fraction shows that relapse increasingly contributes to infectious-compartment replenishment as the recovered population accumulates. Sensitivity analyses identify transmission and immediate relapse as dominant determinants of \(R_h\): a 30\% increase in immediate relapse rate lowers the transmission-rate increase required to cross \(R_h = 1\) from 24\% to 15\%. Immediate relapse most strongly shapes post-peak burden, while the infection-to-transmission delay controls epidemic timing and magnitude. Delays and relapse-mediated feedback can therefore generate transient waves even when \(R_h < 1\), underscoring the importance of monitoring recovered individuals.

尼帕病毒 感染:临床谱、发病机制 和新兴预防和治疗策略的叙述性回顾

Nipah Virus Infection: A Narrative Review of Clinical Spectrum, Pathogenesis, and Emerging Preventive and Therapeutic Strategies
作者： Pankaj C Jambholkar, Swarupa V Chakole, Bhagyesh Sapkale
摘要中文翻译尼帕病毒 (NiV) 已知是副粘病毒科的一种新出现的人畜共患病原体,于 1998 年首次发现,并且由于严重的 脑炎 和呼吸道疾病,也与高发病率和 死亡率 相关。传播通过直接接触受感染的动物、受污染的食物以及人与人之间的传播发生,从而进一步凸显了其流行潜力。尽管在了解其病毒学、发病机制 以及临床表现方面取得了重大进展,但 NiV 暴发 仍然对公共卫生构成重大挑战,通常是在流行地区。目前 NiV 的管理在很大程度上仍然是支持性的,没有获得许可的 抗病毒药物 或 疫苗,因此强调需要采取有效的医疗对策。最近的治疗发展必须集中在单克隆抗体和 抗病毒药物 药物上,包括瑞德西韦,它们在临床前研究中显示出有希望的疗效。疫苗 候选药物,包括重组蛋白、病毒 媒介 和基于 mRNA 的平台,正在临床评估的早期阶段进行检查,显示出强大的免疫保护潜力。未来的研究重点必须包括开发快速护理点 诊断、加强 基因组监测,同时实施整合人类、动物和环境数据的“同一个健康”方法,以预防 跨物种溢出 事件。完善动物模型和阐明保护的免疫相关性是将临床前成功转化为获得充分许可的 NiV 管理方法干预措施的非常重要的方面。国际协作框架以及结构化研发路线图是加速 疫苗 和治疗开发的关键选择,从而进一步确保 暴发 期间的快速部署。这篇叙述性综述文章重点介绍了 NiV 的 流行病学、发病机制、临床管理以及新兴预防治疗策略的当前知识,同时还确定了关键的研究差距。它强调需要采取积极主动的综合方法,将医疗、公共卫生和科学努力结合起来,以减轻全球新病毒的威胁。
