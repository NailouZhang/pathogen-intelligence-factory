Serological and transcriptomic response of African fruit bats to immune challenge with recombinant vaccines expressing Ebola or Nipah virus antigens
作者： Elinor Jax, Romain Garnier, Georgia Tsagkogeorga, Hubert Buczkowski, Louise Gibson, Pius Benia, Richard Suu-Ire, Vera M. Warmuth, Sylvester Languon, Osbourne Quaye

Serological and transcriptomic response of African fruit bats to immune challenge with recombinant vaccines expressing Ebola or Nipah virus antigens

Authors: Elinor Jax, Romain Garnier, Georgia Tsagkogeorga, Hubert Buczkowski, Louise Gibson, Pius Benia, Richard Suu-Ire, Vera M. Warmuth, Sylvester Languon, Osbourne Quaye
Original abstract (English)
Abstract Bats are hosts to diverse zoonotic viruses which can cause high case fatality rates in humans. Since detecting viruses in bats is difficult, antibodies are often used for surveillance, though their significance remains unclear in the absence of in vivo immunological studies. To address this knowledge gap, we measured antibody production and gene expression in bats following injection with vaccines encoding either a Nipah virus G glycoprotein or an Ebola virus GP glycoprotein. We selected two African bat species with overlapping ranges, Eidolon helvum (confirmed reservoir of henipaviruses) and Epomophorus gambianus (putative reservoir of filoviruses). Using a Luminex-based multiplex microsphere immunoassay and a pseudotype virus neutralization assay, we observed an increase in antibodies against the Nipah glycoprotein in most bats that received the Nipah vaccine. In contrast, we could only detect anti-Ebola glycoprotein antibodies in one bat species, Ep. gambianus, in response to the Ebola vaccine. Transcriptomic analysis in Ei. helvum revealed that genes involved in ISGylation and T-cell differentiation pathways were differentially expressed in response to the Ebola, but not the Nipah, vaccine. These results demonstrate species-specific differences in responses to antigen exposure, offering insights into bat adaptive immune systems and providing calibration for serological data from wild bats.

CRISPR/Cas13a-Based Nucleic Acid-Functionalized Discrete Floating-Gate Field-Effect Transistor for Amplification-Free Rapid Nipah Virus Detection

Authors: Zi-Jin Tong, Zhen Huang, Jia Liu, Jun-Hua Su, Qi-Long Zhao, Ren-Long Zhu, Ren-Jie Xiong, Wen-Ke Xie, Rui Xiao
Original abstract (English)
Abstract Nipah virus (NiV) is a highly lethal zoonotic pathogen, and existing rapid diagnostic tools are scarce and reliant on nucleic acid amplification. In this study, we designed and fabricated a thin-film field-effect transistor with an IGZO channel and, for the first time, combined a discrete AuNP floating gate with the CRISPR/Cas13a system to achieve amplification-free detection of NiV. A pair of partially complementary nucleic acid reporter probes was used to attach AuNPs onto the channel, forming a nucleic acid-functionalized discrete floating gate. Under positive gate voltage, the AuNPs electrostatically induce mirror charges in the IGZO channel, amplifying the sensing signal. Additionally, the electrons can be injected from AuNPs into the channel via the Fowler–Nordheim tunneling effect under negative gate voltage, as a potential complementary sensing mechanism. The AuNPs are immobilized via nucleic acid strands, ensuring good biocompatibility. CrRNA was designed and the CRISPR system was optimized for compatibility with the FET, achieving amplification-free detection of NiV standards down to 10° copies/μL within 10 min. This study not only provides a reliable and rapid detection tool for NiV but also establishes a biosensing framework applicable to other pathogens.

Epitope and immunological basis for superior immunogenicity of henipavirus glycoprotein HeV.G over NiV.G in mRNA vaccines

Authors: Qizan Gong, Xinghai Zhang, Weiqi Wang, Yanfeng Yao, Shaohong Chen, Fangxu Li, Jiachen Zhang, Xiaoping Guo, Zhihao Li, Zunhao Zhou
Original abstract (English)
Nipah (NiV) and Hendra (HeV) viruses are highly lethal zoonotic Henipaviruses with pandemic potential, for which no licensed vaccines exist. The surface glycoprotein (G) is the primary target of neutralizing antibodies, yet a systematic comparison of the immunogenicity of NiV.G and HeV.G has been lacking. Here, we engineered mRNA vaccines encoding the full-length G proteins of NiV and HeV. In Syrian hamsters, while both vaccines conferred homologous protection, only HeV.G vaccination at a lower dose (5 μg) provided complete cross-protection against lethal NiV challenge. Comparative immune profiling revealed that HeV.G vaccination induced significantly higher neutralizing antibody titers, which correlated with robust germinal center B cell expansion and a stronger CD4 + T follicular helper (Tfh) cell response, despite equivalent B cell activation. We further developed a bivalent vaccine Gmix, which induced balanced antibody and cellular immune responses against both G proteins, thereby confirming the value of multivalent vaccines in broadening immune coverage. Collectively, this study defines HeV.G as the preferred antigen for pan-henipavirus vaccine development and offers a framework for rational antigen selection against future viral threats.

12 October 2026 • Big Picture Nipah virus explained: How it spreads and how Bangladesh can stay safe Published : 29 January 2026 • Updated : 29 January 2026, 16:28 • HEALTH & FITNESS SHARE A Ayman Anika Ayman Anika Photo: Collected / gabby k / Pexels For many of us, the word “Nipah” is not abstract. It carries memories of quarantined villages, restricted movement, and anxious nights spent waiting for test results. With new Nipah cases reported in neighbouring India, and airports across Asia stepping up screening, the concern feels immediate — not distant. Nipah virus is not new to Bangladesh. Since the first recorded outbreak in 2001, the country has seen repeated cases almost every year, particularly in winter. Unlike many infectious diseases that arrive from elsewhere, Nipah is already part of our ecological reality. Tap here to add The Daily Star as a trusted source How Nipah spreads Fruit bats are the natural carriers of the Nipah virus. In Bangladesh, transmission to humans most commonly occurs through raw date palm sap contaminated by bat saliva or urine. The practice of drinking fresh, unprocessed sap remains one of the biggest risk factors. The virus can also spread by eating fruits partially eaten or contaminated by bats, through close contact with infected animals, and from human to human via bodily fluids such as saliva, respiratory droplets, blood, or urine. Several outbreaks in Bangladesh have shown clear evidence of person-to-person transmission, especially among family members and caregivers. This makes Nipah particularly dangerous in household and hospital settings where close contact is unavoidable. Symptoms that should not be ignored Nipah infection does not always start dramatically. Early symptoms often include fever, headache, muscle pain, sore throat, and fatigue — signs that can easily be mistaken for flu or viral fever. In more severe cases, the virus attacks the brain, leading to encephalitis. Symptoms may escalate quickly to dizziness, confusion, seizures, difficulty breathing, and loss of consciousness. The disease can progress within days. Fatality rates reported in South Asia range between 40 and 75 per cent. Survivors may suffer long-term neurological complications, including memory loss, personality changes, and difficulty concentrating. There is no specific antiviral treatment and no approved vaccine. Medical care focuses on supportive treatment, which makes early detection critical. Why this matters right now Recent cases in India, particularly in West Bengal, are geographically close to Bangladesh. While Nipah does not spread easily through casual contact or air travel, authorities have increased airport screening as a precaution. These steps are meant to detect symptoms early, not to signal widespread danger. For Bangladesh, the bigger challenge lies at home. Rural awareness remains uneven, and seasonal behaviours — especially consuming raw date palm sap — continue despite years of warnings. What prevention actually looks like Preventing Nipah does not require drastic lifestyle changes, but it does require consistency. Avoiding raw or fermented date palm sap is one of the most effective measures. Fruits should be washed thoroughly and not eaten if visibly bitten or damaged. People should avoid close contact with sick animals and seek medical attention if they develop a fever after possible exposure. Caregivers should use protective measures when looking after suspected patients, especially in home settings. Nipah is not a virus that spreads silently across cities. It spreads through habits, proximity, and delayed response. For us, vigilance is not about panic. It is about recognising a familiar threat, understanding how it moves, and choosing prevention over regret. For all latest news, follow The Daily Star's Google News channel. virus,NiV)被世界卫生组织列为优先病原体,其病死率高达40%–75%,宿主范围广,且缺乏特异性治疗药物。2026年初,印度和孟加拉国再次暴发NiV疫情,并出现医护人员感染和死亡案例,凸显其作为“国际关注的突发公共卫生事件”的现实威胁。NiV分为马来西亚株(NiVM)和孟加拉株(NiVB),后者在人群中的病死率更高,且已证实存在人际传播。目前尚无获批用于人类的NiV疫苗或药物。病毒表面的受体结合蛋白(receptor-binding protein,RBP)和融合蛋白(fusion protein,F)是中和抗体的主要靶点,但针对单一抗原的抗体长期使用易诱发病毒逃逸突变,导致耐药株出现。既往的抗体组合或双特异性抗体策略多仅靶向RBP或F蛋白,且已有双特异性抗体DS90-m102.4在仓鼠模型中未能表现出超越单抗的保护效果,提示该策略仍有优化空间。 **研究内容与意义:** 研究人员通过筛选C型纳米抗体(C-Nab)噬菌体展示文库,获得靶向RBP头部结构域的亲本克隆G-0,并经三轮亲和力成熟得到高亲和力克隆G-3-1。将G-3-1与血清白蛋白靶向纳米抗体NbAlb1融合构建半衰期延长型抗体G-3-1-HLE,在仓鼠模型中证实其可提供50%的治疗性保护。进一步通过连接子类型、长度和串联方向的系统优化,研究人员构建了同时靶向F蛋白和RBP的双特异性纳米抗体2A1-3-8-(AP)12-G-3-1,该抗体在体外表现出增强且更为平衡的中和活性,并在小鼠NiV假病毒感染模型中展现出良好的预防保护效果。该研究不仅提供了应对NiV疫情的下一代抗体候选分子,也为双抗原靶向双特异性抗体的设计提供了重要策略。 **关键技术方法:** 研究人员使用来自中国典型培养物保藏中心的Vero、Vero E6、293T细胞,以及HEK293F细胞;NiVM、NiVB和亨德拉病毒(HeV)毒株来自中国科学院武汉病毒研究所国家病毒资源库。主要方法包括:基于噬菌体展示文库的生物淘选与亲和力成熟(error-prone PCR引入随机突变)、酶联免疫吸附试验(ELISA)和生物层干涉法(BLI)测定结合活性、基于VSV载体的假病毒中和试验、BSL-4设施内的真病毒空斑减少中和试验、冷冻电镜(cryo-EM)和AlphaFold 3结构预测、竞争ELISA和丙氨酸扫描突变进行表位鉴定、叙利亚金仓鼠药代动力学及攻毒治疗研究、BALB/c小鼠假病毒感染模型及抗体预防实验。 **研究结果:** **一、靶向RBP的C-Nab的鉴定。** 研究人员以NiVM-RBP头部结构域为抗原筛选C-Nab噬菌体展示文库,获得亲本克隆G-0。通过连续三轮针对NiVM-RBP、NiVB-RBP和HeV-RBP的亲和力成熟,筛选出优势克隆G-3-1。与G-0相比,G-3-1与HNV-RBP的结合活性显著增强,其中对HeV-RBP的结合提高超过500倍。BLI测定其与NiVM-RBP、NiVB-RBP和HeV-RBP的平衡解离常数(KD)分别为0.022 nM、0.250 nM和0.152 nM。在假病毒中和试验中,G-3-1对NiVM和HeV的中和活性较G-0分别提高189倍和650倍;对真实NiVM和NiVB的半数抑制浓度(IC50)分别为2.16 nM和3.58 nM。 **二、半衰期延长的G-3-1-HLE有效保护仓鼠抵抗NiV感染。** 由于纳米抗体分子量小(约15 kDa),体内清除快,研究人员将血清白蛋白靶向纳米抗体NbAlb1融合至G-3-1的C端,构建半衰期延长型抗体G-3-1-HLE。药代动力学分析显示,G-3-1-HLE在仓鼠体内的半衰期(t1/2)为36小时,较G-3-1(1.3小时)延长27.7倍,且生物利用度、清除率、平均驻留时间等参数均获改善。在1000 LD50 NiVM腹腔攻毒模型中,感染后2小时、1天和3天给予20 mg/kg G-3-1-HLE,可提供50%的治疗性保护,存活仓鼠体重呈持续上升趋势。 **三、G-3-1靶向RBP头部结构域上的受体结合域。** 研究人员通过cryo-EM获得NiVM-RBP头部/G-3-1复合物约10 ?分辨率的低分辨率三维结构,显示G-3-1以近似垂直于RBP表面的方向结合。AlphaFold 3预测的复合物模型与三维结构相符,预测埋藏面积为1315.8 ?2,Loop 3贡献最大。竞争ELISA证明G-3-1与Ephrin B2和m102.4(靶向RBD的抗体)完全竞争结合RBP,而与靶向非RBD表位的HENV-32、nAH1.3和hAH1.3无竞争。丙氨酸扫描突变实验表明,NiVM-RBP中F458和G506残基对其结合敏感,HeV-RBP中敏感残基更多,该差异可能与其NiV偏向的中和活性有关。综合结果表明G-3-1的表位位于RBP头部的受体结合域(RBD),其机制是阻断RBP与Ephrin B2受体的结合。 **四、RBP和F靶向C-Nab的串联融合增强中和广度和效力。** 研究人员利用刚性(AP)12连接子将靶向RBP的G-3-1与靶向F蛋白的C-Nab 2A1-3-8串联,构建双特异性纳米抗体2A1-3-8-(AP)12-G-3-1。在连接子优化过程中,刚性连接子(EAAAK)n和(AP)n优于柔性连接子(G4S)n,且2A1-3-8位于N端、G-3-1位于C端的长刚性连接子(AP)12效果最佳。与单克隆抗体2A1-3-8和G-3-1相比,2A1-3-8-(AP)12-G-3-1对NiVM、NiVB和HeV的中和活性表现出更高效力和更均衡的特征,其对NiVM和NiVB的活性较2A1-3-8分别提高1.3倍和6.0倍,较G-3-1分别提高11.5倍、18.7倍和1015.8倍。 **五、2A1-3-8-(AP)12-G-3-1有效预防小鼠NiV假病毒感染。** 研究人员建立了一种基于生物发光成像的BALB/c小鼠NiV假病毒感染模型,确定假病毒主要靶向脾脏,肺部和肝脏有较弱信号。预防实验中,在假病毒攻击前30分钟腹腔注射25 mg/kg抗体,结果显示所有抗体处理组(2A1-3-8、G-3-1、2A1-3-8-(AP)12-G-3-1和m102.4)均显著降低了脾脏、肺和肝脏中的病毒载量。2A1-3-8-(AP)12-G-3-1展现出色的预防活性,但在本实验剂量下未表现出显著优于单个C-Nab的保护效力。 **讨论与结论:** 讨论部分指出,纳米抗体具有组织穿透力强(如穿越血脑屏障)、生产成本低、可识别隐蔽表位等优势,但体内清除快是其主要限制。G-3-1-HLE仅提供50%保护,可能与其本身中和活性不够强或半衰期仍相对有限有关。RBD是NiV RBP上的免疫优势表位,针对RBD的抗体最为丰富且高效。鉴于单靶点抗体的逃逸风险,同时靶向RBP和F蛋白的双特异性抗体策略具有重要意义。连接子的选择对双特异性抗体功能至关重要,本研究通过系统优化确定了最佳连接子。双特异性抗体可能通过同时结合同一病毒颗粒上的两种包膜蛋白,或交联不同病毒颗粒发挥中和作用,但具体机制仍需进一步验证。研究局限性包括:未在真实病毒模型中进行剂量滴定和预防/治疗效果的全面评估、未对双特异性抗体抗逃逸突变能力进行直接验证。结论部分指出,该双特异性纳米抗体2A1-3-8-(AP)12-G-3-1不仅具有强效且平衡的NiV和HeV中和活性,还在小鼠模型中提供了有效的假病毒预防保护,为应对未来NiV暴发提供了有前景的候选分子,并为下一代双抗原靶向双特异性抗体的设计提供了宝贵策略。
