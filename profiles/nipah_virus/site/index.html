灭活病毒培养物中 尼帕病毒 核衣壳抗原的快速检测。

A Rapid Detection of Nipah Virus Nucleocapsid Antigen in Inactivated Viral Cultures.
作者： Gyeongmu Choi, Jung Kim, Hansaem Lee, Pyeonghwa Jeon, Jae Hun Jeong, Yujin Jung, Jin Seo Park, Mi Kyoung Lee, Kyung-Chang Kim, Jong-Hwan Lee
摘要中文翻译尼帕病毒 (NiV) 是一种高度致命的人畜共患 亨尼帕病毒,可导致严重的呼吸道疾病和 脑炎,并且仍然是 暴发 防范的优先病原体。及时的 诊断 对于遏制至关重要,但在容易出现 暴发 且资源有限的环境中,基于核酸的检测通常不切实际。在这里,我们报告了基于纤维素纳米珠(CNB)增强的侧流免疫分析(LFIA)针对病毒核衣壳蛋白(NP)的NiV快速抗原测试的开发。通过迭代生物淘选从人类合成噬菌体展示文库(scFv 和 Fab)中分离出抗 NiV NP 抗体,将其重新格式化为全长 IgG,并使用 ELISA 和免疫荧光测定进行筛选。将四种先导抗体推进到夹心配对矩阵中,该矩阵确定了优化的捕获-检测配置(mAb #5 作为捕获;mAb #2 作为检测)。生物层干涉测量法证实了所选抗体的纳摩尔结合亲和力(mAb #5 的 KD = 39.50 nM;mAb #2 的 KD = 26.36 nM)。最终的 CNB-LFIA 在 15 分钟内产生结果,重组 NiV NP 的分析检测限达到 1 ng/mL。该检测未观察到与 SARS-CoV-2、SARS-CoV、MERS-CoV 或甲型流感病毒的交叉反应性,而仅在高抗原浓度 (2 μg/mL) 下观察到与麻疹病毒 NP 的弱交叉反应性。使用通过 RT-ddPCR 定量的 γ 辐射培养 NiV,LFIA 通过便携式阅读器检测到病毒载量降至 4.1 × 10 7 拷贝/mL(4.1 × 106 拷贝/反应),通过肉眼检测到 8.3 × 10 7 拷贝/mL(8.3 × 106 拷贝/反应)。总的来说,该 CNB-LFIA 提供快速、无需设备的 NiV 检测,支持其在资源有限的环境中对护理点 诊断 的潜在实用性。

问题与背景
尼帕病毒 (NiV) 是一种高后果的人畜共患病原体,可导致严重呼吸道疾病和 脑炎,在核酸检测可能不切实际的资源有限环境中,快速 诊断 对于控制 暴发 至关重要。

该研究开发了一种针对 NiV 的快速抗原检测方法,使用针对病毒核衣壳蛋白的纤维素纳米珠增强侧流免疫测定法。

从人类合成噬菌体展示文库中分离出抗 NiV 核衣壳蛋白抗体,重新格式化为 IgG,通过 ELISA 和免疫荧光进行筛选,并以夹心配置(mAb #5 作为捕获;mAb #2 作为检测)与生物层干涉测量法确认的纳摩尔结合亲和力配对。

最终的测定在 15 分钟内产生结果,重组 NiV NP 的检测分析限为 1 ng/mL,并且通过便携式阅读器检测到的病毒载量低至 4.1 × 10^7 拷贝/mL(4.1 × 10^6 拷贝/反应),通过肉眼检测到 8.3 × 10^7 拷贝/mL(8.3 × 10^6 拷贝/反应),使用 γ 照射的培养 NiV 进行定量RT-ddPCR。
解释与创新
作者将该检测解释为提供快速、无需设备的 NiV 检测,与其他呼吸道病毒的交叉反应性极小,强调了其在 暴发 易发、资源有限的环境中对护理点 诊断 的潜在实用性。
科研与公卫意义
这种快速抗原测试可以使 NiV 诊断 能够及时支持在分子测试不可用或延迟的情况下的 暴发 遏制和患者管理。
局限与证据强度
由于缺乏人类病例的临床样本测试和验证,证据受到限制,这限制了普遍性和现实世界的性能评估。

A Rapid Detection of Nipah Virus Nucleocapsid Antigen in Inactivated Viral Cultures.

Authors: Gyeongmu Choi, Jung Kim, Hansaem Lee, Pyeonghwa Jeon, Jae Hun Jeong, Yujin Jung, Jin Seo Park, Mi Kyoung Lee, Kyung-Chang Kim, Jong-Hwan Lee
Original abstract (English)
Nipah virus (NiV) is a highly lethal zoonotic henipavirus that causes severe respiratory disease and encephalitis and remains a priority pathogen for outbreak preparedness. Timely diagnosis is essential for containment, yet nucleic acid-based testing is often impractical in outbreak-prone, resource-limited settings. Here, we report the development of a rapid antigen test for NiV based on a cellulose nanobead (CNB)-enhanced lateral flow immunoassay (LFIA) targeting the viral nucleocapsid protein (NP). Anti-NiV NP antibodies were isolated from human synthetic phage-display libraries (scFv and Fab) by iterative bio-panning, reformatted as full-length IgGs, and screened using ELISA and immunofluorescence assays. Four lead antibodies were advanced to a sandwich-pairing matrix, which identified an optimized capture-detection configuration (mAb #5 as capture; mAb #2 as detection). Bio-layer interferometry confirmed nanomolar binding affinities for the selected antibodies (K D = 39.50 nM for mAb #5; K D = 26.36 nM for mAb #2). The finalized CNB-LFIA produced results within 15 min and achieved an analytical limit of detection of 1 ng/mL for recombinant NiV NP. The assay observed no cross-reactivity with SARS-CoV-2, SARS-CoV, MERS-CoV, or influenza A, whereas weak cross-reactivity for measles virus NP was observed only at a high antigen concentration (2 μg/mL). Using γ-irradiated cultured NiV quantified by RT-ddPCR, the LFIA detected viral load down to 4.1 × 10 7 copies/mL (4.1 × 106 copies/reaction) by a portable reader and 8.3 × 10 7 copies/mL (8.3 × 106 copies/reaction) by the naked eye. Collectively, this CNB-LFIA provides rapid, equipment-free NiV detection, supporting its potential utility for point-of-care diagnosis in resource-limited settings.

Question and background
Nipah virus (NiV) is a high-consequence zoonotic pathogen causing severe respiratory disease and encephalitis, and rapid diagnosis is critical for outbreak control in resource-limited settings where nucleic acid testing may be impractical.
Design and population
The study developed a rapid antigen detection assay for NiV using a cellulose nanobead-enhanced lateral flow immunoassay targeting the viral nucleocapsid protein.

The authors interpret the assay as providing rapid, equipment-free NiV detection with minimal cross-reactivity to other respiratory viruses, highlighting its potential utility for point-of-care diagnosis in outbreak-prone, resource-limited settings.
Scientific and public-health significance
This rapid antigen test could enable timely NiV diagnosis to support outbreak containment and patient management where molecular testing is unavailable or delayed.
Limitations and evidence strength
The evidence is limited by the absence of clinical sample testing and validation in human cases, which constrains generalisability and real-world performance assessment.

利用苏格兰海洋养殖海藻生产岩藻依聚糖:提取方法优化、生物活性测试和未来纯化趋势

Production of fucoidan from Scottish ocean farmed seaweed: optimisation of extraction methods, biological activity testing and future purification trends
作者： Matthew Chadwic
摘要中文翻译褐藻糖胶是一种长链硫酸化多糖,天然存在于棕色海藻中。文献报道该分子具有多种生物活性,包括 抗病毒药物、抗氧化、抗真菌、抗菌、抗炎、益生元、抗凝血和抗癌特性。因此,该提取物在生物医学和营养保健市场的应用引起了特别的兴趣。根据生物因素(海藻种类、年龄、收获季节和地点)和工艺因素(使用的提取技术和条件),可能有多种提取物结构。这是一个重大挑战,因为提取工艺变量与所得岩藻依聚糖产品特性之间的关系尚未建立。此外,岩藻依聚糖提取物对于给定的生物活性必须具备的具体特征尚不清楚。本博士论文旨在解决这些知识空白,这些知识空白对岩藻依聚糖作为商业产品的实施构成了重大障碍。这项工作最终旨在支持该项目的工业合作伙伴 BioMara Ltd.,希望为营养保健品和保健品市场生产商业海藻衍生产品。最初的工作重点是确定岩藻依聚糖在抗氧化、抗病毒药物、抗真菌、抗菌和益生元活性方面的潜在关键质量属性。这是通过对市售提取物进行详细表征,检查其单糖特征、分子量和元素含量来实现的。然后,这些提取物接受了生物活性测试,重点包括抗氧化作用、尼帕病毒 约 80% 的抑制、都柏林念珠菌生长约 60% 的抑制以及暴露于岩藻依聚糖时干酪乳杆菌生长加倍。还测试了真菌种类耳念珠菌、新型隐球菌和肺炎克雷伯菌。通过相关分析确定了岩藻依聚糖特定生物活性的潜在关键质量属性,表明岩藻糖含量、硫酸化度和分子量值得未来研究。确定了潜在的关键质量属性后,采用实验设计 (DoE) 方法对稀酸和超声辅助提取工艺进行建模和优化,以提高产量以及硫酸盐、抗氧化剂和多酚含量。在建立预测模型之前,采用明确的筛选设计(DSD)来识别给定响应的统计相关过程参数,并使用实验数据集进行验证。接下来,进一步研究了 抗病毒药物 和益生元活性,以更好地表征其作用的广度和商业潜力。抗病毒药物 研究的重点是商业岩藻依聚糖提取物对流感病毒假型的影响。观察到剂量依赖性抑制,并通过拟合 IC50 曲线来确定效力。报告的 IC50 值(16-38 μg mL-1)与文献中的其他岩藻依聚糖和 抗病毒药物 药物相当,表明所测试的提取物具有商业潜力。使用三种测定法进一步探讨了岩藻依聚糖的益生元作用。第一项研究通过基于平板的测定,将岩藻依聚糖对益生菌(干酪乳杆菌和鼠李糖乳杆菌)生长的选择性影响与致病菌(大肠杆菌)进行比较。观察到使用一种市售岩藻依聚糖对干酪乳杆菌的选择性效应以及使用菊粉提取物对鼠李糖乳杆菌的选择性效应。接下来,通过 GC/MS 检查提取物对有益脂肪酸(乙酸、丙酸、丁酸和乳酸)产生的影响,但没有观察到持续影响。最后,研究了岩藻依聚糖对 Caco-2 细胞(作为肠道模型)的细胞毒性作用,没有观察到细胞毒性,这表明提取物可以安全地用作益生元补充剂。最后一章批判性地回顾了目前岩藻依聚糖生产中采用的纯化方法(沉淀、膜和色谱),包括每种方法的基本原理、优点和缺点。根据产品的最终市场和生产规模,建立了选择适当纯化技术的最佳实践。最后,提供了对未来岩藻依聚糖纯化技术的展望,包括使用二维液相色谱进行高价值生物活性应用。

褐藻糖胶是一种来自褐海藻的硫酸化多糖,已报道了 抗病毒药物 特性,但提取变量、产品特性和特定生物活性之间的关系仍知之甚少,限制了其商业应用。

抗病毒药物 测试显示对流感病毒假型的剂量依赖性抑制,报告的 IC50 值在 16 至 38 μg mL-1 之间,与其他岩藻依聚糖和 抗病毒药物 药物相当,表明潜在的商业可行性。

该研究确定了 抗病毒药物 活性的潜在岩藻依聚糖关键质量属性,并证明优化的提取工艺可以产生具有与现有药物相当的 抗病毒药物 效力的提取物,突出了商业潜力。
科研与公卫意义
研究结果表明,岩藻依聚糖提取物可以开发为 抗病毒药物 制剂或营养保健品,特别是对于需要天然生物活性化合物的市场,有待进一步验证和监管评估。
局限与证据强度
证据仅限于使用流感假型的体外 抗病毒药物 测定,并不直接涉及 尼帕病毒;由于依赖商业提取物且缺乏针对特定目标的 尼帕病毒 测试,该研究的强度中等。

Production of fucoidan from Scottish ocean farmed seaweed: optimisation of extraction methods, biological activity testing and future purification trends

Authors: Matthew Chadwic
Original abstract (English)
Fucoidan is a long-chain sulphated polysaccharide, occurring naturally in brown seaweed species. The molecule has been reported in the literature to possess numerous biological activities, including antiviral, antioxidant, antifungal, antibacterial, anti-inflammatory, prebiotic, anticoagulant and anticancer properties. Therefore, the extract has garnered particular interest for application in the biomedical and nutraceutical markets. A diverse range of extract structures is possible, depending on both biological (seaweed species, age, harvest season and location) and process factors (extraction technique and conditions used). This presents a significant challenge because the relationships between extraction process variables and the resulting fucoidan product characteristics are poorly established. Furthermore, the specific characteristics a fucoidan extract must possess for a given bioactivity are unclear. This PhD thesis aimed to address these knowledge gaps, which present a significant barrier to fucoidan’s implementation as a commercial product. With this work ultimately designed to support the project's industrial partner, BioMara Ltd., desire to produce commercial seaweed-derived products for the nutraceutical and health supplement markets. Initial work focused on identifying potential fucoidan key quality attributes for antioxidant, antiviral, antifungal, antibacterial and prebiotic activities. This was achieved through a detailed characterisation of commercially available extracts, examining their monosaccharide profile, molecular weights and elemental content. These extracts then underwent biological activity testing, with highlights including antioxidant effects, approximately 80% inhibition of Nipah virus, around 60% inhibition of Candida dubliniensis growth and a doubling of Lactobacillus casei growth when exposed to fucoidans. Also tested were the fungal species Candida auris, Cryptococcus neoformans and the bacteria Klebsiella pneumoniae. Potential fucoidan key quality attributes for specific bioactivities were identified through a correlation analysis, indicating fucose content, sulphation degree and molecular weight were worthy of future study. Having identified potential key quality attributes, a Design of Experiments (DoE) methodology was employed to model and optimise both dilute acid and ultrasound-assisted extraction processes for yield as well as sulphate, antioxidant and polyphenol contents. A definitive Screening Design (DSD) was employed to identify statistically relevant process parameters for a given response before building a predictive model, which was validated with an experimental dataset. Next, antiviral and prebiotic activities were investigated further to better characterise the breadth of their effects and commercial potential. Antiviral studies focused on the effect of commercial fucoidan extracts on Influenza viral pseudotypes. A dose-dependent inhibition was observed, and the potency was determined through the fitting of IC50 curves. The reported IC50 values (16-38 μg mL-1) were comparable to other fucoidans in the literature and antiviral drugs, suggesting commercial potential for the extracts tested. The prebiotic effect of fucoidan was further explored using three assays. The first examined how selective the effect of fucoidan on probiotic bacterial growth (Lactobacillus casei and Lactobacillus rhamnosus) was compared to pathogenic (Escherichia coli) bacteria through a plate-based assay. Selective effects were observed for L. casei with one commercial fucoidan and for L. rhamnosus with an inulin extract. Next, the effect of extracts on the production of beneficial fatty acids (acetic, propanoic, butyric and lactic acids) was examined through GC/MS, with no sustained effect observed. Finally, the cytotoxic effects of fucoidan on Caco-2 cells, as a model for the gut, were studied, and no cytotoxicity was observed, suggesting extracts could be applied safely as a prebiotic supplement. The final chapter critically reviewed current purification methods (precipitation, membranes, and chromatography) employed in fucoidan production, including the underlying principles, advantages, and disadvantages of each method. Best practices for selecting an appropriate purification technique were established based on the final market of the product and the scale of production. Finally, perspectives on future technologies for fucoidan purification are provided, including the use of two-dimensional liquid chromatography for high-value bioactive applications.

Original abstract (English)
Nipah virus (NiV) is known to be an emerging zoonotic pathogen of the Paramyxoviridae family, which was first identified in 1998, and is also associated with high morbidity and mortality due to severe encephalitis and respiratory illness. Transmission occurs through direct contact with infected animals, contaminated food, and human-to-human transmission , thereby further highlighting its epidemic potential. Despite significant advances in understanding its virology, pathogenesis, as well as clinical manifestations, NiV outbreaks continue to pose substantial public health challenges, usually in endemic regions. Current management of NiV remains largely supportive with no licensed antivirals or vaccines available, thus emphasising the need for effective medical countermeasures. Recent therapeutic developments must focus on monoclonal antibodies and antiviral agents, including Remdesivir, which have shown promising efficacy in preclinical studies. Vaccine candidates, including recombinant protein, viral vector, and mRNA-based platforms, are being examined in their early phases of clinical evaluation, showing potential for robust protection of immunity. Future research priorities must include the development of rapid point-of-care diagnosis, strengthening genomic surveillance while also implementing One Health approaches integrating human, animal and environmental data for the prevention of spillover events. Refining animal models and elucidating immune correlates of protection are very important aspects for translating preclinical successes into adequately licensed interventions for management approaches for NiV. Collaborative international frameworks as well as structured R&D roadmaps are critical options for the acceleration of vaccine and therapeutic development, thereby further ensuring rapid deployment during outbreaks. This narrative review article highlights current knowledge on the epidemiology of NiV, pathogenesis, clinical management, as well as emerging preventive-therapeutic strategies while also identifying critical research gaps. It underscores the need for a proactive, integrated approach which combines medical, public health and scientific efforts for the mitigation of the threat of NiV globally.

Two deaths reported; two hospitalized (one adult, one child); containment zones established across seven villages; risk of human-to-human transmission acknowledged.
Response, status and uncertainty
Containment zones established; schools and offices shut; contact tracing and isolation of symptomatic individuals underway; medical personnel quarantined; preliminary investigation suggests initial case linked to a landowner in Marutonkara; spread to other countries deemed highly unlikely.
