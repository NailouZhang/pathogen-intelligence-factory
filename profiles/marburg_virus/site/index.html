埃博拉病毒病、马尔堡病毒病、克里米亚-刚果出血热和拉沙热的细菌感染

Bacterial infection in Ebola virus disease, Marburg virus disease, Crimean–Congo hemorrhagic fever, and Lassa fever
作者： Brady Page, Elizabeth Moreton, David A. Wohl, William A. Fischer
摘要中文翻译摘要 背景 由于对高后果病毒感染 (HCVI) 患者细菌合并感染的担忧,经验性抗生素给药已成为护理标准。然而,HCVI 中细菌共感染的 发病率、微生物学和临床意义仍不清楚。方法 我们对 病例报告、队列研究 研究以及描述埃博拉病毒病 (EVD)、马尔堡病毒病 (MVD)、克里米亚-刚果出血热 (CCHF) 和拉沙热 (LF) 细菌共感染的横断面分析进行了系统回顾。提取了有关研究设计、设置、诊断方法、病原体、抗生素使用和结果的数据。结果 30 篇出版物符合纳入标准。菌血症在 CCHF 患者中最常见 (n = 46),其次是 EVD (n = 16)、MVD (n = 16) 和 LF (n = 2)。在非洲以外地区接受治疗的 27 名埃博拉病毒病患者中,有 7 名 (26%) 患有细菌合并感染。在 HCVI 中,菌血症在症状出现后的中位时间(范围 0-23)检测到 11 天,并且与败血症的特征相关。病原体包括肠道菌群、医疗保健相关微生物、机会性病原体和人畜共患细菌,尤其是布鲁氏菌属。头孢曲松是最常见的经验性抗生素,但预计对少数血流分离株具有活性。EVD 或 MVD 合并感染患者的 死亡率 与未合并细菌感染的患者相似,而合并 CCHF 患者的 死亡率 高于未合并感染的患者(15.5%;95% CI 8.0–25.9% vs 5.7%;95% CI 5.1–6.4%)。结论 尽管是推动经验性抗生素使用的一个主要问题,但 HCVI 中的细菌合并感染仍然缺乏描述,无法确定其 发病率 或临床意义。需要对细菌 诊断 的标准化方案和抗菌药物耐药性特征进行前瞻性研究来指导抗菌策略。

Bacterial infection in Ebola virus disease, Marburg virus disease, Crimean–Congo hemorrhagic fever, and Lassa fever

Authors: Brady Page, Elizabeth Moreton, David A. Wohl, William A. Fischer
Original abstract (English)
Abstract Background Concern for bacterial coinfection in patients with high consequence viral infections (HCVIs) has made the empiric antibiotic administration the standard of care. However, the incidence, microbiology, and clinical significance of bacterial coinfection in HCVIs remain unclear. Methods We conducted a systematic review of case reports, cohort studies, and cross-sectional analyses describing bacterial coinfection in Ebola virus disease (EVD), Marburg virus disease (MVD), Crimean–Congo hemorrhagic fever (CCHF), and Lassa fever (LF). Data were extracted on study design, setting, diagnostic methods, pathogens, antibiotic use, and outcomes. Results Thirty publications met inclusion criteria. Bacteremia was most commonly reported among patients with CCHF (n = 46), followed by EVD (n = 16), MVD (n = 16), and LF (n = 2). Of 27 EVD patients treated outside Africa, seven (26%) had bacterial coinfection. Across HCVIs, bacteremia was detected a median of 11 days after symptom onset (range 0–23) and was associated with features of sepsis. Pathogens included enteric flora, healthcare-associated organisms, opportunistic pathogens, and zoonotic bacteria—most notably Brucella spp. Ceftriaxone was the most common empiric antibiotic but is predicted to have activity against a minority of bloodstream isolates. Mortality among coinfected patients with EVD or MVD was similar to those without bacterial coinfection, whereas coinfected CCHF patients had higher mortality than those without coinfection (15.5%; 95% CI 8.0–25.9% vs 5.7%; 95% CI 5.1–6.4%). Conclusions Despite being a major concern driving empiric antibiotic use, bacterial coinfection in HCVIs remains poorly described, precluding determination of its incidence or clinical significance. Prospective studies with standardized protocols for bacterial diagnosis and characterization of antimicrobial resistance are needed to guide antimicrobial strategies.

摘要中文翻译
查看研究七要素
问题与背景
该研究旨在了解埃博拉病毒如何在中枢神经系统的免疫特权环境中建立持续感染,解决病毒利用免疫特权生态位作为长期 储存宿主 的知识差距。
设计与对象
该研究利用人脑类器官来研究中枢神经系统中的埃博拉病毒感染动态,检查多种神经细胞类型,包括神经元、星形胶质细胞、少突胶质细胞和小胶质细胞。
核心方法
该研究描述了持续感染期间的病毒复制动力学、传播 机制和 进化,分析了缺陷病毒 基因组 和激活的小胶质细胞产生的促炎介质。

在人脑类器官中,埃博拉病毒通过出芽和直接细胞间传播感染多种神经细胞类型,并与病毒进化相关的持续感染和缺陷病毒基因组的积累相关。
解释与创新
作者解释说,免疫特权使埃博拉病毒能够作为一种主动感染持续存在,这代表了对病毒如何利用特殊环境来建立慢性感染和复发的新认识。
科研与公卫意义
这些发现为监测在免疫特权部位持续存在病毒储存宿主的策略提供了信息,并强调了针对埃博拉幸存者慢性神经炎症和神经元损伤的治疗的需要。
局限与证据强度
由于依赖类器官模型,证据强度中等,该模型可能无法完全复制体内人类中枢神经系统的复杂性。

Question and background
The study aimed to understand how Ebola virus establishes persistent infection within the immune-privileged environment of the central nervous system, addressing the knowledge gap of viral exploitation of immune-privileged niches as long-term reservoirs.
Design and population
The research utilized human brain organoids to investigate Ebola virus infection dynamics in the central nervous system, examining multiple neural cell types including neurons, astrocytes, oligodendrocytes and microglia.

摘要中文翻译蛋白质-脂质相互作用是膜相关蛋白质结构和功能的基本调节剂,从而影响涉及病毒生命周期和正常细胞功能的过程。因此,这些相互作用对于理解多种疾病进展的基本原理变得很重要。病毒基质蛋白是研究这些过程的绝佳模型系统,因为这些蛋白严重依赖 宿主 细胞膜来协调运输和组装。在本论文中,我们研究了各种蛋白质的膜关联的分子机制,重点是埃博拉病毒和马尔堡病毒基质蛋白VP40。这些病毒引起高度致命的出血热,迫切需要找到对抗它们的方法,因此,了解这些病毒如何利用它们的蛋白质来利用马尔堡病毒细胞膜是对抗它们的非常重要的一步。这一知识已进一步扩展到人副流感病毒 3 (HPIV-3) 基质蛋白 M 和人蛋白神经酰胺激酶 (CERK),以检查它们如何与某些脂质相互作用。在诱变、细胞分析以及生化和生物物理方法的帮助下,本研究揭示了 VP40 膜结合残基在协调病毒生命周期多个阶段中的重要作用。这还鉴定了两种蛋白质中的关键残基,并表明对 突变 的敏感性存在差异。我们还确定了 HPIV-3 M 和 CERK 的脂质结合模式。总体而言,本文强调了蛋白质-脂质相互作用作为膜相关蛋白质功能决定因素的重要性,并揭示了不同的分子结构如何实现相似的生物学功能。这些发现增进了我们对病毒组装的理解以及对脂质依赖性细胞过程的更广泛的了解。

<b>UNDERSTANDING LIPID-PROTEIN INTERACTIONS IN THE CONTEXT OF EBOLA AND MARBURG VIRUSES TO UNCOVER UNDERLYING MECHANISMS OF DISEASE PROGRESSION</b>

Authors: Barsha Bhowal
Original abstract (English)
Protein-lipid interactions are fundamental regulators of membrane-associated protein structure and function, thus influencing processes involving both viral life cycle and normal cellular functioning. Therefore, these interactions become important in understanding the underlying principles of progression of multitude of diseases. Viral matrix proteins are a fantastic model system to study these processes because these proteins rely heavily on host cellular membranes to coordinate trafficking and assembly. in this thesis, we have investigated the molecular mechanisms of membrane association of various proteins, with a focus on Ebola and Marburg virus matrix protein VP40.These viruses cause a highly fatal hemorrhagic fever and there is a dire need for finding ways to combat them, hence, learning how these viruses use their proteins to exploit host cellular membranes is a very important step in the battle against them. This knowledge has been further extended to the human parainfluenza virus 3 (HPIV-3) matrix protein M and the human protein ceramide kinase (CERK) to examine how they interact with certain lipids.With the help of mutagenesis, cellular assays and biochemical and biophysical approaches, this study has uncovered the essential roles of membrane binding residues of VP40 in coordinating multiple stages of viral life cycle. This has also identified critical residues in both proteins and indicated a difference in sensitivity towards mutations. We have also identified lipid binding modes of HPIV-3 M and CERK.Overall, this thesis highlights the importance of protein-lipid interactions as determinants of membrane-associated protein function and reveals how distinct molecular structure can achieve similar biological functions. These findings advance our understanding of viral assembly as well as a broader insight into lipid-dependent cellular processes.

Digital Health for Pandemic Preparedness: A Critical Review of Innovations, Equity Gaps, and Governance in Infectious Disease Surveillance

Authors: Abdolreza Babamahmoodi, F. Babamahmoodi, Majid Marjani
Original abstract (English)
Context: Recent infectious disease emergencies, including SARS-CoV-2, mpox (2022 - 2024), highly pathogenic avian influenza A(H5N1) in US dairy cattle (2024 - 2025), and the Marburg virus in Rwanda (2024), have strained traditional surveillance systems. Digital health has been proposed as a paradigm shift; however, most reviews have focused on diagnostic accuracy while neglecting equity and governance. Evidence Acquisition: We conducted a structured thematic narrative review of peer-reviewed and grey literature published from January 2010 to March 2025, reported in accordance with PRISMA 2020 and PRISMA-ScR. PubMed, Scopus, and Web of Science were searched from February 5 to 18, 2025. Search keywords combined three concept blocks: (1) Digital-health technologies (artificial intelligence, machine learning, mHealth, eHealth, telemedicine, electronic health records, wearables, wastewater surveillance, genomic surveillance, natural-language processing); (2) infectious diseases (outbreak, pandemic, epidemic, COVID-19, Ebola, Zika, mpox, influenza, antimicrobial resistance, Marburg); and (3) public-health functions (surveillance, detection, forecasting, contact tracing, governance, equity). Full Boolean syntax is provided. Records were deduplicated in EndNote X20 and screened by 2 blinded reviewers in Rayyan (Cohen κ = 0.81). Empirical studies were appraised using the Mixed Methods Appraisal Tool 2018, and reviews were appraised using Joanna Briggs Institute checklists. The synthesis followed Braun and Clarke's reflexive thematic analysis and was interpreted through sociotechnical systems and data justice lenses. Results: Of 1498 records, 142 studies met the inclusion criteria; 48% (69/142) were rated as high quality. The evidence base was heavily skewed regionally (high-income countries [HICs], 41%; sub-Saharan Africa or South Asia, 19%). No technology was universally superior. Artificial intelligence (AI) studies were concentrated in HICs (18/32, 56%), whereas short-message-service (SMS) mHealth studies were concentrated in low- and middle-income countries (LMICs; 15/25, 60%). Recurring barriers included algorithmic bias, fragmented interoperability, donor-dependent sustainability, and slow benefit-sharing of pathogen data with LMICs. Conclusions: Across 142 included studies the synthesis supports a measured conclusion: Digital health is necessary but not sufficient for pandemic preparedness. The patterns most consistently observed in the evidence — regional skew, recurring governance failures, donor-dependent sustainability, and design choices that determine equity outcomes — together indicate that equity, sustainability, and accountability are constituents of effectiveness rather than obstacles to it. The binding constraint is increasingly political and institutional will rather than analytic uncertainty.
