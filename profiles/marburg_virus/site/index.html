来自马尔堡病毒糖蛋白的MHC-I表位整合免疫反应分析

Integrated immunoreactivity analysis of MHC-I epitopes derived from the Marburg virus glycoprotein
作者： Yu-Lin Yang, Jing Wang, En-Qi Guan, Heng-Zheng Wei, Yuan-Jie Sun, Jian-Chang Li, Yuxuan Zhang, Hao-Bo Kang, Zhen-Chi Fang, Jie-Rui Zhao
摘要中文翻译引言 马尔堡病毒(MARV)是一种引起出血热的丝状病毒,其糖蛋白(GP)是疫苗研究的重要靶标。MHC I类(MHC-I)限制性T细胞表位对于诱导细胞毒性CD8 + T细胞免疫应答至关重要。本研究旨在通过整合生物信息学和实验方法,识别来自马尔堡病毒安哥拉株GP的MHC-I限制性T细胞表位。方法 我们获取了马尔堡病毒安哥拉株GP序列,并将其划分为673个重叠的9-mer肽段。通过整合五个计算预测算法、免疫原性评分工具和BLASTp保守性分析,我们筛选出来自马尔堡病毒安哥拉株GP的9-mer肽段。使用HPEPDOCK对MHC-I结构进行分子对接,并进行分子动力学(MD)分析。采用双向层次聚类分析来分析不同MHC-I分子间的结合相似性。在表位区域中识别出的突变被测试其对亲和力的影响。使用专门工具预测表位的毒性和过敏性。最后,用编码马尔堡病毒GP的DNA 疫苗免疫BALB/c和C57BL/6小鼠。随后通过ELISpot检测小鼠脾细胞中IFN-γ的分泌,通过ELISA检测血清IgG,通过流式细胞术检测CD8 + T细胞细胞因子的产生。结果 在673个GP肽段中,八个表位(GAFFLYDRI、REGDMFPFL、DMFPFLDGL、NENDCDAEL、AGLSWIPFF、NQNNLVCRL、TGWGLGGKW、SDWGVLTNL)被预测为具有高亲和力的MHC-I结合能力、强免疫原性和种内保守性。双向层次聚类分析、分子对接、序列比对和毒性/过敏性预测表明,这些表位在选定的等位基因中具有结合能力、结构稳定性、有限的突变影响以及低预测毒性。在后续的ELISpot验证中,八个表位引发了强烈的IFN-γ反应。此外,免疫GP的小鼠血清中含有能够结合相应线性肽序列的IgG。结论 通过整合计算与实验方法的流程,我们鉴定了八个保守且具有免疫原性的MHC-I表位,这些表位可能成为未来疫苗设计的有前景候选物。

Integrated immunoreactivity analysis of MHC-I epitopes derived from the Marburg virus glycoprotein

Authors: Yu-Lin Yang, Jing Wang, En-Qi Guan, Heng-Zheng Wei, Yuan-Jie Sun, Jian-Chang Li, Yuxuan Zhang, Hao-Bo Kang, Zhen-Chi Fang, Jie-Rui Zhao
Original abstract (English)
Introduction Marburg virus (MARV) is a hemorrhagic fever virus, and its glycoprotein (GP) represents an important target for vaccine research. MHC class I (MHC-I)-restricted T cell epitopes are critical for inducing cytotoxic CD8 + T cell responses. In this study, we aimed to identify MHC-I-restricted T cell epitopes derived from the MARV Angola strain GP through an integrated bioinformatics and experimental approach. Methods We retrieved the MARV Angola strain GP sequence and divided it into 673 overlapping 9-mer peptides. By integrating five computational prediction algorithms, immunogenicity scoring tools and BLASTp conservation analysis, we screened 9-mer peptides from the MARV Angola GP. Molecular docking was performed with MHC-I structures using HPEPDOCK and molecular dynamics (MD) analysis. Bidirectional hierarchical clustering was used to analyze binding similarity across MHC-I molecules. Mutations identified in epitope regions were tested for their effect on affinity. Toxicity and allergenicity of the epitopes were predicted using dedicated tools. Finally, BALB/c and C57BL/6 mice were immunized with a DNA vaccine encoding MARV GP. IFN-γ secretion in mouse splenocytes was then measured by ELISpot, serum IgG by ELISA, and CD8 + T cell cytokine production by flow cytometry. Results Among 673 GP peptides, eight epitopes (GAFFLYDRI, REGDMFPFL, DMFPFLDGL, NENDCDAEL, AGLSWIPFF, NQNNLVCRL, TGWGLGGKW, SDWGVLTNL) were predicted to exhibit high-affinity MHC-I binding, strong immunogenicity, and intraspecies conservation. Bidirectional hierarchical clustering analysis, molecular docking, sequence alignment, and toxicity/allergenicity predictions suggested binding across selected alleles, structural stability, limited impact from identified mutations, and low predicted toxicity. In subsequent ELISpot validation, eight epitopes elicited strong IFN-γ responses. In addition, serum from GP-immunized mice contained IgG that bound the corresponding linear peptide sequences. Conclusion An integrated computational-experimental pipeline identified eight conserved, immunogenic MHC-I epitopes from MARV GP. These selected epitopes may serve as promising candidates for future vaccine design.

Question and background
The study aimed to address the gaps in understanding the biological and epidemiological characteristics of Marburg virus and its implications for quarantine and surveillance in non-human primates. This is critical due to the increasing emergence of zoonotic infectious diseases and the role of NHPs in preclinical research.
Design and population
The study reviewed national quarantine frameworks for non-human primates, examined pathogen-specific features, and discussed their relevance to disease surveillance and control. It focused on Marburg virus and its related entities, particularly in the context of NHP quarantine procedures.
Core methods
The study conducted a comprehensive review of existing literature and national quarantine policies, analyzing the biological and epidemiological characteristics of Marburg virus and its related pathogens. It also evaluated the implications of these characteristics for quarantine and surveillance practices.
Main results
The study found that Marburg virus can be transmitted through direct contact, particularly via blood, and that it poses significant zoonotic risks. It also highlighted the need for active microbiological monitoring due to the potential for asymptomatic or mild infections in NHPs.
Interpretation and novelty
The authors interpreted that the biological features of Marburg virus, such as its ability to establish persistent infections and its transmission routes, make complete elimination difficult. They emphasized the importance of integrating risk-based approaches into quarantine practices.
Scientific and public-health significance
The findings inform the development of more effective quarantine and surveillance strategies for non-human primates, which are crucial for preclinical research and public health preparedness against emerging viral diseases.
Limitations and evidence strength
The study is limited by its reliance on existing literature and policies, which may vary in quality and completeness. The evidence is moderate, as it is based on a review of published data rather than original research.

免疫调节RIG-I激动剂SLR14对多种高致病性负链RNA病毒具有保护作用

Immunomodulatory RIG-I Agonist SLR14 Confers Protection Against a Range of Highly Pathogenic Negative Sense RNA viruses
作者： Kristina Meier, Judith Olejnik, Reyes JM, Jonna B. Westover, Pushpinder Bawa, Elizabeth R. Wrobel, Corey W. Henderson, Pan Hu, Adam J. Hume, Alexander M. Holtz
摘要中文翻译新兴和再新兴的动物源性RNA病毒,包括丝状病毒、亨尼帕病毒和裂谷热病毒(RVFV),对人类健康构成重大威胁。由于其流行潜力和高致病性,这些病毒被归类为世界卫生组织优先病原体,但我们仍缺乏针对大多数这些危险病原体的有效医疗对策(MCMs),这表明针对丝状病毒的抗病毒药物将有助于广泛作用的MCMs。在此,我们评估了SLR14,一种免疫调节茎环RNA,其激活RIG-I的能力,针对一组高致病性病毒的活性。无论是在预防性还是治疗性给药情况下,SLR14 治疗均提供了广泛的保护作用,显著减少了埃博拉病毒(EBOV)、马尔堡病毒和苏丹病毒在人类原代单核细胞来源的巨噬细胞中的感染,以及在iPSC来源的内皮细胞中尼帕病毒的复制。在体内,单次剂量的SLR14在预防性给药时可保护小鼠免受致命的RVFV感染,并且值得注意的是,即使在感染后两天内给药,仍能保持显著的疗效。治疗性给药的SLR14也改善了埃博拉病毒感染小鼠的存活率。尽管埃博拉病毒感染和SLR14 治疗在人类巨噬细胞中激活了重叠的先天免疫通路,但病毒感染诱导了非保护性反应,而SLR14则引发了良好调控、显著更强且具有保护作用的干扰素刺激和炎症基因特征,同时避免了干扰素基因的长期上调。我们的数据表明SLR14是一种针对丝状病毒的强效治疗药物,对一些最致命的病毒病原体具有广泛的抗病毒药物疗效。

本研究的局限性包括使用人类原代细胞和小鼠,这可能限制其普遍适用性。本研究未探讨SLR14 治疗的长期效应,也未包括与其他抗病毒药物 治疗的详细比较。由于研究处于预印本状态,且需要在更大规模的临床试验中进一步验证,其证据强度为中等。

Immunomodulatory RIG-I Agonist SLR14 Confers Protection Against a Range of Highly Pathogenic Negative Sense RNA viruses

Authors: Kristina Meier, Judith Olejnik, Reyes JM, Jonna B. Westover, Pushpinder Bawa, Elizabeth R. Wrobel, Corey W. Henderson, Pan Hu, Adam J. Hume, Alexander M. Holtz
Original abstract (English)
Emerging and re-emerging zoonotic RNA viruses, including filoviruses, henipaviruses and Rift Valley Fever virus (RVFV), pose a significant threat to human health. Due to their epidemic potential and high pathogenicity, these viruses are categorized as WHO priority pathogens, yet we still lack effective medical countermeasures (MCMs) against most of these dangerous pathogens, supporting that host-directed antivirals will be advantageous for broadly acting MCMs. Here we assessed the antiviral efficacy of SLR14, an immunomodulatory stem-loop RNA that activates RIG-I, for its activity against a panel of highly pathogenic viruses. SLR14 treatment provided broad-spectrum protection, markedly reducing infection with Ebola virus (EBOV), Marburg virus, and Sudan virus in human primary monocyte-derived macrophages, and Nipah virus replication in iPSC-derived endothelial cells, whether administered prophylactically or therapeutically. In vivo, a single dose of SLR14 protected mice against lethal RVFV infection when administered prophylactically, and, notably, retained significant efficacy when administered up to two days post-infection. Therapeutic SLR14 administration also improved survival rates of EBOV-infected mice. Although EBOV infection and SLR14 treatment activated overlapping innate immune pathways in human macrophages, viral infection induced a nonprotective response, whereas SLR14 elicited a well-regulated, markedly stronger and protective interferon-stimulated and inflammatory gene signature while avoiding prolonged IFN gene upregulation. Our data identify SLR14 as a potent host-directed therapeutic with broad antiviral efficacy against some of the most lethal viral pathogens.

Original abstract (English)
Abstract Recent global changes, largely driven mostly by human activities, have increased the frequency and intensity of contacts between humans and wildlife, leading to the emergence of new zoonotic diseases at human/wildlife interfaces. Within the framework of the One Health paradigm, a comprehensive understanding of the ecology of host species involved in multi-host pathogen dynamics is essential. This understanding can be enhanced through the study of these dynamics within individual hosts and across different host species. Establishing evolutionary relationships through accurate and robust phylogenetic analyses, combined with the ecological characteristics of the species can help to predict possible new host species. Chiropterans have been identified as significant reservoirs for various zoonotic pathogens, predominantly viruses. In Africa, species within the Pteropodidae family (Old World Fruit Bat) have been identified as potential maintenance hosts of the Filoviridae viruses including Ebola and Marburg virus, which present a public health importance. Our study refines African Pteropodidae phylogenies by incorporating multiple specimens per species, adding 20 new mitochondrial genome sequences, and integrating sequences of five nuclear markers (BRCA1, FGB, RAG1, RAG2, vWF) from 33 specimens. These additions reduce phylogenetic artefacts and provide a comprehensive view of African Pteropodidae evolutionary history.

范围与问题
本评论聚焦于针对RNA病毒的广谱直接作用抗病毒药物药物(BSAs)的开发,特别强调了开发此类药物以应对当前未满足的医疗需求并增强大流行病应对能力的必要性。核心问题是:是否可以开发出针对多种RNA病毒属、科甚至跨科的BSAs,以及如何有效实施这一方法。
证据基础与方法
本评论是对现有文献的叙述性综合,没有明确提到系统性搜索方法或元分析。它提供了针对RNA病毒的已批准和正在研究的抗病毒药物药物的非详尽概述,并讨论了其广谱活性背后的生物决定因素。作者还概述了在暴发情境下评估此类药物所需的临床开发框架。
共识与结论
本评论得出结论,开发针对RNA病毒的广谱抗病毒药物药物对于克服当前抗病毒药物治疗药物的局限性至关重要,因为这些药物通常仅针对特定的病毒病原体。它强调了针对保守的病毒成分以实现广谱抗病毒药物活性的重要性,并指出需要持续投资于抗病毒药物药物发现,以满足当前和未来的医疗需求。
争议与缺口

Scope and question
The review focuses on the development of broader-spectrum direct-acting antivirals (BSAs) against RNA viruses, particularly emphasizing the need for such drugs to address current unmet medical needs and enhance pandemic preparedness. The central question is whether BSAs can be developed to target multiple RNA virus genera, families, or even cross families, and how this approach can be implemented effectively.
Evidence base and method
The review is a narrative synthesis of existing literature, with no explicit mention of systematic search methods or meta-analysis. It provides a non-exhaustive overview of approved and investigational antiviral agents active against RNA viruses, and discusses the biological determinants underlying their antiviral spectrum. The authors also outline the clinical development frameworks required for evaluating such drugs, particularly in outbreak contexts.
Consensus and conclusions
The review concludes that developing broader-spectrum antivirals is essential to address the limitations of current antiviral therapies, which are often limited to specific viral pathogens. It highlights the importance of targeting conserved viral components to achieve broad antiviral activity and emphasizes the need for sustained investment in antiviral drug discovery to meet both current and future medical needs.
Controversies and gaps
The review identifies a lack of antiviral drugs for many RNA viruses, particularly those causing life-threatening infections. It also notes that while advances in metagenomics and sequencing have expanded our understanding of viral diversity, the development of BSAs remains challenging due to the complexity of viral biology and the need for further research into conserved targets.
Research and practice implications
The review suggests that future research should focus on identifying conserved viral targets that can be exploited for the development of BSAs. It also highlights the need for clinical development frameworks that can rapidly evaluate these drugs during outbreaks, as well as the importance of continued investment in antiviral drug discovery to address the gaps in current therapeutic options.
