Lassa fever and Argentine hemorrhagic fever treatment in guinea pigs using broad-spectrum cap-dependent endonuclease inhibitors
作者： Junki Maruyama, Satoshi Taniguchi, Takeshi Saito, Shintaro Yamada, Rachel A. Reyna, Ruchi Paroha, Daiki Kobayashi, Gavriella Siman-Tov, Christine Click, Kirsten Littlefield
摘要中文翻译摘要 布尼亚病毒纲包括几种引起致命出血热的高致病性病毒。由于预防和 治疗 选项有限以及致病性高,这些病毒需要在生物安全 4 级设施中进行处理。在布尼亚病毒中,沙粒病毒 因其致病性和引起人类严重出血性疾病的能力而特别引人注目。帽子依赖性核酸内切酶(CEN)是参与这些病毒复制周期的独特且关键的酶。由于人类不具有类似的酶,CEN 是 抗病毒药物 药物开发的理想靶点,可降低副作用风险。最近,我们发现了一种有前途的 CEN 抑制剂 (CENi),它可以有效抑制病毒复制。在这篇手稿中,我们展示了 CENis 在致死性出血热豚鼠模型中对 拉沙热 和阿根廷出血热病毒感染的成功治疗效果。此外,我们还鉴定了几种对其他高致病性 沙粒病毒 具有 抗病毒药物 活性的 CENi。这些发现进一步支持了 CENis 作为治疗 沙粒病毒 感染的潜力,这种感染会导致严重且往往致命的出血热。总的来说,我们的结果表明,CENis 是泛 沙粒病毒 疗法的有希望的候选者,并且还可能对目前尚无批准的 抗病毒药物 治疗 存在的其他包含 CEN 的病毒具有更广泛的用途。重要性 沙粒病毒,例如拉沙病毒和胡宁病毒,会导致人类严重出血热,并与高 死亡率 发病率和有限的 死亡率 选项相关。由于其致病性和 暴发 的潜力,这些病毒构成了重要的全球健康威胁。病毒帽依赖性核酸内切酶 (CEN) 通过实现病毒 mRNA 合成所需的帽抢夺过程,在 沙粒病毒 复制中发挥着关键作用。值得注意的是,哺乳动物细胞缺乏类似的酶,这使得 CEN 成为 抗病毒药物 药物开发的有吸引力的目标。在这项研究中,我们证明针对 沙粒病毒 CEN 的抑制剂可有效抑制病毒复制,并在致命拉沙病毒和胡宁病毒感染的动物模型中提供治疗保护。我们还鉴定了对多种高致病性 沙粒病毒 具有 抗病毒药物 活性的化合物。这些发现表明,CEN 抑制是开发广谱 抗病毒药物 的一种有前景的策略,可对抗导致危及生命的出血热的 沙粒病毒。

Lassa fever and Argentine hemorrhagic fever treatment in guinea pigs using broad-spectrum cap-dependent endonuclease inhibitors

Authors: Junki Maruyama, Satoshi Taniguchi, Takeshi Saito, Shintaro Yamada, Rachel A. Reyna, Ruchi Paroha, Daiki Kobayashi, Gavriella Siman-Tov, Christine Click, Kirsten Littlefield
Original abstract (English)
ABSTRACT The class Bunyaviricetes encompasses several highly pathogenic viruses that cause lethal hemorrhagic fevers. Due to their limited prevention and treatment options and high pathogenicity, these viruses require handling in biosafety level-4 facilities. Within the bunyaviruses, arenaviruses are particularly notable for their pathogenicity and ability to cause severe hemorrhagic disease in humans. The cap-dependent endonuclease (CEN) is a unique and crucial enzyme involved in the replication cycle of these viruses. As humans do not possess a similar enzyme, CEN represents an ideal target for antiviral drug development with reduced risk of side effects. Recently, we identified a promising CEN inhibitor (CENi) demonstrating potent inhibition of virus replication. In this manuscript, we demonstrate the successful therapeutic efficacy of CENis against Lassa fever and Argentine hemorrhagic fever virus infections in guinea pig models of lethal hemorrhagic fever. In addition, we identified several CENis with antiviral activity against other highly pathogenic arenaviruses. These findings further support the potential of CENis as therapeutic agents for arenavirus infections that cause severe and often lethal hemorrhagic fever. Collectively, our results suggest that CENis are promising candidates for pan-arenavirus therapy and may also have broader utility against other CEN-containing viruses for which no approved antiviral treatments currently exist. IMPORTANCE Arenaviruses, such as Lassa virus and Junin virus, cause severe hemorrhagic fevers in humans and are associated with high mortality rates and limited treatment options. Because of their pathogenicity and potential for outbreaks, these viruses represent an important global health threat. The viral cap-dependent endonuclease (CEN) plays a critical role in arenavirus replication by enabling the cap-snatching process required for viral mRNA synthesis. Notably, mammalian cells lack a comparable enzyme, making CEN an attractive target for antiviral drug development. In this study, we demonstrate that inhibitors targeting the arenavirus CEN effectively suppress viral replication and provide therapeutic protection in animal models of lethal Lassa virus and Junin virus infections. We also identify compounds with antiviral activity against multiple highly pathogenic arenaviruses. These findings establish CEN inhibition as a promising strategy for the development of broad-spectrum antivirals against arenaviruses that cause life-threatening hemorrhagic fevers.

重组水泡性口炎病毒载体疫苗在不同基因组位置表达马丘波病毒糖蛋白的免疫原性

Immunogenicity of recombinant vesicular stomatitis virus-vectored vaccines expressing the Machupo virus glycoprotein in different genome positions
作者： Rachel Erickson, Paul Bates
摘要中文翻译摘要 重组水泡性口炎病毒 (rVSV) 是一个强大的 疫苗 平台,可引发持久的细胞和体液 免疫应答。rVSV 疫苗 已成功用于多种引起出血热的病毒。Here,we create and evaluate rVSV 疫苗 for Machupo virus (MACV),a highly pathogenic New World (NW) mammarenavirus that causes Bolivian hemorrhagic fever. VSV 基因组 重排影响病毒 媒介 和 免疫应答 至 疫苗 的衰减。因此,在本研究中,我们通过评估在 基因组 (rVSV-MACV1) 第一个位置和传统糖蛋白基因位置表达 MACV GPC 的 rVSV 的复制、抗原表达和免疫原性,直接比较改变 VSV 基因组 中 MACV 糖蛋白复合物 (GPC) 位置对体液和细胞 免疫应答 与 疫苗接种 的影响。(rVSV-MACV5). rVSV-MACV1 在受感染的细胞和病毒粒子中表达更高水平的 MACV GPC,但增加的抗原不会转化为更强大的 免疫应答。rVSV-MACV5 elicits higher levels of total IgG and neutralizing antibodies than rVSV-MACV1,but similar levels of MACV-specific T cells.为了进一步研究针对 MACV 和其他 NW 沙粒病毒 如 Junín 病毒 (JUNV) 的 疫苗 策略,我们评估了体液 免疫应答 与表达 MACV 和 JUNV GPC 的 rVSV 的顺序免疫。使用 rVSV-MACV5 初免和随后的 rVSV-JUNV 加强获得最高水平的 MACV 和 JUNV 反应性抗体。我们研究的数据对 rVSV 疫苗 设计有影响,并为开发用于 MACV 和其他 NW 沙粒病毒 的 rVSV 矢量 疫苗 奠定了基础。重要性 马丘波病毒 (MACV) 是一种高致病性新世界 (NW) 乳房病毒,可引起玻利维亚出血热 (BHF)。由于 死亡率 比率较高,MACV 和其他西北地区的 死亡率 构成了重大的公共卫生威胁。然而,尚无批准用于 MACV 的 疫苗。在这里,我们描述了重组水泡性口炎病毒(rVSV)疫苗,其在VSV 基因组的不同位置表达MACV糖蛋白复合物(GPC),并表征了每种病毒的体液和细胞免疫应答。我们还研究了表达 MACV GPC 和相关 NW 沙粒病毒抗原 Junín 病毒 GPC 的 rVSV 的序贯免疫,以引发广泛的交叉反应抗体反应。我们的研究结论有两个:这里提供的数据提供了对 rVSV 疫苗 媒介 以及如何利用 基因组 重排进行 疫苗 开发的进一步理解,并且我们的研究结果可能会为 MACV 和其他 NW 沙粒病毒 的未来 疫苗 开发提供信息。

问题与背景
马丘波病毒 (MACV) 会导致玻利维亚出血热,且 死亡率 值较高,但尚无经批准的 疫苗,因此公共卫生部门迫切需要采取有效的对策。该研究解决了重组水泡性口炎病毒 (rVSV) 媒介 中 MACV 糖蛋白复合物 (GPC) 的 基因组 位置如何影响免疫原性的知识空白。
设计与对象
该研究设计了在两个 基因组 位置表达 MACV GPC 的 rVSV 媒介,并使用序贯免疫策略将它们与相关的新世界 沙粒病毒、胡宁病毒 (JUNV) 进行比较。对 疫苗 候选物的复制、抗原表达和 免疫应答 进行评估。
核心方法
rVSV-MACV1和rVSV-MACV5 媒介分别在第一个或第五个基因组位置用MACV GPC构建,并对感染细胞和病毒体中的抗原表达进行定量。在 疫苗接种 后测量体液和细胞 免疫应答,并测试用 rVSV-MACV5 初免和随后的 rVSV-JUNV 加强的顺序免疫。
主要结果
rVSV-MACV1 在细胞和病毒颗粒中表达的 MACV GPC 水平高于 rVSV-MACV5,但 rVSV-MACV5 引发了更高的总 IgG 和针对 MACV 的中和抗体。使用 rVSV-MACV5 初免和 rVSV-JUNV 加强的顺序免疫实现了最高水平的 MACV 和 JUNV 反应性抗体。
解释与创新
研究结果表明,较高的抗原表达并不一定与较强的免疫应答相关,并且rVSV 媒介中的基因组位置可以调节免疫原性。该研究提供了关于序贯免疫策略的新证据,以扩大针对 New World 沙粒病毒 的交叉反应抗体反应。
科研与公卫意义
这些结果通过识别 基因组 位置对免疫原性的影响并支持序贯免疫作为增强交叉反应性抗体应答的策略,为 MACV 和相关 New World 沙粒病毒 的 rVSV 疫苗 设计提供信息。
局限与证据强度
该研究仅限于受控实验环境中的体外抗原表达和 免疫应答 测量,其研究结果需要在动物模型和临床试验中进行验证,以评估保护功效和安全性。

Immunogenicity of recombinant vesicular stomatitis virus-vectored vaccines expressing the Machupo virus glycoprotein in different genome positions

Authors: Rachel Erickson, Paul Bates
Original abstract (English)
ABSTRACT Recombinant vesicular stomatitis virus (rVSV) is a powerful vaccine platform that elicits durable cellular and humoral immune responses. rVSV vaccines have successfully been used for various hemorrhagic fever-causing viruses. Here, we create and evaluate rVSV vaccines for Machupo virus (MACV), a highly pathogenic New World (NW) mammarenavirus that causes Bolivian hemorrhagic fever. VSV genome rearrangements impact attenuation of the viral vector and the immune response to the vaccine. Therefore, in this study, we directly compare the effect of altering the MACV glycoprotein complex (GPC) position in the VSV genome on humoral and cellular immune responses to vaccination by evaluating replication, antigen expression, and immunogenicity of rVSVs expressing MACV GPC in the first position of the genome (rVSV-MACV1) and in the traditional glycoprotein gene position (rVSV-MACV5). rVSV-MACV1 expresses higher levels of MACV GPC in infected cells and in virions, but the increased antigen does not translate to a more robust immune response. rVSV-MACV5 elicits higher levels of total IgG and neutralizing antibodies than rVSV-MACV1, but similar levels of MACV-specific T cells. To further investigate vaccine strategies for MACV and other NW arenaviruses such as Junín virus (JUNV), we evaluated the humoral immune response to sequential immunization with rVSVs expressing MACV and JUNV GPCs. The highest levels of MACV and JUNV reactive antibodies were achieved using an rVSV-MACV5 prime followed by rVSV-JUNV boost. The data from our study have implications for rVSV vaccine design and provide a foundation for the development of rVSV-vectored vaccines for MACV and other NW arenaviruses. IMPORTANCE Machupo virus (MACV) is a highly pathogenic New World (NW) mammarenavirus that causes Bolivian hemorrhagic fever (BHF). Because of their high mortality rates, MACV and other NW arenaviruses represent a significant public health threat. However, no approved vaccines for MACV exist. Here, we describe recombinant vesicular stomatitis virus (rVSV) vaccines that express the MACV glycoprotein complex (GPC) in different positions of the VSV genome and characterize the humoral and cellular immune response to each. We also investigate sequential immunization with rVSVs expressing MACV GPC and a related NW arenaviral antigen, Junín virus GPC, in an effort to elicit broadly cross-reactive antibody responses. The conclusions from our study are twofold: data presented here provide further understanding of rVSV vaccine vectors and how genome rearrangements can be leveraged for vaccine development, and the results from our study may inform future vaccine development for MACV and other NW arenaviruses.

Question and background
Machupo virus (MACV) causes Bolivian hemorrhagic fever with high mortality and no approved vaccines exist, creating a critical public-health need for effective countermeasures. The study addresses a knowledge gap on how genome position of the MACV glycoprotein complex (GPC) in recombinant vesicular stomatitis virus (rVSV) vectors influences immunogenicity.
Design and population
The study engineered rVSV vectors expressing MACV GPC in two genome positions and compared them to a related New World arenavirus, Junín virus (JUNV), using sequential immunization strategies. Vaccine candidates were evaluated for replication, antigen expression, and immune responses.
Core methods
rVSV-MACV1 and rVSV-MACV5 vectors were constructed with MACV GPC in the first or fifth genome position, respectively, and antigen expression was quantified in infected cells and virions. Humoral and cellular immune responses were measured after vaccination, and sequential immunization with rVSV-MACV5 prime followed by rVSV-JUNV boost was tested.
Main results
rVSV-MACV1 expressed higher MACV GPC levels in cells and virions than rVSV-MACV5, yet rVSV-MACV5 elicited higher total IgG and neutralizing antibodies against MACV. Sequential immunization with rVSV-MACV5 prime and rVSV-JUNV boost achieved the highest levels of MACV- and JUNV-reactive antibodies.
Interpretation and novelty
The findings indicate that higher antigen expression does not necessarily correlate with stronger immune responses, and genome position in rVSV vectors can modulate immunogenicity. The study provides new evidence on sequential immunization strategies to broaden cross-reactive antibody responses against New World arenaviruses.
Scientific and public-health significance
These results inform rVSV vaccine design for MACV and related New World arenaviruses by identifying genome position effects on immunogenicity and supporting sequential immunization as a strategy to enhance cross-reactive antibody responses.
Limitations and evidence strength
The study is limited to in vitro antigen expression and immune response measurements in a controlled experimental setting, and its findings require validation in animal models and clinical trials to assess protective efficacy and safety.

Concurrent Lassa fever outbreaks in Kaduna and Kano States, Nigeria: Lessons in Health Worker Safety and Emergency Response

Authors: Amina Jummai Shehu, Mayana Abubakar, Mohammed Abede, Umar Ibrahim, Isiaq Hadji Shehu, Jeremiah Daikwo, Abdullahi Musa Garba, Yetunde Abioye, Sulaiman Iliyasu Hamisu, Abdulwahab Kabir Sulaiman
Original abstract (English)
Introduction: Lassa fever remains a major public health threat in Nigeria, with recurrent seasonal outbreaks causing significant morbidity and mortality. In 2025, concurrent outbreaks occurred in Kaduna and Kano States, prompting coordinated surveillance and emergency response efforts. This study documents the epidemiology of these outbreaks and highlights lessons in outbreak detection, healthcare worker safety, and response coordination. Methods: A retrospective descriptive study was conducted using surveillance, laboratory, and case management data from the NCDC-supported surveillance system in Kaduna State and the Kano State Centre for Disease Control (KNCDC), with technical support from the World Health Organization (WHO) in Kano State. Laboratory confirmation was performed using real-time reverse transcriptase polymerase chain reaction (RT-qPCR). Kaduna’s outbreak occurred from epidemiological weeks 1–28 (January–July 2025), while Kano’s outbreak occurred in epidemiological week 15 (April 2025). All suspected cases meeting the national case definition were included. Data sources included SORMAS, situation reports, laboratory records, and health facility data. Descriptive and comparative analyses were performed using Microsoft Excel 365. Results: Kaduna reported 54 suspected cases, 8 confirmed cases, and 5 deaths among confirmed cases (CFR 62.5%), including 2 healthcare worker infections. Kano reported 7 suspected cases, 2 confirmed cases, and 2 deaths. One death occurred among confirmed cases (CFR: 50.0%, 1/2), while the second occurred among a suspected case (CFR: 28.6%, 2/7). Using confirmed cases as the denominator, Kaduna had a higher CFR than Kano (62.5%, 5/8 vs. Kaduna’s outbreak was prolonged, while Kano’s outbreak was rapidly contained. Both states achieved complete contact tracing. Conclusion: Differences in outbreak outcomes highlight the importance of timely detection, strong infection prevention and control (IPC), healthcare worker protection, coordinated response and effective surveillance systems for preventing wider transmission.

Health Five States Account for 86% of Nigeria’s 1,000 Lassa Fever Cases BrandIconImage 2 days ago Helen Aremu Bauchi, Ondo, Taraba, Benue and Edo remain the country’s major hotspots as death toll rises to 237 Five states have accounted for 86 per cent of Nigeria’s 1,000 confirmed Lassa fever cases recorded so far in 2026, with Bauchi, Ondo, Taraba, Benue and Edo continuing to bear the heaviest burden of the disease. The latest figures from the Nigeria Centre for Disease Control and Prevention (NCDC) show that Lassa fever has been reported across 23 states and 116 local government areas since the beginning of the year. According to the agency’s Lassa Fever Situation Report for epidemiological Week 30, covering July 20 to 26, Nigeria recorded 17 new confirmed infections during the week, pushing the cumulative number of cases to 1,000. The outbreak has also claimed 237 lives, translating to a case fatality rate of 23.7 per cent. Although the number of new infections recorded in Week 30 declined compared with the previous week, when 20 cases were confirmed, the overall disease burden continued to rise. The cumulative number of confirmed cases increased from 983 in Week 29 to 1,000 in Week 30, while the death toll rose from 231 to 237. The case fatality rate also edged upwards from 23.5 per cent to 23.7 per cent. Four states record fresh infections The NCDC report showed that the 17 new cases recorded during the latest reporting week came from four states — Ondo, Bauchi, Edo and Benue. The continued emergence of new infections in states that already account for a significant proportion of the national caseload highlights the persistent transmission of Lassa fever in some parts of the country. Bauchi, Ondo, Taraba, Benue and Edo have consistently featured among the states most affected by the disease, accounting collectively for the vast majority of confirmed infections recorded in 2026. The concentration of cases in the five states also underscores the uneven geographical distribution of Lassa fever in Nigeria and places increased pressure on health authorities in affected areas to maintain surveillance and strengthen early detection. For the states at the centre of the outbreak, sustained access to diagnostic services, effective case management and infection-prevention measures remains critical to limiting further transmission and reducing deaths. Young adults among most affected The latest epidemiological data also indicate that Lassa fever continues to affect a predominantly young adult population. People between the ages of 21 and 30 years were identified as the most affected age group, pointing to a significant burden among Nigerians in their economically active years. The pattern reinforces the need for continued public awareness and preventive measures, particularly in communities where the disease is endemic. Healthcare workers face occupational risk The outbreak is also posing a continuing threat to healthcare workers involved in the treatment of infected patients. The NCDC reported one new Lassa fever infection among healthcare workers during Week 30, compared with no new healthcare worker infection in the preceding week. The development highlights the occupational risks faced by doctors, nurses, laboratory personnel, caregivers and other health workers who may come into contact with patients whose infections have not yet been diagnosed. Because Lassa fever can be transmitted through contact with the blood and other body fluids of infected people, strict infection-prevention and control measures remain essential in healthcare settings. Early recognition and diagnosis are particularly important because patients may initially present with symptoms that resemble other common illnesses, potentially increasing the risk of delayed treatment and exposure among healthcare personnel. How Lassa fever spreads Lassa fever is an acute viral illness that is endemic in parts of West Africa, including Nigeria. The disease remains a recurring public-health challenge, particularly during periods when transmission increases in affected communities. The primary route of transmission to humans is through contact with food or household items contaminated by the urine or faeces of infected multimammate rats, which are the principal reservoir of the virus. Human-to-human transmission can also occur, particularly through direct contact with the blood or body fluids of infected individuals. This makes healthcare facilities a critical point for infection prevention, especially where patients present with symptoms that could be associated with Lassa fever. Surveillance remains critical With confirmed infections now reaching 1,000 in 2026, the latest figures highlight the importance of sustained surveillance across affected and neighbouring states. While the decline in weekly cases from 20 to 17 may offer some indication of a reduction in new infections during the reporting period, the rise in cumulative cases and deaths shows that the outbreak remains a significant public-health concern. Health authorities will therefore need to maintain vigilance, particularly in the five states responsible for the overwhelming share of reported infections, while strengthening early detection, laboratory confirmation, treatment, contact tracing and infection-prevention measures. The continued infection of healthcare workers also serves as a reminder that controlling Lassa fever requires not only community-level prevention but also robust protection for those on the frontline of the response.
