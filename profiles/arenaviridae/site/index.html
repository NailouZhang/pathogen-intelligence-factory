尼洛碱作为抗拉沙病毒糖蛋白和核蛋白的潜在多靶点结合剂的计算评估

Computational evaluation of Niloticin as a potential multi-target binder against Lassa virus glycoprotein and nucleoprotein
作者： Mudassar Mehmood Baig, Stalin Fathima Merlin, Xiao-Rui Kang, Quan Zou, Ximei Luo, Antony Stalin
摘要中文翻译拉沙热 是一种潜在致命疾病,死亡率 发病率很高,目前缺乏有效的 治疗,构成重大威胁,特别是在西非。在这项研究中,尼罗丁(一种天然存在的原柠檬苦素三萜类化合物)通过网络药理学、分子对接、分子动力学模拟、氢键占据分析和 MM/PBSA 结合能估计,被计算评估为对抗拉沙病毒相关 宿主 和病毒靶点的潜在多靶点结合剂。使用 GeneCards、OMIM 和 SwissTargetPrediction 数据库确定了八个重叠的候选 宿主 相关靶点,即 F10、ABL1、AKT1、AR、BCHE、EGFR、KIT 和 NLRP3。功能富集分析表明可能与细胞凋亡、免疫调节、炎症、凝血和细胞信号通路有关。分子对接预测尼罗霉素与 LASV 糖蛋白区域 GP2 和 SSP 的良好结合,对接分数分别为 - 6.85 和 - 7.17 kcal/mol,以及与核蛋白 dTTP 结合口袋的良好结合,对接分数为 - 7.36 kcal/mol。尼洛霉素与 GP2、SSP 和 NP 中的关键残基形成预测的氢键和疏水相互作用,包括 GP2 中的 ARG422 和 PHE434、SSP 中的 ALA25 和 VAL18 以及 NP 结合口袋周围的残基。在 MD 模拟过程中,尼罗霉素结合系统表现出相对稳定的轨迹行为,并得到 RMSD、RMSF、回转半径、SASA 和氢键分析的支持。氢键占据分析进一步表明重复的配体-蛋白质接触涉及GP2中的ARG422、SSP中的VAL18和ALA25以及NP中的THR178、GLU299、ARG323和LYS253。MM/PBSA 分析显示,尼罗霉素的相对结合能估计值良好,糖蛋白相关系统的相对结合能约为 − 84 至 − 100 kJ/mol,NP 的相对结合能约为 − 62 kJ/mol,主要由范德华和非极性溶剂化作用支持。总体而言,这些计算结果表明尼罗霉素可能会参与多个 LASV 相关病毒和 宿主 靶标,并且可能会优先进行进一步的实验研究。然而,结果完全基于计算分析,并没有证实 抗病毒药物 抑制、直接 宿主 靶标调节或 LASV 进入或复制的功能阻断。需要通过细胞毒性测定、生化结合研究、假病毒进入测定和感染性病毒 抗病毒药物 测定进行实验验证,以确定尼洛霉素的抗 LASV 潜力。

Computational evaluation of Niloticin as a potential multi-target binder against Lassa virus glycoprotein and nucleoprotein

Authors: Mudassar Mehmood Baig, Stalin Fathima Merlin, Xiao-Rui Kang, Quan Zou, Ximei Luo, Antony Stalin
Original abstract (English)
Lassa fever is a potentially fatal disease with a high mortality rate and currently lacks an effective treatment, posing a significant threat, particularly in West Africa. In this study, Niloticin, a naturally occurring protolimonoid triterpenoid, was computationally evaluated as a potential multi-target binder against Lassa virus–associated host and viral targets using network pharmacology, molecular docking, molecular dynamics simulation, hydrogen-bond occupancy analysis, and MM/PBSA binding-energy estimation. Eight overlapping candidate host-associated targets, namely F10, ABL1, AKT1, AR, BCHE, EGFR, KIT, and NLRP3, were identified using the GeneCards, OMIM, and SwissTargetPrediction databases. Functional enrichment analysis suggested possible associations with apoptosis, immune regulation, inflammation, coagulation, and cell-signaling pathways. Molecular docking predicted favorable binding of Niloticin to LASV glycoprotein regions GP2 and SSP, with docking scores of − 6.85 and − 7.17 kcal/mol, respectively, and to the nucleoprotein dTTP-binding pocket with a docking score of − 7.36 kcal/mol. Niloticin formed predicted hydrogen-bonding and hydrophobic interactions with key residues in GP2, SSP, and NP, including ARG422 and PHE434 in GP2, ALA25 and VAL18 in SSP, and residues surrounding the NP binding pocket. During MD simulations, Niloticin-bound systems exhibited comparatively stable trajectory behavior, as supported by RMSD, RMSF, radius of gyration, SASA, and hydrogen-bond analyses. Hydrogen-bond occupancy analysis further indicated recurrent ligand–protein contacts involving ARG422 in GP2, VAL18 and ALA25 in SSP, and THR178, GLU299, ARG323, and LYS253 in NP. MM/PBSA analysis showed favorable relative binding-energy estimates for Niloticin, ranging from approximately − 84 to − 100 kJ/mol for glycoprotein-associated systems and approximately − 62 kJ/mol for NP, mainly supported by van der Waals and nonpolar solvation contributions. Overall, these computational findings suggest that Niloticin may engage multiple LASV-associated viral and host targets and may be prioritized for further experimental investigation. However, the results are based entirely on computational analyses and do not confirm antiviral inhibition, direct host-target modulation, or functional blockade of LASV entry or replication. Experimental validation through cytotoxicity assays, biochemical binding studies, pseudovirus entry assays, and infectious-virus antiviral assays is required to establish the anti-LASV potential of Niloticin.

2024年12月至2025年2月尼日利亚埃博尼州确诊的拉沙热病例的临床和流行病学特征

Clinical and epidemiological characteristics of confirmed Lassa fever cases between December 2024 and February 2025 in Ebonyi State, Nigeria
作者： Gordon Igbodo, Nneka Chika-Igwenyi, Oluwole Mathew Temitope, Umahi Chukwu, Nnennaya Ajayi, Nsikan Primus Okon, Ogbonna Nwambeke, Ehiakhamen Odianosen, Olukemi Titilope Olugbade, Elizabeth Adedire
摘要中文翻译简介:拉沙热 (LF) 是西非国家流行的一种急性病毒性出血性疾病。该疾病仍然是尼日利亚的一个主要公共卫生问题,也是每年全球报告病例最多的疾病。尽管 监测 和案例管理有所改进,但 死亡率 仍然很高。埃博尼州经历了反复出现的 暴发,但指导早期检测和临床反应的病例数据有限。方法:对 2024 年 12 月至 2025 年 2 月期间在埃博尼州指定 LF 治疗 中心收治的实验室确诊 LF 病例进行回顾性病例系列。数据来自医院记录、尼日利亚疾病控制和预防中心 (NCDC) 病例调查表和州 监测 线路清单。这些变量包括人口统计学、临床和结果特征。使用中位数和比例总结描述性统计数据。结果:多个地方政府管辖区发现了 10 例实验室确诊病例。中位年龄为 25.5 岁(四分位距:18.8 – 29.0),60% 为男性。最常见的症状是发烧、头痛和全身无力。在怀疑 LF 之前,十名患者中有五名 (50%) 最初接受了疟疾或伤寒治疗。在致命病例中更常见的是严重并发症,特别是出血和 AKI。总体病死率为 40%,所有死亡均发生在症状出现 ≥5 天后的患者中(出现症状的中位时间:7.5 天[死亡]与 7.0 天[康复])。结论:在 2024-2025 年 LF 传播 季节期间,埃博尼州的死亡病例中,延迟就诊、初始误诊和严重并发症的发生率更高。早期临床怀疑、及时转诊以及及时开始利巴韦林和支持治疗可能对提高生存率很重要,这与西非类似 拉沙热 治疗 环境中的证据一致。加强临床医生意识、诊断能力和社区就医行为对于减少流行地区 LF 相关的 死亡率 仍然至关重要。

Clinical and epidemiological characteristics of confirmed Lassa fever cases between December 2024 and February 2025 in Ebonyi State, Nigeria

Authors: Gordon Igbodo, Nneka Chika-Igwenyi, Oluwole Mathew Temitope, Umahi Chukwu, Nnennaya Ajayi, Nsikan Primus Okon, Ogbonna Nwambeke, Ehiakhamen Odianosen, Olukemi Titilope Olugbade, Elizabeth Adedire
Original abstract (English)
Introduction: Lassa Fever (LF) is an acute viral haemorrhagic illness endemic to West African countries. The disease remains a major public health concern in Nigeria and accounts for most reported global cases annually. Despite improvements in surveillance and case management, mortality remains high. Ebonyi State experiences recurrent outbreaks, yet limited case-level data exist to guide early detection and clinical response. Methods: A retrospective case series was conducted of laboratory-confirmed LF cases admitted at the designated LF treatment centre in Ebonyi State between December 2024 and February 2025. Data were extracted from hospital records, the Nigeria Centre for Disease Control and Prevention (NCDC) case investigation forms, and state surveillance line lists. The variables included demographic, clinical, and outcome characteristics. Descriptive statistics were summarised using medians and proportions. Results: Ten laboratory-confirmed cases were identified across multiple LGAs. The median age was 25.5 years (interquartile range: 18.8 – 29.0), and 60% were male. The most common presenting symptoms were fever, headache, and generalised weakness. Five of ten patients (50%) were initially treated for malaria or typhoid before LF was suspected. Severe complications, particularly bleeding and AKI, were observed more frequently among fatal cases. The overall case fatality rate was 40%, with all deaths occurring among patients presenting ≥5 days after symptom onset (median time to presentation: 7.5 days [fatal] versus 7.0 days [recovered]). Conclusion: Delayed presentation, initial misdiagnosis and severe complications were observed more frequently among fatal cases during the 2024–2025 LF transmission season in Ebonyi State. Early clinical suspicion, prompt referral, and timely initiation of Ribavirin and supportive care are likely important for improving survival, consistent with evidence from similar Lassa fever treatment settings in West Africa. Strengthening clinician awareness, diagnostic capacity, and community health-seeking behaviour remains essential to reducing LF-related mortality in endemic regions.

Question and background
This study aimed to investigate the temporal dynamics, climate associations, and surveillance performance of Lassa fever in Nigeria from 2020 to 2025, while assessing how the coronavirus disease 2019 (COVID-19) pandemic affected these metrics.

Authors: Olanrewaju Olaiya, Kayode Akanbi, Kwarbai Aaron Maitala, Adamu Ishaku Akyala, Islamiyat Adekemi Olatinwo, Abah Michael Idoko, Imaobong Udo Isang, Olasunkanmi Samson Coker
Original abstract (English)
Lassa fever is a viral haemorrhagic fever endemic to Nigeria that exacts a heavy toll of morbidity and mortality annually. While Nigeria experiences annual outbreaks of the disease, household-level risk factors for transmission in areas of both high and low disease burden have not been fully described. The objective of this study was to evaluate and compare household risk factors for Lassa fever transmission in two high-burden (Ondo and Edo) and two low-burden (Bauchi and Ebonyi) states in Nigeria. Four states (sample size determined by quota sampling) were purposively selected and each allocated 150 consenting adult respondents yielding an overall sample size of 600 respondents. Multistage stratified random sampling technique was employed. Data on fourteen variables were collected using a pre-tested structured questionnaire. The variables include; household history of LF; frequency of rodents per sighting; food storing habits; prevention practices against rodents; awareness about LF; accessibility to healthcare facilities; respondents who had ever attended health training; ever diagnosed with LF in the last five years; severity of symptoms; disease outcome; age distribution; respondents’ educational status; household hygiene level and hand washing practices among others. Responses for each independent variable were analysed using descriptive statistics and the results were presented based on states with high LF burden versus states with low LF burden. Among the respondents, 35.3% reported previous history of Lassa fever case in the household and 32.7% were not sure if they had a prior history of Lassa fever case. 42.0 percent of respondents reported seeing rodents daily or weekly in and around their households. Only 35.0% of households stored food in sealed containers. 36.7 percent practiced rodent preventive measures. 68.7 % reported that they had heard of Lassa fever prior to survey completion. Overall, there seemed to be gap between awareness of LF and taking preventative measures. 53.3 percent of participants reported challenges accessing health care. 53.3 percent of respondents reported that they had never participated in any health training about Lassa fever. 33.3% of the respondents have been diagnosed with Lassa fever in the past five years. Ten percent of respondents have had someone in their home die from Lassa fever. 46.7 % of respondents self-reported their hygiene practices as being either fair or poor. 30.0 percent of respondents stated that they always washed their hands. Those who responded from high burden states reported higher frequencies of rodent sightings, less frequent use of sealed storage containers, and more challenges with healthcare access when compared to respondents from low burden states. Persistent rodent exposure, unsafe food storage, low adoption of preventive measures, healthcare inaccessibility, and suboptimal hygiene are widespread across all four states and are more pronounced in high-burden states. A significant awareness-to-action gap indicates that knowledge alone is insufficient to change behaviour. Integrated community-level interventions addressing rodent control, food storage, health education outreach, and primary healthcare strengthening are urgently required and should be embedded within a One Health framework.

Original abstract (English)
Background: Survival from Lassa fever may be followed by persistent physical symptoms, sensory difficulties, psychological distress, social disruption, and stigma. This study explored how survivors described the illness trajectory and life after treatment in Southern Nigeria. Methods: A qualitative phenomenological design was used. 尼日利亚国家快速反应小组 (NRRT) 对 2024 年 12 月至 2025 年 2 月期间贡贝州 LF 病例进行了一项描述性横断面研究,研究范围为 2025 年 2 月 12 日至 26 日,覆盖九个 (82%) 地方政府地区 (LGA)。数据按人物、地点和时间进行描述性分析。响应活动包括主动病例搜索、回顾性记录审查、使用 监测 暴发 响应管理和分析系统 (SORMAS) 协调 监测 数据、IPC 评估、环境风险评估、社区参与和州际协调。
共识与结论
暴发 期间发现 39 例疑似病例、11 例确诊病例、1 例疑似病例和 6 例死亡。与塔拉巴州和包奇州有关的输入病例对 暴发 贡献很大。监测 数据协调改进了 SORMAS 中 暴发 记录、医疗机构记录、实验室记录和州行列表的协调性。贡贝 暴发 证明了传统上不被视为 LF 热点的州对州际进口和本地化 传播 的脆弱性。
争议与缺口
IPC 评估发现了分诊系统、个人防护装备使用、隔离能力和标准预防措施遵守方面的差距。在响应期的后期阶段,没有发现其他确诊病例或医护人员感染。
科研与实践启示
州际 监测 协调、数据统一、强化的 IPC 措施以及多部门响应活动支持了 暴发 的检测和响应工作。加强这些系统可以改善对未来 暴发 的准备和响应。

Strengthening interstate surveillance and data sharing: Lessons from Gombe State’s Lassa fever outbreak response, Nigeria

Authors: Ebelechukwu Chinwe Metuh, William Enyeribe Nwachukwu, Rejoice Kudirat Luka-Lawal, Yetunde Abioye, Bala Buratai, Stephen Adeshina Ohuneni, Chinedu Okoroafor, Bright Friday Onwe, Chukwuemeka Nnamdi Okeh, Sulaiman Abubakar
Original abstract (English)
Introduction: Lassa fever (LF) endemicity in West Africa poses amplified risks due to population mobility and health system gaps. A LF outbreak in Gombe State, Nigeria (December 2024–February 2025), a non-hotspot state, exposed gaps in regional health security, interstate surveillance and coordination. The index case (a traveller from Taraba State) highlighted interstate border transmission risks. This study reviews Gombe State’s 2024/2025 LF outbreak data to strengthen LF outbreak control, enhance future outbreak preparedness and improve interstate collaboration. Methods: Nigeria’s National Rapid Response Team (NRRT) conducted a descriptive cross-sectional study of Gombe State LF cases between December 2024 and February 2025 across nine (82%) of the Local Government Areas (LGAs) from 12th to 26th February 2025. Response activities included active case search, retrospective record reviews, harmonisation of surveillance data using the Surveillance Outbreak Response Management and Analysis System (SORMAS), IPC assessments, environmental risk assessment, community engagement, and interstate coordination. Data were analysed descriptively by person, place, and time. Results: Thirty-nine suspected cases, 11 confirmed cases, one probable case, and six deaths were identified during the outbreak period. Imported cases linked to Taraba and Bauchi States contributed substantially to the outbreak. Surveillance data reconciliation improved harmonization of outbreak records across SORMAS, health facility records, laboratory records, and state line lists. IPC assessments identified gaps in triage systems, PPE utilisation, isolation capacity, and adherence to standard precautions. No additional confirmed case or healthcare worker infection was identified during the later phase of the response period. Conclusion: The Gombe outbreak demonstrates the vulnerability of states not traditionally considered LF hotspots to interstate importation and localised transmission. Interstate surveillance coordination, data harmonization, strengthened IPC measures, and multisectoral response activities supported outbreak detection and response efforts. Strengthening these systems may improve preparedness and response to future outbreaks.

News Brief
The World Health Organization (WHO) Regional Office for Africa, in collaboration with the Mano River Union Secretariat and the Ministry of Health and Sanitation in Sierra Leone, conducted a four-day regional conference on Lassa fever and other infectious diseases from 9-12 August 2011. The conference aimed to develop a strategic plan for the prevention and control of Lassa fever and other emerging infectious diseases in the West African Region. The event was reported in a WHO communiqué, highlighting the importance of regional cooperation in addressing public health threats. While the conference itself was a planned and confirmed activity, no specific case counts, death counts, or exposure numbers were provided in the evidence. The conference was opened by Hon Minister of Health and Sanitation, Hon Zainab Bangura, and included representatives from seven West African countries, WHO offices, and technical health partners. The event is not related to any ongoing outbreak or risk assessment, as it is a strategic planning initiative.
