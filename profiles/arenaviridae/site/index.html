预测Lassa病毒糖蛋白突变影响病毒进入和抗体逃逸

Predicting Lassa Virus Glycoprotein Mutations Affecting Viral Entry and Antibody Escape
作者： Oluremi Israel Ajayi, Blessing Oluwatobi Olorunfemi, Jolly Amoche Adole, Amoge Chidinma Ogu, Ifeoluwa O. Bejide, Blossom Akinnawo, Christian T. Happi, Chinedu A. Ugwu
摘要中文翻译摘要 拉沙病毒 (LASV) 是西非病毒性出血热的主要原因,由于其广泛的遗传多样性以及缺乏广泛有效的 疫苗 和基于抗体的治疗方法,拉沙病毒仍然是一个重大的公共卫生挑战。病毒糖蛋白复合物 (GPC) 内的 突变 是中和抗体的主要靶标,可以改变病毒进入和对抗体介导的中和的敏感性。这项研究开发了一个集成的机器学习框架,以预测 LASV GPC 中经实验验证的氨基酸取代对病毒进入能力和抗体逃逸的影响。将已发表的假病毒进入和中和研究的突变数据映射到参考 GPC 序列,并在代表性 LASV 谱系中进行分析。将病毒进入建模为二元分类任务,而使用基于 突变 位置、野生型​​残基和取代氨基酸的抗体特异性回归模型来预测抗体逃逸。基于树的集成方法优于线性和单树算法,极端梯度提升 (XGBoost) 实现了病毒进入的最高预测性能 (ROC-AUC = 0.871)。在世系 I-III 中,有进入能力的 突变 和进入能力较差的 突变 的出现比例几乎相等(分别约为 53% 和 47%),而 3,923 个 突变 在所有三个谱系中均得到保留。抗体逃逸分析揭示了表位特异性差异:25.10C 显示出最广泛的预测逃逸图谱,而 37.2D 和 37.7H 显示出有限的逃逸图谱。12.1F、25.6A 和 8.9F 观察到中间逃逸模式。该框架提供了一种实用方法,用于优先考虑功能重要的 突变、识别具有有限逃逸潜力的保守表位,并支持广泛保护性 疫苗、治疗性抗体组合和 基因组监测 策略的合理设计。

Predicting Lassa Virus Glycoprotein Mutations Affecting Viral Entry and Antibody Escape

Authors: Oluremi Israel Ajayi, Blessing Oluwatobi Olorunfemi, Jolly Amoche Adole, Amoge Chidinma Ogu, Ifeoluwa O. Bejide, Blossom Akinnawo, Christian T. Happi, Chinedu A. Ugwu
Original abstract (English)
Abstract Lassa virus (LASV) is a major cause of viral haemorrhagic fever in West Africa and remains a significant public health challenge because of its extensive genetic diversity and lack of broadly effective vaccines and antibody-based therapeutics. Mutations within the viral glycoprotein complex (GPC), the principal target of neutralizing antibodies, can alter viral entry and susceptibility to antibody-mediated neutralization. This study developed an integrated machine learning framework to predict the effects of experimentally validated amino acid substitutions within LASV GPC on viral entry competence and antibody escape. Mutational data from published pseudovirus entry and neutralization studies were mapped to a reference GPC sequence and analysed across representative LASV lineages. Viral entry was modelled as a binary classification task, while antibody escape was predicted using antibody-specific regression models based on mutation position, wild-type residue, and substituted amino acid. Tree-based ensemble methods outperformed linear and single-tree algorithms, with Extreme Gradient Boosting (XGBoost) achieving the highest predictive performance for viral entry (ROC-AUC = 0.871). Across Lineages I–III, entry-competent and poor-entry mutations occurred in near-equal proportions (approximately 53% and 47%, respectively), while 3,923 mutations were conserved across all three lineages. Antibody escape analyses revealed epitope-specific differences: 25.10C exhibited the broadest predicted escape landscape, whereas 37.2D and 37.7H displayed restricted escape profiles. Intermediate escape patterns were observed for 12.1F, 25.6A, and 8.9F. The framework provides a practical approach for prioritizing functionally important mutations, identifying conserved epitopes with limited escape potential, and supporting rational design of broadly protective vaccines, therapeutic antibody combinations, and genomic surveillance strategies.

Nosocomial Outbreak of Lassa Fever in Conakry, Guinea, 2022.

Authors: Giuditta Annibaldis, Barré Soropogui, Kékoura Ifono, Jacob Camara, Mohamed Lamine Kaba, Fanta Berete, Sarah Ryter, Moussa Conde, Saa Lucien Millimono, Mette Hinrichs
Original abstract (English)
BACKGROUND: Lassa fever is endemic in Guinea, with high seroprevalence in the forest region. However, clinical cases have been only anecdotally reported. In August 2022, a nosocomial outbreak occurred at a private clinic in the capital, Conakry, an area previously considered low risk. METHODS: Suspected cases were confirmed by real-time reverse-transcription polymerase chain reaction within 24 hours. Viremia was monitored during hospitalization, and whole-genome sequencing was performed in-country within 13 days of outbreak detection. Outbreak investigation involved rodent testing in the home village of the suspected primary case. RESULTS: Six cases were laboratory-confirmed, 5 of which were healthcare workers of the clinic. The case fatality rate was 16.7%. Viral RNA remained detectable in blood of survivors for a median of 26 days (interquartile range, 24-41 days) post-disease onset. Epidemiological investigations identified a suspected primary case, who had died of a febrile disease compatible with Lassa fever, had contact with all secondary cases, and had a travel history from Kissidougou area. Three near-complete and 1 partial Lassa virus genomes were recovered from the secondary cases, which phylogenetically clustered with genomes from central Guinea. Consistent with a common transmission source, the 4 genomes were almost identical. Rodent testing revealed a new reservoir area in eastern-central Guinea. CONCLUSIONS: This outbreak highlights the vulnerability of healthcare settings in low-prevalence areas of West Africa to nosocomial Lassa virus transmission due to human mobility. Facilitated by capacity-building programs for viral hemorrhagic fevers, rapid diagnosis, genomic analysis, and ecological assessment enabled an efficient outbreak response and control.

2025年尼日利亚塔拉巴州流行病学的描述性暴发分析

Descriptive epidemiology of Lassa fever outbreak in Taraba State, Nigeria, 2025
作者： Munzali Shamsu, Olukemi Titilope Olugbade, Lois Oluwatoyin Olajide, Ahmad Muhammad Aliyu, Murtala Rabi’u, Moses Job Tarfa, Zainab Dambazau, Musa Hassan Muhammad, Sa’adatu Aliyu Abubakar, Mustapha Lawal
摘要中文翻译引言:拉沙热(LF)在西非流行,构成重大的公共卫生安全威胁。尽管尼日利亚负担最重,但关于塔拉巴州等东北部州的详细州级流行病学数据仍十分有限。本研究提供了2025年流行病学第1至第7周塔拉巴州拉沙热的全面描述性流行病学模式,填补了关键证据空白,并为有针对性的公共卫生干预措施提供了依据。方法:我们进行了一项描述性流行病学研究,分析了2025年流行病学第1至第7周塔拉巴州的监测和接触追踪数据。疑似病例定义为任何出现乏力、发热、头痛、咽痛、咳嗽、恶心、呕吐、腹泻、肌肉疼痛、胸痛、听力丧失,并有接触啮齿动物排泄物或接触拉沙热病例史的人。确诊病例则定义为通过逆转录聚合酶链反应(RT-PCR)实验室确认的疑似病例。我们回顾了响应日志、风险沟通和医疗物资分发情况。计算了描述性统计指标,包括频率、比例、阳性率和病例死亡率(CFR),并对时间分布进行了分析。结果:共报告了165例疑似病例,其中70例(42.6%)经实验室确认。3例疑似病例(1.8%;3/165)发生在医疗工作者中。37例确诊病例死亡,病例死亡率为52.9%。15-24岁年龄组占疑似病例的40.6%(67/165),其中52.7%(87/165)为男性。巴利(Bali)地区记录了最高的阳性率(67.7%;23/35),占确诊病例的最高比例(32.9%;23/70)。在伊比(Ibbi)和武卡里(Wukari)地区,死亡率高达100%;加索尔(Gassol)地区为75%;阿尔多-科拉(Ardo-Kola)地区为53.3%;巴利地区为52.2%。暴发在2025年流行病学第4周(1月下旬)达到高峰。结论:塔拉巴州的拉沙热暴发以高病例死亡率、年轻男性病例集中以及流行病学第4周的高峰为特征,需要立即采取有针对性的公共卫生干预措施,包括加强监测监测、对高风险群体实施密集的社区意识宣传活动,并确保高负担地区居民能够及时获得挽救生命的治疗治疗。

Descriptive epidemiology of Lassa fever outbreak in Taraba State, Nigeria, 2025

Authors: Munzali Shamsu, Olukemi Titilope Olugbade, Lois Oluwatoyin Olajide, Ahmad Muhammad Aliyu, Murtala Rabi’u, Moses Job Tarfa, Zainab Dambazau, Musa Hassan Muhammad, Sa’adatu Aliyu Abubakar, Mustapha Lawal
Original abstract (English)
Introduction: Lassa fever (LF) is endemic in West Africa, representing a significant public health security threat. While Nigeria bears the highest burden, detailed state-level epidemiological data, particularly in North-Eastern states like Taraba, remain scarce. This study provided a comprehensive descriptive epidemiological pattern of Lassa fever in Taraba State from epidemiological weeks 1 – 7, 2025, addressing a critical evidence gap and informing targeted public health interventions. Methods: We conducted a descriptive epidemiological study analysing Taraba State surveillance and contact tracing data during the outbreak response from epidemiological week 1 – 7, 2025. A suspected case was defined as any person with malaise, fever, headache, sore throat, cough, nausea, vomiting, diarrhoea, myalgia, chest pain, hearing loss and a history of contact with rodent excreta or with a case of Lassa fever, while a confirmed case was defined as a suspected case that is laboratory-confirmed with Reverse-Transcription Polymerase Chain Reaction. We reviewed response logs, risk communication, and medical supply distribution. Descriptive statistics, including frequencies, proportions, positivity rate, and case fatality rate (CFR), were calculated, and the temporal distribution was analysed. Results: A total of 165 suspected cases were reported, of which 70 (42.6%) were laboratory-confirmed cases. Three suspected cases (1.8%; 3/165) occurred among healthcare workers. Thirty-seven confirmed cases died, giving a case fatality rate of 52.9%. The 15- 24-year age group accounted for 40.6% (67/165) of suspected cases, and 52.7% (87/165) were male. Bali LGA recorded the highest positivity rate (67.7%; 23/35), accounted for the highest proportion of confirmed cases (32.9%; 23/70) and high CFRs were noted in Ibbi and Wukari LGA (100%), Gassol (75%), Ardo-Kola (53.3%) and Bali (52.2%). The outbreak peaked during epidemiological week 4 (late January 2025). Conclusion: The Lassa fever outbreak in Taraba State was characterised by a high CFR, concentration of cases among young adult males and the epidemiological week 4 peak necessitated immediate and targeted public health interventions, including strengthening surveillance, implementing intensive community awareness campaigns for high-risk groups, and ensuring prompt access to life-saving treatment in high-burden LGAs.

Digital Tools for Lassa Fever Surveillance and Outbreak Response in Nigeria: A Focused Scoping Review
作者： Ayodele Israel Ajayi, Oluwafemi Emmanuel Ooju, Kennedy Oberhiri Obohwemu, Celestine Emeka Ekwuluo, Gordon Mabengban Yakpir, Kenneth Oshiokhayamhe Iyevhobu, Oluwakemi Laguda-Akingba, Eddy Eidenehi Esezobor, Nera Perpetual Kadiri-Eneh, Oladipo Vincent Akinmade
摘要中文翻译背景 拉沙热 仍然是尼日利亚持续存在的公共卫生威胁,经常发生的 暴发 对 监测、实验室服务和 暴发 响应能力提出了相当大的要求。及时识别疑似病例尤为重要,因为延迟通知和实验室确认可能会妨碍病例管理、接触者随访和感染预防。数字化 监测 已越来越多地融入尼日利亚的应对工作中,监测 暴发 应对管理和分析系统 (SORMAS) 支持基于案例的电子报告和实时 暴发 监控。然而,有关这些数字能力如何对 拉沙热 监测 做出具体贡献的证据仍然分散在州级调查和实施研究中。方法进行了重点范围界定审查,综合了尼日利亚用于 拉沙热 监测 和 暴发 应对措施的数字技术的证据。证据基础是从 2015 年至 2026 年间发表的文献中确定的,并通过 PubMed、ScienceDirect、SpringerLink、BMJ Global Health 和 Google Scholar 进行搜索。合格的证据仅限于报告与尼日利亚 拉沙热 直接相关的数字 监测 应用或结果的研究。由于研究设计、设置和报告结果的异质性,数据以叙述方式综合。该综合检查了基于电子案例的 监测、报告和通知、接触者追踪、实时 暴发 监控和实施限制。结果 SORMAS 是 拉沙热 监测 确定的主导数字平台。尼日利亚几个州报告了证据,包括贝努埃、卡杜纳、阿南布拉、埃博尼和克罗斯河。基于数字病例的监测支持对疑似病例和确诊病例的实时跟踪,并能够对疾病负担集中的区域进行地理识别。报告的州级病死率差异很大,从埃博尼的 24% 到卡杜纳的 73.3%,这表明改进的 监测 能力与相当大的 死亡率 共存。数字 监测 还与特定响应流程的改进相关。在 Ebonyi,接触者追踪的完整性从 45% 提高到 85%,而 Cross River 的证据表明,引入实时警报后,响应时间缩短了五天。持续存在的限制包括电力不可靠、互联网连接差、数字素养有限、数字设备短缺以及继续依赖平行纸质和电子报告。结论 数字 监测 加强了尼日利亚 拉沙热 监测 和 暴发 响应的重要操作组成部分,特别是基于病例的报告、实时监控、接触者追踪和 传播 信息的快速 传播。证据还表明,在临床、实验室、基础设施和劳动力限制仍然存在的情况下,较强的 死亡率 并不一定会转化为较低的 死亡率。因此,数字平台应定位为集成 暴发 响应系统的组件,而不是独立的解决方案。持续的基础设施、劳动力能力、互操作性和长期融资仍然是必要的,以便将 监测 智能的改进转化为更强大的 拉沙热 控制。

Digital Tools for Lassa Fever Surveillance and Outbreak Response in Nigeria: A Focused Scoping Review

Authors: Ayodele Israel Ajayi, Oluwafemi Emmanuel Ooju, Kennedy Oberhiri Obohwemu, Celestine Emeka Ekwuluo, Gordon Mabengban Yakpir, Kenneth Oshiokhayamhe Iyevhobu, Oluwakemi Laguda-Akingba, Eddy Eidenehi Esezobor, Nera Perpetual Kadiri-Eneh, Oladipo Vincent Akinmade
Original abstract (English)
Background Lassa fever remains a persistent public health threat in Nigeria, where recurrent outbreaks place considerable demands on surveillance, laboratory services and outbreak-response capacity. Timely identification of suspected cases is particularly important because delayed notification and laboratory confirmation can impede case management, contact follow-up and infection prevention. Digital surveillance has increasingly been incorporated into Nigeria's response, with the Surveillance Outbreak Response Management and Analysis System (SORMAS) supporting electronic case-based reporting and real-time outbreak monitoring. Evidence on how these digital capabilities have contributed specifically to Lassa fever surveillance, however, remains dispersed across state-level investigations and implementation studies. Methods A focused scoping review was undertaken to synthesise evidence on digital technologies used for Lassa fever surveillance and outbreak response in Nigeria. The evidence base was identified from literature published between 2015 and 2026 and searched through PubMed, ScienceDirect, SpringerLink, BMJ Global Health and Google Scholar. Eligible evidence was restricted to studies reporting digital surveillance applications or outcomes directly relevant to Lassa fever in Nigeria. Data were synthesised narratively because of heterogeneity in study designs, settings and reported outcomes. The synthesis examined electronic case-based surveillance, reporting and notification, contact tracing, real-time outbreak monitoring and implementation constraints. Results SORMAS was the dominant digital platform identified for Lassa fever surveillance. Evidence was reported from several Nigerian states, including Benue, Kaduna, Anambra, Ebonyi and Cross River. Digital case-based surveillance supported real-time tracking of suspected and confirmed cases and enabled geographical identification of areas with concentrated disease burden. Reported state-level case fatality rates varied substantially, from 24% in Ebonyi to 73.3% in Kaduna, indicating that improved surveillance capability coexisted with considerable mortality. Digital surveillance was also associated with improvements in specific response processes. In Ebonyi, contact-tracing completeness increased from 45% to 85%, while evidence from Cross River reported a five-day reduction in response time following the introduction of real-time alerts. Persistent constraints included unreliable electricity, poor internet connectivity, limited digital literacy, shortages of digital equipment and continued dependence on parallel paper and electronic reporting. Conclusion Digital surveillance has strengthened important operational components of Lassa fever surveillance and outbreak response in Nigeria, particularly case-based reporting, real-time monitoring, contact tracing and rapid transmission of surveillance information. The evidence also shows that stronger surveillance does not necessarily translate into low mortality where clinical, laboratory, infrastructural and workforce limitations persist. Digital platforms should therefore be positioned as components of an integrated outbreak-response system rather than stand-alone solutions. Sustained infrastructure, workforce capability, interoperability and long-term financing remain necessary to convert improvements in surveillance intelligence into stronger Lassa fever control.
